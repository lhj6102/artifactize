Current: a branch's worktree as it is now, in Chat's Graph view

A Chat session's Graph view: the session's header row with the view switch on Graph and the scan's state, the tool strip, the Current graph of the session's branch lit by what verify would do, and the composer still under it. The bar and the Pane are Chat's; Verify shows Runs only. A Current is also where the branch's Artifacts are defined: the second figure shows an Artifact dragged into a whole's box, the third a drop that was refused, the fourth two refusals for the file an edit would write, and the last two a comment being written and, separately, another being moved.

  1. 1
    Scan stateIn the Main area's header row, after the view's name: while the scan runs, the checking icon and how many Artifacts are still being checked, as a count that falls (1 left: here release, the one node still checking); once it ends, the time it was scanned at; only a failure is said in words. Refresh at the row's end.
  2. 2
    Current graphDrawn at once from the definitions, every node first checking, then lit in place as the scan learns what verify would do.
  3. 12
    Composer under the graphThe session's composer stays under the Graph view, as in Conversation: what is asked of the agent and the graph it changes are seen together; the view switch in the header row goes back to the conversation.
  4. 3
    New ArtifactIn the tool strip after Fit, only on a Current: a dialog asks for the name and the Path and writes the definition file.
  1. 4
    Reset layout, top levelIn the tool strip while the top level is placed by hand; a click lays the top level out automatically again, every top-level node's place leaving its file, while the boxes in it keep their own arrangements.
  2. 5
    Dragged ArtifactRaised one step with the faint shadow, following the pointer with its edges; its face, icon, strip and tally as they were. The place it left stays empty.
  3. 6
    Drop targetThe box a release would make its whole, outlined in the light neutral accent while the drop would be accepted. Released, desktop-design becomes a part of desktop, written in desktop's definition, and is drawn in desktop's box where it was dropped, below the box's parts when it would cover one; in the same edit it leaves the whole it was dragged out of, its place moving from that whole's file into desktop's. Released outside every box, it only leaves that whole and is drawn at the top level, its place written into its own file, its other wholes unchanged.
  4. 7
    Reset layout, a boxA box placed by hand has it in its title row before Minimize; a box laid out automatically has none. A click lays out that box alone automatically again, its places leaving the whole's file; the boxes nested inside it keep their own arrangements.
  1. 8
    RefusalThe Main area's notice in red says why in a few words; a click shows it in full. Nothing was written and the dragged Artifact is back where it was.
  1. 12
    Changed on diskA placing release, a drop, Reset layout or a comment whose file changed on disk since the Current read it writes nothing: the notice names that file, what was dragged is back where it was, and the Current is scanned again, so the graph shows what is on disk.
  2. 13
    Unsaved SourceThe same edits are refused while that file's Source holds typed text, so the graph never overwrites it: the notice names the file whose Source holds the text, nothing is written and the text stays, until that Source is saved or reverted. Neither notice has a line and column: those belong to an error in a Source's text, said under the Source.
  1. 9
    Comment fieldOpened in place under the node by the Definition's Comment or a click on the comment, holding it whole: Enter writes it into the node's file, Shift+Enter breaks the line, a click outside the field (a press and release without moving) puts it back, nothing written, and Enter on an empty field removes it. Closed by Enter, Esc or a click that leaves the Detail open, focus returns to where it was opened from: the Comment button, or the graph for a click on the comment; a click on the Detail's Close or the graph's background puts the comment back and closes the Detail with it (J-COMMENT-EDIT).
  2. 10
    CommentText alone in the quieter ink, at most three lines; the rest is in its field and in the Source.
  1. 11
    Comment draggedIts text alone follows the pointer, lifted by a faint shadow on its letters, with no card under it; an Artifact without a comment under the pointer is the drop target. Released there, the comment moves from one file to the other in one edit. A press on a comment followed by moving starts its drag; a press and release without moving is a click, which opens its field.

Rules

D-CUR-1
The Current's scan state stands in the Chat session's header row (D-CHAT-2), after the view switch on Graph (D-CHAT-10): the scan's state in --ink-2 with tabular figures, said without a sentence: while it runs, the checking icon in --checking and n left, how many Artifacts are still being checked, a count that falls to none and is read aloud as Checking, n Artifacts left; once it ends, the time it was scanned at, HH:MM:SS, read aloud as Scanned at HH:MM:SS; only a failure is said in words, why it failed in --red. A small Refresh button sits at the row's end; a refresh asked while a scan runs keeps that scan shown, the header still counting, and one more scan follows once it ends. a sentence for a scan that runs or ended well; a ratio that reads like a node's tally (3/24); a spinner without the count; the state in a banner; a second row for the scan's state; a key cap on Refresh.
D-CUR-10
The Graph view of a Chat session (J-CHAT-GRAPH) takes the conversation's place under the session's header row: the graph's tool strip (D-GRAPH-4, D-CUR-3), the canvas (D-GRAPH-1), the Detail docked at its right as in Verify (D-INS-1), and under the canvas, 8px below it, the composer as in Conversation (D-CHAT-6), in the same --measure column centred on the area, so what is typed and the graph are seen together. For a session whose branch cannot be worked on now, the graph's place holds one --ink-2 line saying why, as the library says it (its worktree gone, its branch no longer checked out), with no tool strip, nothing live and the composer off (D-CHAT-9). the Current in Verify; a second Current per session on one branch; the composer hidden in Graph view; the graph under the composer.
D-CUR-2
The Current graph is drawn as a Run's (D-GRAPH-1, D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4) under the same tool strip (D-GRAPH-4), with marks of its own (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking, each with its icon, named in Help, D-OVL-5); a node still being checked uses --checking and recolours in place. Read aloud, a node says its name and what verify would do with it (reused, would run, waiting or blocked), with how many of its evals would run, whatever it would do once it is checked (none, for a reused one), or that it is still being checked (J-MAIN-CURRENT). a different node shape for a Current; a redraw that moves the camera.
D-CUR-3
On a Current the tool strip (D-GRAPH-4) adds, after Fit, New Artifact, a small button with the add icon and its words, and, while the top level is placed by hand, Reset layout, a small button with the reset icon and its words; a Run's strip has neither. an edit control on a Run's graph; New Artifact as an icon alone; Reset layout shown, or disabled, for a top level laid out automatically; a key cap on either.
D-CUR-4
A dragged Artifact (a node, a minimized whole, or a whole with its parts) floats: raised to --surface-raised with the faint shadow (D-SYS-2), its outline, icon, strip and tally as they were, its edges and its comment following it; the place it left stays empty. A press and release without moving is a click (it opens the Detail); a press followed by moving starts the drag; a whole is dragged by its title row. Released in the box it was in (or at the top level when it was in none), it lands where it was released, settled again at its own surface with no drop target highlighted; what that release writes is J-NODE-MOVE. a ghost left at the old place; a drag that starts on a press and release without moving; a node that turns into a block or a different shape while dragged; the camera moving while a node is dragged.
D-CUR-5
The drop target, the box or node a release would make the dragged Artifact's whole, is outlined 2px in --accent, its face and strip unchanged, only on a destination where the drop would make the dragged Artifact a part, as the graph knows from its own definitions during the drag, without reading any file (J-PARTS-DRAG). The box the Artifact is dragged within, an Artifact a drop would make a cycle with, and the top level show nothing. Released on an accepted target, the dragged Artifact is added to that Artifact's parts and drawn where it was dropped, below the box's parts when it would cover one; in the same edit it leaves the whole whose box it was dragged out of, its place written into the new whole's file and removed from the old one's. Released outside every box, it only leaves the whole it was dragged out of and is drawn at the top level where it was dropped, its place written into its own file; for a copy of a shared part, only the whole it was dragged out of changes, its other wholes keeping it. A drop the files refuse is J-DEFINITION-CONFLICT's, said as the Main area's notice (D-CUR-7). a status colour or red for a drop target; a dropped part snapped back into a slot of the automatic layout, or drawn over a part it would cover; a drop outside every box that removes a shared part from its other wholes; a mark for a drop that would make a cycle; drop zones drawn on the canvas; a highlighted edge.
D-CUR-6
A box placed by hand carries Reset layout in its title row, before Minimize: a button of Minimize's size and border (D-NODE-3) with the reset icon, named Reset layout of {whole} when read aloud; a box laid out automatically has none, so the button alone tells a box placed by hand. A click lays out that box alone automatically again, its places leaving the whole's file; the strip's Reset layout (D-CUR-3) does the same for the top level, every top-level node's place leaving its file. Either way the boxes nested inside keep their own arrangements, those placed by hand staying as placed (J-LAYOUT-RESET). a badge, a lock or a pin marking a placed box; a Reset layout that also resets the boxes nested inside; a tooltip on the button (D-SYS-7); Reset layout on a Run's graph.
D-CUR-7
An edit made on the graph (a placing release, a drop, Reset layout, Enter in a comment field or a comment's drop) that is refused writes nothing, puts the dragged Artifact or comment back where it was, or leaves the comment field as it was, and says why as the Main area's notice (D-MAIN-1): --red, a few words, its whole text a click away. What it names tells the refusals apart: a cycle or a definition the project would not accept names the Artifacts and why; a file changed on disk since the Current read it names that file, {file} changed on disk; nothing written, and the Current is scanned again; a file whose Source in the Detail holds typed text names that file, {file} has unsaved text in its Source; nothing written, the typed text kept, until that Source is saved or reverted. A graph's notice never carries a line and column, which only an error in a Source's text has (D-INS-11). a dialog or a toast for a refusal; a refusal said on the canvas; a refused drop left where it was dropped; a refusal that does not name the file it concerns; a graph edit that overwrites or clears a Source's typed text.
D-CUR-8
The comment field opens in place of the comment, or where it would be, under its node (D-NODE-6): a --surface-input field with a 1px --line-input border and, while typed in, the 1px --current-mark editing outline of every field (D-SYS-13), the --focus ring only for keyboard focus, as wide as the comment, growing with its lines to show the comment whole, its text --text-small in --ink with its line breaks; the graph around it stays as it was. It has no buttons and no hint: what it writes or puts back, and where focus returns, is J-COMMENT-EDIT; a refusal is said as the Main area's notice (D-CUR-7) and the field stays with its text. a dialog, a popover or a Detail section for a comment; Save and Cancel buttons on the field; a line that explains its keys; a field that covers other nodes' names without growing the space under its own.
D-CUR-9
A comment is dragged by its text: while dragged its text alone follows the pointer, lifted by a faint --scrim shadow on its letters, in the same ink, width and lines as under its node, with no surface, outline, rounded corners or box around it; the Artifact a release would give it, one without a comment, is the drop target of D-CUR-5, and nothing else shows. Released elsewhere it returns under its node. a dragged comment on a card, a raised surface or a rounded box; a drop target on an Artifact that has a comment; a comment left on the canvas where it was dropped.
