Current: a branch's worktree as it is now

The Main area when a branch's Current is chosen: the scan header, the Current graph lit by what verify would do, and the expandable structure text. The bar and the Pane are as on the Monitor page; the Pane's Current entry sits in its pill.

  1. 1
    Scan headerWhile the scan runs, how many Artifacts are still being checked; once it ends, when it was scanned; a failure says why. Refresh at the right.
  2. 2
    Current graphDrawn at once from the declarations, every node first checking, then lit in place as the scan learns what verify would do.
  3. 3
    Structure textFolded under the graph; expanded, every criterion and labeled relation as monospace text in an input-surface box.

Rules

D-CUR-1
The Current's header is one row under the Main area header: the scan's state in --ink-2 (Checking n of m Artifacts…, Scanned at HH:MM:SS in tabular figures, or why it failed in --red) and a small Refresh button at the right, disabled while a scan runs. a spinner without the count; the state in a banner.
D-CUR-2
The Current graph is drawn and furnished as a Run's (D-GRAPH-1, D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4) with its own legend (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking); a node still being checked uses --light-checking and recolours in place. a different node shape for a Current; a redraw that moves the camera.
D-CUR-3
The structure text folds under the graph behind a ▸/▾ summary line opened above by --line-group; expanded, it is a --surface-input box in --mono at --text-small that wraps and scrolls within at most 40% of the window's height. the text widening the Main area; the fold hiding the graph.
