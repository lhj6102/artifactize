Current: a branch's worktree as it is now, in Chat's Graph view

A Chat session's Graph view: the stage header with the view tabs on Graph and the graph's tools, the Current graph of the session's branch filling the area under it, lit by what verify would do, the scan's state in the canvas's status line, and the chat floating over the graph's bottom: the composer and a reply's card. The bar and the Pane are Chat's; Verify shows Runs only. The graph reads the branch's code under .artifactize/ and never changes it: nothing on it is created, dragged, placed or commented, its nodes are laid out automatically, and an Artifact's Definition and Source in the Detail are read (detail.html).

  1. 1
    Scan stateThe stage's status line, on the canvas at its top left: while the scan runs, the checking icon and how many Artifacts are still being checked, as a count that falls (1 left: here release, the one node still checking); once it ends, the time it was scanned at; only a failure in words, in red. Never in the stage header.

    Floating composerThe session's composer, floating at the canvas's bottom centre at the conversation column's width: a dark face inside a thin edge, no shadow. Fit and the zoom keep the graph's nodes above it.
  6. 13

Rules

D-CUR-1
The Current's scan state stands in the stage's status line (D-MAIN-2), on the canvas at its top left, never in the stage header: the scan's state in --ink-2 with tabular figures, said without a sentence: while it runs, the checking icon in --checking and n left, how many Artifacts are still being checked, a count that falls to none and is read aloud as Checking, n Artifacts left; once it ends, the time it was scanned at, HH:MM:SS, read aloud as Scanned at HH:MM:SS; only a failure is said in words, why it failed in --red. Refresh, a word alone, is the last of the Current's tools in the stage header (D-CUR-3); a refresh asked while a scan runs keeps that scan shown, the status line still counting, and one more scan follows once it ends. the scan state in the stage header; a sentence for a scan that runs or ended well; a ratio that reads like a node's tally (3/24); a spinner without the count; the state in a banner; a face or box behind the status line; a key cap on Refresh.
D-CUR-10
The Graph view of a Chat session (J-CHAT-GRAPH) gives the Main area under the stage header (D-MAIN-1) to the canvas (D-GRAPH-1), its whole height, with the Detail docked at its right as in Verify (D-INS-1); the stage header's middle shows Graph as the shown tab (D-CHAT-10) and its right the graph's tools and the Current's (D-GRAPH-4, D-CUR-3). The chat floats over the canvas's bottom, 12px above it, centred in the canvas left of the Detail when one is open, the conversation column's width (--measure, narrower when the canvas is): the composer as in Conversation (D-CHAT-6), its face --surface-input inside its 1px --line-input edge, no shadow, and, once a message is sent from this view, the reply card 8px above it: a --surface-input face inside a 1px --line-group edge of --radius-chip, no shadow; its head a --row-head row, the unfold icon (the fold icon while folded), the message sent in --ink-2 at --text-small cut with an ellipsis, and at its right the in-progress icon in --running while the turn runs; under it the reply as the conversation draws a turn (D-CHAT-3 to D-CHAT-5), at most 120px high, following its newest line. A click on the head folds the card to its head and unfolds it again. The card holds the newest message sent from this view and its reply alone; the conversation keeps every turn, and the card is gone once Conversation is shown. The camera works in one protected view: the canvas less the status line's 32px at its top (D-MAIN-2) and less the floating band at its bottom, as tall as the floating chat is now plus 12px (about 126px with the card folded, about 210px unfolded), followed as the card folds, unfolds, grows with a streaming reply or the composer grows. Fit and the first view fit the graph into it; the zoom buttons zoom around its centre; an Artifact found, created or reached by the keyboard is brought into it, so no focused or selected node ends under the band; the wheel and panning stay the person's own. The Navigator (D-GRAPH-5) stands at the band's right, above it. For a session whose branch cannot be worked on now, the canvas holds one --ink-2 line saying why, as the library says it (its worktree gone, its branch no longer checked out), with no graph tools, nothing live and the composer off (D-CHAT-9). a fixed inset that misses the card's folding; a focused or selected node left under the band; the Current in Verify; a second Current per session on one branch; the composer under the canvas instead of over it; a shadow, a raised face or a scrim behind the floating chat; the whole conversation over the graph; a node fitted under the floating chat; the floating chat centred on the window with the Detail open.
D-CUR-2
The Current graph is drawn as a Run's (D-GRAPH-1, D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4) with the same tools (D-GRAPH-4), with marks of its own (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking, each with its icon, named in Help, D-OVL-5); a node still being checked uses --checking and recolours in place. Read aloud, a node says its name and what verify would do with it (reused, would run, waiting or blocked), with how many of its evals would run, whatever it would do once it is checked (none, for a reused one), or that it is still being checked (J-MAIN-CURRENT). a different node shape for a Current; a redraw that moves the camera.
D-CUR-3
On a Current the stage header's tools (D-GRAPH-4) add, after Fit and a separator, Refresh (D-CUR-1), and in Chat the session's Move last (D-CHAT-12); a Run's tools have no Refresh. The graph reads the code and has no edit control: nothing creates, renames, deletes or comments an Artifact, nothing places a node or a box, and a press on a node followed by moving pans the canvas as a press on its background does (D-GRAPH-1); a box carries Minimize alone in its title row (D-NODE-3). New Artifact, Reset layout, a drag handle or any other edit control on either graph; a node, a box or a comment that follows the pointer; a comment drawn under a node; a border around a tool; a key cap on any.
