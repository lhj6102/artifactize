Current: a branch's worktree as it is now

The Main area when a branch's Current is chosen: the header row with the scan's state, the tool strip and the Current graph lit by what verify would do. The bar and the Pane are as on the Monitor page; the Pane's Current entry sits in its pill. A Current is also where the branch's Artifacts are defined: the second figure shows an Artifact dragged into a whole's box, the third a drop that was refused, the fourth a comment being written and another being moved.

  1. 1
    Scan stateIn the Main area's header row, after the view's name: while the scan runs, the checking icon and how many Artifacts are still being checked, as a count that falls (1 left: here release, the one node still checking); once it ends, the time it was scanned at; only a failure is said in words. Refresh at the row's end.
  2. 2
    Current graphDrawn at once from the definitions, every node first checking, then lit in place as the scan learns what verify would do.
  3. 3
    New ArtifactIn the tool strip after Fit, only on a Current: a dialog asks for the name and the Path and writes the definition file.
  1. 4
    Reset layout, top levelIn the tool strip while the top level is placed by hand; a click lays it out automatically again.
  2. 5
    Dragged ArtifactRaised one step with the faint shadow, following the pointer with its edges; its face, icon, strip and tally as they were. The place it left stays empty.
  3. 6
    Drop targetThe box a release would make its whole, outlined in the accent while the drop would be accepted. Released, desktop-design becomes a part of desktop, written in desktop's definition.
  4. 7
    Reset layout, a boxA box placed by hand has it in its title row before Minimize; a box laid out automatically has none.
  1. 8
    RefusalThe Main area's notice in red says why in a few words; a click shows it in full. Nothing was written and the dragged Artifact is back where it was.
  1. 9
    Comment fieldOpened in place under the node by the Definition's Comment or a click on the comment, holding it whole: Enter writes it into the node's file, Shift+Enter breaks the line, Esc puts it back, Enter on an empty field removes it.

  3. 11
    Comment draggedIts text floats with the faint shadow; an Artifact without a comment under the pointer is the drop target. Released there, the comment moves from one file to the other in one edit.

Rules

D-CUR-1
The Current's scan state is the headline of the Main area's header row (D-MAIN-1), after Current · {project} {branch}: the scan's state in --ink-2 with tabular figures, said without a sentence: while it runs, the checking icon in --checking and n left, how many Artifacts are still being checked, a count that falls to none and is read aloud as Checking, n Artifacts left; once it ends, the time it was scanned at, HH:MM:SS, read aloud as Scanned at HH:MM:SS; only a failure is said in words, why it failed in --red. A small Refresh button sits at the row's end (Ctrl+R and F5 do the same); a refresh asked while a scan runs keeps that scan shown, the header still counting, and one more scan follows once it ends. a sentence for a scan that runs or ended well; a ratio that reads like a node's tally (3/24); a spinner without the count; the state in a banner; a second row for the scan's state; a key cap on Refresh.
D-CUR-2
The Current graph is drawn as a Run's (D-GRAPH-1, D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4) under the same tool strip (D-GRAPH-4), with marks of its own (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking, each with its icon, named in Help, D-OVL-5); a node still being checked uses --checking and recolours in place. a different node shape for a Current; a redraw that moves the camera.
D-CUR-3
On a Current the tool strip (D-GRAPH-4) adds, after Fit, New Artifact, a small button with the add icon and its words, and, while the top level is placed by hand, Reset layout, a small button with the reset icon and its words; a Run's strip has neither. an edit control on a Run's graph; New Artifact as an icon alone; Reset layout shown, or disabled, for a top level laid out automatically; a key cap on either.
D-CUR-4
A dragged Artifact (a node, a minimized whole, or a whole with its parts) floats: raised to --surface-raised with the faint shadow (D-SYS-2), its outline, icon, strip and tally as they were, its edges and its comment following it; the place it left stays empty. A drag begins once the pointer has moved a few pixels with the button held, so a click stays a click; a whole is dragged by its title row. a ghost left at the old place; a drag that starts on a click; a node that turns into a block or a different shape while dragged; the camera moving while a node is dragged.
D-CUR-5
The drop target, the box or node a release would make the dragged Artifact's whole, is outlined 2px in --accent, its face and strip unchanged, only while the drop would be accepted. The box the Artifact is dragged within, an Artifact a drop would make a cycle with, and the top level show nothing. a status colour or red for a drop target; a mark for a drop that would be refused; drop zones drawn on the canvas; a highlighted edge.
D-CUR-6
A box placed by hand carries Reset layout in its title row, before Minimize: a button of Minimize's size and border (D-NODE-3) with the reset icon, named Reset layout of {whole} when read aloud; a box laid out automatically has none, so the button alone tells a box placed by hand. a badge, a lock or a pin marking a placed box; a tooltip on the button (D-SYS-7); Reset layout on a Run's graph.
D-CUR-7
An edit made on the graph that is refused (a cycle, a definition the project would not accept, a file changed on disk) puts the dragged Artifact back where it was and says why as the Main area's notice (D-MAIN-1): --red, a few words naming the Artifacts or the file, its whole text a click away. a dialog or a toast for a refusal; a refusal said on the canvas; a refused drop left where it was dropped.
D-CUR-8
The comment field opens in place of the comment, or where it would be, under its node (D-NODE-6): a --surface-input field with a 1px --line-input border and the --focus ring, as wide as the comment, growing with its lines to show the comment whole, its text --text-small in --ink with its line breaks; the graph around it stays as it was. It has no buttons and no hint: Enter writes, Shift+Enter breaks the line, Esc or a click outside puts back, and Enter on an empty field removes the comment; a refusal is said as the Main area's notice (D-CUR-7) and the field stays with its text. a dialog, a popover or a Detail section for a comment; Save and Cancel buttons on the field; a line that explains its keys; a field that covers other nodes' names without growing the space under its own.
D-CUR-9
A comment is dragged by its text: while dragged it floats as a dragged node does (D-CUR-4), its text on --surface-raised with the faint shadow and no outline; the Artifact a release would give it, one without a comment, is the drop target of D-CUR-5, and nothing else shows. Released elsewhere it returns under its node. a comment that keeps a card after the drag; a drop target on an Artifact that has a comment; a comment left on the canvas where it was dropped.
