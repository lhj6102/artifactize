Current: a branch's worktree as it is now

The Main area when a branch's Current is chosen: the header row with the scan's state, the tool strip, the Current graph lit by what verify would do, and the expandable structure text. The bar and the Pane are as on the Monitor page; the Pane's Current entry sits in its pill.

  1. 1
    Scan stateIn the Main area's header row, after the view's name: while the scan runs, how many Artifacts are still being checked; once it ends, when it was scanned; a failure says why. Refresh at the row's end.
  2. 2
    Current graphDrawn at once from the declarations, every node first checking, then lit in place as the scan learns what verify would do.
  3. 3
    Structure textUnder the graph behind a section header row, folded at first; expanded, every criterion and labeled relation as monospace text in an input-surface box.

Rules

D-CUR-1
The Current's scan state is the headline of the Main area's header row (D-MAIN-1), after Current · {project} {branch}: the scan's state in --ink-2 (Checking n of m Artifacts…, Scanned at HH:MM:SS in tabular figures, or why it failed in --red) and a small Refresh button at the row's end with its Ctrl+R key cap (F5 does the same); a refresh asked while a scan runs keeps that scan shown, the header still saying its progress, and one more scan follows once it ends. a spinner without the count; the state in a banner; a second row for the scan's state.
D-CUR-2
The Current graph is drawn as a Run's (D-GRAPH-1, D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4) under the same tool strip (D-GRAPH-4), whose legend is its own (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking, each with its icon); a node still being checked uses --checking and recolours in place. a different node shape for a Current; a redraw that moves the camera.
D-CUR-3
The structure text folds under the graph behind a section header row of the inspector's anatomy (D-INS-9): the fold icon (the unfold icon while expanded), Structure and evaluation criteria, and the graph's fingerprint as its summary, opened above by --line-group; it starts folded; expanded, it is a --surface-input box in --mono at --text-small that wraps and scrolls within at most 40% of the window's height. the text widening the Main area; the fold hiding the graph.
