Monitor: the one screen with a Run shown

The window after artifactize-desktop starts: the global bar, the Pane on the left (search, branches, the Waiting stack) and the Main area: one header row with the shown Run's name and headline, the graph's tool strip and the graph. Nothing is selected on the graph yet; the Detail is closed. Started without arguments, the Main area shows Nothing chosen instead (D-MAIN-3) until a branch or a waiting review is chosen in the Pane.

  1. 1
    Global barBrand left, then the Pane switch, on while the Pane is shown; reviewer, update notice, Settings and Commands right. One line below it separates it from the work.
  2. 2
    SearchA field one step below the Pane, monospace query, its starting sort:recent. Focused with suggestions, a raised list of key and value completions drops under it, the active one on the selection background.
  3. 3

  5. 5

Where edges reach it a triangle points in on its left side, and where they leave it a square sits on its right: shape and side tell the direction. Under the pointer its outline only steps up.
  8. 8

Rules

D-BAR-1
One global bar of --bar-height on --surface-bar, closed below by --line-panel: the brand (mark and name, semibold) at the left, then the Pane switch (the pane icon, Pane and its Ctrl+B key cap, borderless: --ink over a 2px --accent line at its bottom while the Pane is shown, --ink-2 without the line while it is hidden); at the right, in this order, the reviewer in --ink-2, the update notice, Settings and Commands with its Ctrl+K key cap. a shadow under the bar; a second toolbar; page-like navigation; a switch whose state shows by colour alone; a switch for the Detail, which a node opens and Esc closes.
D-PANE-1
The Pane is one --surface-panel column, top to bottom: the Search field (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group), the Branches, and the Waiting stack opened above by --line-group. The Pane and the Main area meet at a 1px --line-panel divider that takes a drag. Hidden by the bar's Pane switch (D-BAR-1), the Pane and its divider are gone and the Main area takes the window's width; shown again, the Pane has the width it had. Focused with suggestions, the Search drops a list under the field on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: --row-dense rows in --mono at --text-small (keys, projects, branches, sort orders), the active one on --selection; Tab or Enter takes it, Esc closes it. headings such as Scope or Runs; a screen switch; a gap instead of the divider; suggestions that widen the Pane.
D-PANE-2
A branch row: a 28px head with the chevron (the fold icon in a 20px box, the unfold icon while expanded), the name at --text-title as project / in --ink-2 and the branch semibold, cut with an ellipsis, and the star at the end (the star icon in --ink-3 until on, then the filled star in --ink); folded, a 12px line under the name with an 8px dot in the status colour, the status word semibold in the same colour and the age right-aligned in tabular figures. Rows are separated by --line-group. a card per branch; the project louder than the branch; an age in proportional figures.
D-PANE-3
An expanded branch's timeline: entries of two 12px lines (status word semibold and age right; Run ID, then a comment cut to one line, in --ink-2), dots of 8px joined by a 1px --line-group line, the Current a ring, the entry shown in the Main area in a --surface-group pill of --radius-control; the selected entry has the --selection background and a 2px inset bar while the keyboard is in the Pane. While older Runs exist, Older runs ends the timeline as a text button in --accent at --text-small, indented to the entries' text, and brings them in below the oldest. pills on every entry; a status colour as the pill; a pager instead of Older runs.
D-PANE-4
The Waiting stack: an 11px uppercase label WAITING (n) in --ink-2, then 12px rows of the eval (its status's icon in --human), the project and branch, and age · claimant in --ink-2; selected rows use --selection. a bold heading; the stack taller than half the Pane.
D-MAIN-1
The Main area header is one 32px row that says what is shown: the view's name at --text-small semibold in --ink-2 (--ink while the Main area has focus): Run {id}, Current · {project} {branch}, Review; then, in the same row at --text-small, the view's headline (a Run's verdict icon and word bold in the status colour and its headline in --ink-2 with tabular figures, its tally as each status's icon in that status's colour and its count in --ink-2; a Current's scan state, D-CUR-1; a review's eval, title and Artifact, D-REV-1), cut with an ellipsis when the row is short; then a notice in --red as one cut line, at least 96px wide, taking the remaining room; actions at the end as small buttons with key caps. A click on the notice shows it in full, wrapped within the header's width on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border, what is below it neither moved nor resized (E-MON-NOTICE); another click folds it to one line. A focused panel shows a 2px --accent line at its top. a large title; a second row for the headline; a notice that moves the graph; a notice that cannot be read whole.
D-MAIN-2
Under the header a Run shows only what it has to add, above its graph's tool strip: its comment in full (wrapping, in --ink) when it has one, and what needs attention as 12px lines each led by its status's icon in the status colour; a Run with neither goes straight from the header to the tool strip. The Run's headline, in the header row, takes the click that opens the Run's Detail. a coloured banner; the attention list inside the graph; the comment cut short here.
D-MAIN-3
The graph's place is kept whatever the Main area has to say about it: with nothing chosen (a launch without arguments), while a Run loads, when a Run cannot be read, or with an empty graph, one --ink-2 line says so at the top of the Main area's body (for a Run whose graph is empty, under that Run's header, comment and attention of D-MAIN-1 and D-MAIN-2, where its graph's tool strip would begin) (Choose a branch or a waiting review in the Pane., Loading the Run…, This Run cannot be read; choose another.) and the area below stays empty on --surface-panel, without a tool strip, which comes with a graph; a notice about other Runs still goes to the header (D-MAIN-1). an empty-state illustration; a dialog for a Run that cannot be read; the Pane changing size with the Main area's state.
D-GRAPH-1
The graph canvas is the working surface, --surface-app, opened by a --line-group line under the graph's tool strip (D-GRAPH-4). Nothing rests on it but the graph, over its grid (D-GRAPH-6): the find field (D-OVL-4) floats over its top left only while it is open, and the Navigator (D-GRAPH-5) sits at its bottom right only while part of the graph lies outside the view; the legend, the zoom controls and the key hint are in the tool strip. a white canvas inside a dark panel; a legend, controls or a hint floating on the canvas.
D-GRAPH-4
The graph's tool strip is one row of --row-head between what the view shows above its graph and the canvas, on --surface-panel, opened above by --line-group; a Run's and a Current's graph each have it. From the left: Find, a small button with the find icon, its word and its Ctrl+F key cap, which opens the find field as the key does; the zoom group, one bordered group 22px high of Zoom out (the minus icon), the zoom in percent in tabular figures, and Zoom in (the add icon); Fit, a small button with the fit icon and its word, which is Fit View; then the hint that m minimizes or expands a whole, the key cap and its words at --text-label in --ink-2. At the row's end sits the legend at --text-label in --ink-2: each status's icon in its colour and its word (a Run's: GREEN, in progress, waiting, RED, ERROR, blocked by upstream; a Current's: D-CUR-2). The Detail docks under the strip, so the strip stays whole and usable with the Detail open. The strip is always one row: when it is too short for everything, Find, the zoom group, Fit and the hint stay whole and the legend, last in the row, is cut at the row's end. controls floating on the canvas; Find as an icon alone; a legend of colour dots; the strip under the Detail; a second strip or a second line.
D-NODE-5
Ports tell an edge's direction by shape and side, and carry no status: a node, a whole's box or a minimized whole that edges reach shows one input port, a triangle pointing into it, at the middle of its left side; one that edges leave shows one output port, a square, at the middle of its right side. A port is --port wide and high, straddles the outline, is filled in the edges' neutral --edge and takes no pointer; a side that no drawn edge meets has no port, and many edges on one side share its one port. The neighbours drawing in the Detail draws the same ports. a port per edge; the same shape on both sides; a port in a status colour; arrowheads on the edges instead; a port that can be dragged or clicked.
D-GRAPH-2
A graph too large to keep as elements (J-GRAPH-LARGE) is a picture of the same graph wherever the camera has more Artifacts in view than the window keeps as elements, painted at two levels by what the zoom lets a person read. Where a node's name would be too small to read, the node is one block filled with its status's colour, without outline, icon, name, tally or ports; a whole is its box's outline in its status's colour; an edge is one device pixel of --edge, or of its Artifact's colour when it leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked one (D-NODE-4). From the zoom at which names read, each node is painted as its element draws it (the --surface-panel face, the --line-node outline, the icon and the strip in its status's colour, its name, its tally, its ports), a whole as its neutral box with its title row, and edges at their regular stroke. At both levels the opened Artifact keeps its --outline-selected outline. Nothing a graph can do is lost in the picture: a click on a painted node, block or whole opens its Detail, which names it, and m minimizes or expands the whole whose Detail is open; zooming or panning draws the Artifacts it brings into view as elements again, a whole's title row, name and Minimize button included, whole in every part of D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4. a second palette for the picture; blocks where names would read; neutral boxes too small to tell apart; a painted button that takes no click.
D-GRAPH-3
A graph first shows whole and never enlarged: the camera fits every node into the canvas and stops at the nodes' own size, so a small graph sits at 168 × 40–44px nodes with 13px names in the middle of the canvas, the working surface empty around it; Fit View does the same. Only the person's own zooming goes beyond that size. a few nodes blown up to fill the canvas; a node's name larger than the UI's text when the graph first shows.
D-GRAPH-5
The Navigator shows whenever part of a graph lies outside the view, whatever the graph's size, and goes when all of it is in view: a panel about 172px wide at the canvas's bottom right, 8px in, left of the Detail when one is open; it floats as the find field does (D-SYS-2), on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border, --radius-control corners and at most the faint shadow. It draws the whole graph in small, each Artifact a block of its status's colour (the blocks of D-GRAPH-2), and frames the part in view with a 1px --outline-selected line over --selection. A click or a drag in it moves the camera there (E-GRAPH-NAVIGATE); hovering it opens nothing. a Navigator shown for a graph that is all in view; one that covers the Detail; names or edges drawn in it; a second palette.
D-GRAPH-6
The canvas carries a faint grid: 1px lines of --line-group at 40% every --grid, moving and scaling with the camera, under the edges and the nodes. It is left out wherever the zoom makes its cells smaller than 8px on screen, as in the picture of a large graph zoomed far out. a grid as strong as a group line; dots or crosses; a grid that stays still while the graph pans; a grid on a panel.
D-NODE-1
A node is 168 × 40–44px: a --surface-panel face, a 1px --line-node outline, --radius-control corners. Its status shows in two places and nowhere else, both in the status's one colour (D-SYS-6): the status's icon (--icon, D-SYS-8) before the name, and a strip --strip high along its bottom, inside the outline. After the icon comes the name at 13px medium in --ink, cut with an ellipsis; then, when the Artifact has evals, its tally at --text-label in --ink-2 with tabular figures: how many of its evals are GREEN of how many it has (2/3), on a Current how many would be reused; an Artifact still being checked shows none. The legend pairs each status's icon with its word. an outline or a face in the status colour; a plain dot where the icon goes; a name or a tally in the status colour; a colour for a status that a list does not use for it.
D-NODE-2
Selection and focus change the outline, the face and the strip unchanged, and show never more than two marks at once: the opened Artifact's node has its own outline turned --outline-selected and 2px, with no gap; the node under the pointer has its outline stepped up to --ink-3; the 2px --focus ring sits 2px outside and shows only while the keyboard moves focus, so a node opened by a click carries the one outline. The opened node and the Detail name the same Artifact; when the opened Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, that whole carries the outline (every copy of a shared part carries it too). the accent as the selection; a filled face for selection; a focus ring left on a node after a click; three rings on one node.
D-NODE-3
A whole is a box with a 1px --line-node outline and a face of --surface-panel at 40%; its title row (28–32px) holds its status's icon, its name semibold, its tally and the Minimize button (18–20px, --line-group border, the minus icon) at the right, and is closed below by the strip in its status's colour, across the box. Minimized, it is a node of a node's size and anatomy with its parts' count in --ink-2 under its name, a second --line-node outline drawn behind it, and the same button at its right now reading Expand (the plus icon). a whole's outline or face in its status colour; the button inside the parts' area; a minimized whole without its Expand button.
D-NODE-4
Edges are 1.25–1.5px strokes in the neutral --edge, meeting nodes at their sides and boxes at their edges. One kind of edge is coloured: an edge that leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked Artifact is drawn in that Artifact's status colour, so what holds a blocked Artifact can be traced upstream by eye; every other edge, from a GREEN, in progress or waiting Artifact, is neutral. A selected node gets no highlighted edges. an edge in the colour of GREEN, in progress or waiting; thick or animated edges; arrowheads larger than the node's icon.
