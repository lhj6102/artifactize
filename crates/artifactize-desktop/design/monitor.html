Verify: the monitor with a Run shown

The window after artifactize-desktop starts: the Verify mode above the bottom bar, its Pane on the left (search and branches) and its Main area: one header row with the shown Run's name, its verdict, what needs attention and, for a Run a Chat session started, the way back to that session; then the graph's tool strip and the graph. Nothing is selected on the graph yet; the Detail is closed. Started without arguments, the Main area shows Nothing chosen instead (D-MAIN-3) until a branch or one of its Runs is chosen in the Pane, or a Run is opened from a reference. The waiting reviews are Review's; the bar counts them.

  1. 1
    Bottom barAlong the window's bottom in every mode: brand and the Pane switch left, on while the Panes are shown; Chat, Verify and Review in the middle, Verify on, Review with the count of Human reviews waiting; reviewer, the release notice, Commands and Settings right, Commands with the one key cap a working screen may show. One line above it separates it from the work. The release notice (0.22.0 available) is there only while a newer stable release is out, and never on a Microsoft Store install.
  2. 2
    SearchA field one step below the Pane, monospace query, its starting sort:recent. The query narrows the Branches below it, starred branches first under either sort order. Focused with suggestions, a raised list of key and value completions drops under it, the active one on the selection background; a click on one takes it.
  3. 3
    BranchesChevron, project / quiet and branch bold, star at the line's end; folded, the newest Run's dot, status word and age (a running one its time so far); expanded, the timeline, newest first, starting with the Current's ring, or with the Run in progress (19), and holding the shown entry's pill, each Run with one line under its status (its comment, or else its ID), and Older runs at its end while older Runs exist.
  4. 19
    A Run in progressWhile a Run is in progress on the branch, its expanded timeline starts with that Run, Running and its time so far in place of an age, and has no Current ring; once the Run ends, its entry shows its final status and age and the Current's ring comes back at the top.
  5. 4
    Way back to ChatA Run a Chat session started names that session in its header row, after the headline: a reference chip, the Chat icon and Chat · {session title} in the accent. A click shows Chat with that session at the Run's reference. A Run started from the CLI or an MCP client has no chip; its Detail says what started it.
  6. 5
    Main area headerOne row: the Run's ID in 12px, then its headline: the verdict's icon and word in its colour, then what needs attention, each eval its status's icon and its name. A notice says what went wrong in a few red words at the right; a click shows it in full. The tally, the duration and the comment are in the Run's Detail, which a click on the headline opens.
  7. 7
    NodeA neutral face in a neutral outline: the status's icon, the name, and how many of its evals are GREEN of how many; a 2px strip along its bottom repeats the status's colour. Where edges reach it a triangle points in on its left side, and where they leave it a square sits on its right: shape and side tell the direction. Under the pointer its outline only steps up.
  8. 8
    WholeA neutral box around its parts; the strip in its status's colour runs along the box's top, and under it the title row holds its status's icon, its name, its tally and the Minimize button. The parts stand where the branch's definitions place them, as on its Current (D-GRAPH-7); on a Run's graph nothing here can be moved.
  9. 9
    Tool stripWhat acts on the graph, in one row above the canvas: Find, the zoom group with the zoom in percent, and Fit. Nothing else is in it. On the canvas only two things ever rest over the graph: the find field, floating at its top left while it is open, and the Navigator, anchored to its bottom right corner while part of the graph lies outside the view; Help says what the marks mean.
  10. 10
    EdgesNeutral lines. The one coloured edge leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked Artifact, in that Artifact's colour: here cli, RED, holds docs.
  11. 17
    GridA faint grid marks the canvas as the working surface and moves with the camera; it never competes with a node's outline or an edge.
  12. 18
    CommentsThe note left on an Artifact, as text alone under its node, or under a whole's title row inside its box, in the quieter ink: here on core and on desktop. It is no part of the graph's meaning and has no box of its own. On a Run's graph a click on a comment opens its Artifact's Detail, as a click on the node does, and a drag that starts on it pans the graph: the comment does not move and no field opens.
  13. 11
    Minimized wholeA node with a second outline behind it, its parts' count under its name and the Expand button at its right.
  1. 12
    BlocksWhere a name would be too small to read, a node is painted as one block of its status's colour: the whole graph reads as where it is GREEN, where it waits and where it failed.
  2. 13
    The trace of a blockEdges stay neutral at one device pixel, except every edge that leaves the RED Artifact, in RED's colour, and every edge that leaves an Artifact it blocks, in blocked's: each edge takes the colour of the Artifact it leaves, so the failure's whole reach shows, branches included. An edge that enters a blocked Artifact from a GREEN one stays neutral.
  3. 14
    WholeA whole is its box's outline in its status's colour. No Detail is open here, so no block carries the selection's outline; an opened Artifact's block would.
  4. 15
    NavigatorShown while part of the graph lies outside the view: the whole graph in small, each Artifact a block of its status's colour, a whole a fainter block of its colour behind its parts (here desktop's), the part in view framed. A click or a drag in it moves the camera there, its zoom kept, and opens no Detail.
  5. 16
    Pane hiddenThe bar's Pane switch reads as off; the Main area takes the window's width, its view as it was, and the graph and its Detail work as before. Hidden while focus was in it, focus moves to the Main area. Shown again, the Pane has the width, the search, the selection and the expanded branches it had.
  1. 20
    Notice, expandedThe same place in the header row, raised one step inside a panel line, the whole text with its details wrapped at that place's width; what does not fit in the row's height scrolls inside it. The row stays 32px, so nothing below moves or is covered; another click folds it to its few words.

Rules

D-BAR-1
One bar of --bar-height along the window's bottom, on --surface-bar, opened above by --line-panel; it stands in the same place in every mode and nothing in it moves when a mode is switched. Three parts: at the left the brand (mark and name, semibold), then the Pane switch (the pane icon and Pane, borderless: --ink under a 2px --accent line along its top while the Panes are shown, --ink-2 without the line while they are hidden); in the middle, centred on the window, the three modes Chat, Verify and Review, each its icon and word, borderless and as tall as the bar, the shown one on --surface-group in --ink semibold under a 2px --accent line along its top, the others in --ink-2; Review's word is followed, while at least one Human review waits for a person, by their count: tabular figures at --text-label semibold in --human inside a pill of --radius-chip with a --line-group border, and nothing while none waits; at the right, in this order, the reviewer in --ink-2, the release notice, Commands, which may carry the palette's key cap, the one key cap a working screen shows (D-SYS-12), and Settings. The release notice, {version} available as a button before Commands, shows only while the app's release lookup (at start and once a day) has found a stable release newer than the running one, and never on a Microsoft Store install, which the Store updates; a click on it opens the Update dialog (D-OVL-5), and it stays after that dialog closes. a bar at the top; a bar that moves or changes size with a mode; a mode shown by colour alone; a count of zero; the count in the accent; a release notice for the running version, a pre-release or a Store install; a badge or dot standing in for it; a shadow over the bar; a second toolbar; page-like navigation; a key cap on the Pane switch or a mode; a switch whose state shows by colour alone; a switch for the Detail, which a node opens and Esc closes.
D-PANE-1
Verify's Pane is one --surface-panel column, top to bottom: the Search field (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group) and the Branches; the waiting reviews are Review's (D-REV-7) and the bar counts them (D-BAR-1). The Pane and the Main area meet at a 1px --line-panel divider that takes a drag. Hidden by the bar's Pane switch (D-BAR-1) or the palette, the Pane and its divider are gone and the Main area takes the window's width, its view as it was; hidden while focus is in it, focus moves to the Main area, the view unchanged. Shown again, the Pane has the width it had, its search, its selection and its expanded branches as they were (J-PANE-TOGGLE). The Search's query filters in place: every term must match, and the Branches keep only the branches it matches, in their own place and with their own rows; the starred branches stay first, then the others, each group in the query's order (sort:recent, the branch with the most recent Run first and branches without Runs last; sort:name, by project and branch). Focused with suggestions, the Search drops a list under the field on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: --row-dense rows in --mono at --text-small (keys, projects, branches, sort orders), the active one on --selection; a click on one completes the key or value in the field (J-PANE-SUGGEST). Right after a key is taken, the same list shows that key's values, none of them active yet; with the field unfocused or empty, no list shows. headings such as Scope or Runs; a list of waiting reviews in Verify's Pane; a gap instead of the divider; suggestions that widen the Pane.
D-PANE-2
A branch row: a 28px head with the chevron (the fold icon in a 20px box, the unfold icon while expanded), the name at --text-title as project / in --ink-2 and the branch bold, cut with an ellipsis, and the star at the end (the star icon in --ink-3 until on, then the filled star in --ink); folded, a 12px line under the name with an 8px dot in the status colour, the status word semibold in the same colour and the age right-aligned in tabular figures (a running Run its time so far, Running in --running); a branch without Runs has no such line. Rows are separated by --line-group. a card per branch; the project louder than the branch; an age in proportional figures.
D-PANE-3
An expanded branch's timeline: entries of at most two 12px lines (status word semibold and age right; under it one line in --ink-2, the comment the Run was requested with, cut to one line, or else its Run ID; the Current has its word alone), dots of 8px joined by a 1px --line-group line, the Current a ring; newest first, it starts with the Current's ring, or, while a Run is in progress on the branch, with that Run (its dot in --running, Running and its time so far in tabular figures where an age stands) and no Current ring until that Run ends; the entry shown in the Main area in a --surface-group pill of --radius-control; the selected entry has the --selection background and a 2px inset bar while the keyboard is in the Pane. While older Runs exist, Older runs ends the timeline as a text button in --accent at --text-small, indented to the entries' text, and brings them in below the oldest. pills on every entry; a status colour as the pill; a Run's ID and its comment both; a line that explains the Current; a pager instead of Older runs; a Current ring above or beside a Run in progress.
D-MAIN-1
The Main area header is one 32px row that says what is shown: the view's name at --text-small semibold in --ink-2 (--ink while the Main area has focus): a Run's ID, Current · {project} {branch}, Review; then, in the same row at --text-small, the view's headline (a Run's verdict icon and word bold in the status colour, then what needs attention: each eval that is RED or ERROR and each sign-off that waits, as its status's icon in that status's colour and its name in --ink, the failures first; a Current's scan state, D-CUR-1; a review's eval, as {Artifact}/{eval}, and its title, D-REV-1), cut at the row's end when the row is short; then a notice in --red, a few words that say what went wrong and how many (2 Runs cannot be read), at least 96px wide, taking the remaining room; actions at the end as small buttons. A Run's tally, its duration and its comment are in the Run's Detail (D-INS-7), which a click on the headline opens. A click on the notice shows it in full, with its details, within the header (J-MAIN-RUN): in its own place in the row, on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border and --radius-control corners, its text wrapped at that place's width and scrolling inside the row's height, so the header keeps its 32px and the tool strip and the graph below it are neither moved nor covered; another click folds it to its few words. A focused panel shows a 2px --accent line at its top. a large title; a second row for the headline; a sentence that repeats the verdict; a tally or a duration in the header; a notice that moves the graph; a notice that cannot be read whole; an expanded notice that grows the header or drops over the tool strip or the graph.
D-MAIN-2
Nothing stands between the header row and a graph's tool strip. What needs attention is in the Run's headline (D-MAIN-1); its comment in full and what it waits for are in the Run's Detail (D-INS-7), and the Pane's timeline shows the comment cut to one line (D-PANE-3). The Run's headline, in the header row, takes the click that opens the Run's Detail. a coloured banner; a row of comment or attention under the header; the attention list inside the graph.
D-MAIN-3
The graph's place is kept whatever the Main area has to say about it: with nothing chosen (a launch without arguments), while a Run loads, when a Run cannot be read, or with an empty graph, one --ink-2 line says so at the top of the graph's place (Choose a branch in the Pane., or, with the Pane hidden, Show the Pane with the Pane switch to choose a branch., Loading the Run…, This Run cannot be read; choose another.) and the area below stays empty on --surface-panel. Once a Run is chosen, the tool strip stands directly under the header row in its usual place whatever its graph's state: while the Run loads, when it cannot be read and with an empty graph, the strip stays and that line stands below it, in the graph's place; while the Run loads or cannot be read there is nothing to act on, and the strip's controls are unavailable, not hidden; on a Run read whole whose graph is empty they stay usable as on any graph: Find opens its field, there saying how to star an Artifact (D-OVL-4), and the zoom and Fit View act on the empty canvas. The strip is left out only while nothing is chosen. What went wrong goes to the header's notice (D-MAIN-1), a few words that a click shows in full: for the chosen Run that cannot be read, why it cannot be read (Run cannot be read, its full reason on a click), while the graph's place keeps its line; for other Runs, how many cannot be read. an empty-state illustration; a dialog for a Run that cannot be read; the reason a Run cannot be read written in the graph's place instead of the header's notice; the Pane changing size with the Main area's state; the tool strip removed, or the message put above it, while a Run loads or cannot be read.
D-MAIN-4
A Run a Chat session started carries its way back in the header row, after the headline and before a notice: a reference chip (D-REF-1), the Chat icon and Chat · {session title}, the title cut with an ellipsis at the chip's 320px; the chip never wraps or pushes the notice out of the row, and a long headline is cut before it. A Run started from the CLI or an MCP client shows no chip; its Detail's Provenance says what started it (D-INS-7). the session's ID instead of its title; the way back as a sentence or a second row; a chip on a Current.
D-REF-1
A reference that shows another mode's exact target, in a header row or a panel, is a chip 20px high: a --line-group border of --radius-chip, its icon and words in --accent at --text-small, IDs in --mono, one line cut with an ellipsis. Its icon says where it goes: the Chat icon to a session, the back icon from a review to its Run and Artifact (Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}, D-REV-8). A reference inside a list or a card is a text button in --accent led by the open icon, naming the mode it opens: Open in Verify (D-CHAT-5), Open in Review (D-INS-12). Hovering steps the chip's border up to --line-input and opens nothing. a reference in a status colour; a reference that does not name its target; an underlined URL; a reference that opens on hover.
D-GRAPH-1
The graph canvas is the working surface, --surface-app, opened by a --line-group line under the graph's tool strip (D-GRAPH-4). Nothing rests on it but the graph, over its grid (D-GRAPH-6): the find field (D-OVL-4) floats over its top left only while it is open, and the Navigator (D-GRAPH-5) sits at its bottom right only while part of the graph lies outside the view; the zoom controls are in the tool strip, and what a graph's marks mean is in Help (D-OVL-5). a white canvas inside a dark panel; a legend, controls or a hint floating on the canvas.
D-GRAPH-4
The graph's tool strip is one row of --row-head between the Main area's header row and the canvas, on --surface-panel, opened above by --line-group; a Run's and a Current's graph each have it. It holds what acts on the graph and nothing else, from the left: Find, a small button with the find icon and its word, which opens the find field; the zoom group, one bordered group 22px high of Zoom out (the minus icon), the zoom in percent in tabular figures, and Zoom in (the add icon); Fit, a small button with the fit icon and its word, which is Fit View; on a Current, then, New Artifact and Reset layout (D-CUR-3). The rest of the row stays empty. The Detail docks under the strip, so the strip stays whole and usable with the Detail open. controls floating on the canvas; Find as an icon alone; a legend, a hint or a key cap in the strip; the strip under the Detail; a second strip or a second line.
D-NODE-5
Ports tell an edge's direction by shape and side, and carry no status: a node, a whole's box or a minimized whole that edges reach shows one input port, a triangle pointing into it, at the middle of its left side; one that edges leave shows one output port, a square, at the middle of its right side. A port is --port wide and high, straddles the outline, is filled in the edges' neutral --edge and takes no pointer; a side that no drawn edge meets has no port, and many edges on one side share its one port. The neighbours drawing in the Detail draws the same ports. a port per edge; the same shape on both sides; a port in a status colour; arrowheads on the edges instead; a port that can be dragged or clicked.
D-GRAPH-2
A graph too large to draw at once (J-GRAPH-LARGE) draws what the camera brings into view, at one of two levels chosen by the zoom alone, by whether names can be read, never by how many Artifacts are in view. Where a node's name would be too small to read, the node is one block filled with its status's colour, without outline, icon, name, tally or ports; a whole is its box's outline in its status's colour; an edge is one device pixel of --edge, or of its Artifact's colour when it leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked one (D-NODE-4). From the zoom at which names read, each node is painted as its element draws it (the --surface-panel face, the --line-node outline, the icon and the strip in its status's colour, its name, its tally, its ports), a whole as its neutral box with its title row, and edges at their regular stroke. At both levels the opened Artifact keeps its --outline-selected outline. In the picture a click on a painted node, block or whole opens its Detail, which names it, and a click on a painted Minimize or Expand button acts as on any graph (J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE); a drag that starts on a block pans the camera, on a Current too, and never moves the Artifact: a node is dragged only where it is drawn as a node (D-CUR-4); panning at a zoom where names do not read draws the Artifacts it brings into view as blocks and whole outlines too; only zooming in until names read draws them as elements again, a whole's title row, name and Minimize button included, whole in every part of D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4, and panning at that zoom draws what it brings into view as elements. a second palette for the picture; blocks where names would read; neutral boxes too small to tell apart; a painted button that takes no click; a block that can be dragged to move its Artifact.
D-GRAPH-3
A graph first shows whole and never enlarged: the camera fits every node into the canvas and stops at the nodes' own size, so a small graph sits at 168 × 40–44px nodes with 13px names in the middle of the canvas, the working surface empty around it; Fit View does the same. Only the person's own zooming goes beyond that size. a few nodes blown up to fill the canvas; a node's name larger than the UI's text when the graph first shows.
D-GRAPH-5
The Navigator shows whenever part of a graph lies outside the view, whatever the graph's size, and goes when all of it is in view: a panel about 172px wide at the canvas's bottom right, 8px in, left of the Detail when one is open. Unlike the find field, which floats over the top left only while it is open, the Navigator is anchored to that corner of the canvas: it does not move with the camera, is not opened or closed by a person, and is raised one step as what floats is (D-SYS-2), on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border, --radius-control corners and at most the faint shadow. It draws the whole graph in small, each Artifact a block of its status's colour (the blocks of D-GRAPH-2), a whole included, as a fainter block of its colour behind its parts' blocks, outlined in that colour, and frames the part in view with a 1px --outline-selected line over --selection. A click or a drag in it moves the camera there (J-GRAPH-NAVIGATOR); hovering it opens nothing. a Navigator shown for a graph that is all in view; one that covers the Detail; names or edges drawn in it; a second palette.
D-GRAPH-6
The canvas carries a faint grid: 1px lines of --line-group at 40% every --grid, moving and scaling with the camera, under the edges and the nodes. It is left out wherever the zoom makes its cells smaller than 8px on screen, as in the picture of a large graph zoomed far out. a grid as strong as a group line; dots or crosses; a grid that stays still while the graph pans; a grid on a panel.
D-GRAPH-7
A Run's graph is laid out from the branch's definitions as they are now: wherever they save places ([annotations.style], written on a Current, D-CUR-4), the Run's graph draws its Artifacts at those places, at the top level and inside each whole's box, the same places as the branch's Current, also after a restart; an Artifact the definitions keep no place for (one added later, or one this Run has but the definitions do not place) goes into free space of its box, and a box or top level without saved places is laid out automatically. The Run's graph only shows these places: it has no Reset layout, no New Artifact, no drop target and no lifted node, and a drag on it pans. a Run laid out apart from its branch's saved places; an Artifact without a place drawn over another; a layout control or a draggable node on a Run's graph.
D-NODE-1
A node is 168 × 40–44px: a --surface-panel face, a 1px --line-node outline, --radius-control corners. Its status shows in two places and nowhere else, both in the status's one colour (D-SYS-6): the status's icon (--icon, D-SYS-8) before the name, and a strip --strip high along its bottom, inside the outline. After the icon comes the name at 13px medium in --ink, cut with an ellipsis; then, when the Artifact has evals, its tally at --text-label in --ink-2 with tabular figures: how many of its evals are GREEN of how many it has (2/3), on a Current how many would be reused; an Artifact still being checked shows none. Help pairs each status's icon with its word (D-OVL-5), as does every list of evals that names a status (an eval row, a status line; an eval chip shows the icon beside the eval's name without a status word, D-INS-3); the Pane is the exception: its branches and timelines say a Run's status by an 8px dot in its colour and its word, and the Current by a ring, without the icon (D-PANE-2, D-PANE-3). an outline or a face in the status colour; a plain dot where the icon goes; a name or a tally in the status colour; a colour for a status that a list does not use for it.
D-NODE-2
Selection and focus change the outline, the face and the strip unchanged, and show never more than two marks at once: the opened Artifact's node has its own outline turned --outline-selected and 2px, with no gap; the node under the pointer has its outline stepped up to --ink-3; the 2px --focus ring sits 2px outside and shows only while the keyboard moves focus, so a node opened by a click carries the one outline. The opened node and the Detail name the same Artifact; when the opened Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, that whole carries the outline (every copy of a shared part carries it too). the accent as the selection; a filled face for selection; a focus ring left on a node after a click; three rings on one node.
D-NODE-3
A whole is a box with a 1px --line-node outline and a face of --surface-panel at 40%; along the box's top edge, across the box, runs a --strip strip in its status's colour, and right under it its title row (28–32px) holds its status's icon, its name semibold, its tally and the Minimize button (18–20px, --line-group border, the minus icon) at the right, on a Current led by Reset layout while the box is placed by hand (D-CUR-6); the title row is not closed below by a strip, the parts' area following it. A part of several wholes is drawn as a copy inside each expanded whole that holds it, each copy with the part's own edges, and stays drawn inside those not minimized. A whole that is itself a part of another is a box inside that whole's box, laid out with the other parts there, as deep as the parts go; edges to and from a whole meet its box's edge. Minimized, it is a node of a node's size and anatomy with its parts' count in --ink-2 under its name, a second --line-node outline drawn behind it, and the same button at its right now reading Expand (the plus icon); either toggle lays the graph out again with the whole's top left corner kept where it was on the screen, opening and closing no Detail, and the whole stays minimized, or expanded, on every graph of its branch, its Runs' and its Current, until it is toggled again or the app quits; the edges of the Artifacts it hides meet that node instead, those between it and the same Artifact drawn once in the light that comes first, and edges among the Artifacts it hides are not drawn. the whole's strip under its title row or at the box's bottom; two edges between a minimized whole and the same Artifact; an edge drawn to an Artifact a minimized whole hides; a nested whole drawn outside its whole's box; a whole's outline or face in its status colour; the button inside the parts' area; a minimized whole without its Expand button.
D-NODE-6
An Artifact's comment is text and nothing else, on both graphs: --text-small in --ink-2, starting 4px under its node, or under a whole's title row inside its box at the title's left inset, under a minimized whole, and under every copy of a shared part; it wraps at the width of what it is under (a node's 168px, a box's inner width), keeps its line breaks and shows at most three lines, the third ending in an ellipsis when there is more. It moves with its node, and the space it takes counts in the layout, so a box fits around its parts with their comments. Where Artifacts are painted as blocks (D-GRAPH-2) and inside a minimized whole, no comment is drawn. Read aloud after its node, it is not a Tab stop of its own. On a Run's graph a comment takes no edit: a click on it opens its Artifact's Detail as a click on its node does, and a drag that starts on it pans the camera, the comment staying where it is and no comment field opening (J-COMMENT). a card, outline, background, colour, icon or count for a comment; a comment that opens or grows on hover; Markdown rendered; an author or a time; a comment overlapping a node or an edge.
D-NODE-4
Edges are 1.25–1.5px strokes in the neutral --edge, meeting nodes at their sides and boxes at their edges. One kind of edge is coloured: an edge that leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked Artifact is drawn in that Artifact's status colour, so what holds a blocked Artifact can be traced upstream by eye; every other edge, from a GREEN, in progress or waiting Artifact, is neutral. A selected node gets no highlighted edges. an edge in the colour of GREEN, in progress or waiting; thick or animated edges; arrowheads larger than the node's icon.
