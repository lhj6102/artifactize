Verify: the monitor with a Run shown

The window after artifactize-desktop starts: the Verify mode above the bottom bar, its Pane on the left (search and branches), where the shown Run's entry is the selected row, and its Main area: the stage header, controls only and the same row as Chat's, with the way back to the Chat session that started the Run at its left and the graph's tools at its right; then the graph, the whole height under it, a status line at its top left. Nothing is selected on the graph yet; the Detail is closed. Started without arguments, the Main area shows Nothing chosen instead (D-MAIN-3) until a branch or one of its Runs is chosen in the Pane, or a Run is opened from a reference. The waiting reviews are Review's; the bar counts them.

  1. 1
    Bottom barAlong the window's bottom in every mode, as the reference screens draw it: the brand alone at the left, which opens the command palette; Chat, Verify and Review in the middle, Verify on, Review with the count of Human reviews waiting; Settings alone at the right. One line above it separates it from the work. Measured on Resolve's own page bar (Blackmagic's Edit page screenshots, the Edit and Color pages' bars at 2100px): items not shown have a grey label (≈ #919191 at its brightest) and a grey icon (≈ #848589) on the bar (≈ #17181a); the shown page has a white label (#ffffff) and its icon lit, on a black cell (≈ #010000), with a thin red marker along the cell's bottom (blurred to ≈ #951f1f at that scale); its label covers about as many pixels as the same word unshown (60 against 66 at half its peak), so Resolve does not bold it; no separators between items; no screenshot shows a hover. Our tokens take those levels: --bar-ink #909296, --bar-ink-on #ffffff, --bar-active #0b0b0d, --bar-mark #e0443c.

Rules

D-BAR-1
One bar of --bar-height along the window's bottom, a page bar as a video editor's is: on --surface-bar, the darkest surface, opened above by --line-panel and with no other line in it; it stands in the same place in every mode and nothing in it moves when a mode is switched. Three parts: at the left the brand alone (mark and name, semibold, in --bar-ink), a borderless control whose click opens the command palette (D-OVL-2), hovering only stepping its ink to --ink; in the middle, centred on the window, the three modes Chat, Verify and Review side by side with no gap or line between them, each a borderless cell 80px wide and as tall as the bar, its icon at --icon-bar over its word at --text-label, both in --bar-ink; the shown one's cell is --bar-active, the bar's black, its icon and word in --bar-ink-on, white, at the same weight as the others, with a 2px --bar-mark line along the cell's bottom: emphasis is a grey level, white for the shown mode and grey for the rest, never boldness; hovering a mode not shown steps its icon and word to --ink and nothing else. While at least one Human review waits for a person, their count sits 3px right of Review's icon, at its top: regular tabular figures at --text-label in --bar-count-ink, centred on a borderless, filled --bar-count-size circle in --bar-count-fill. Counts above 99 read 99+ in a pill of the same height; the accessible name keeps the exact count. The chip has at least 4.5:1 text contrast, stays quieter than the shown mode, and does not appear while none waits; at the right Settings alone, as its icon only: the gear, i-settings, at --icon-bar in --bar-ink, in a cell as tall as the bar and 44px wide, hovering stepping it to --ink, its name given to assistive technology and as its tooltip, never shown as on. The bar's mark is the bar's alone: it is not a status, and the shown mode is also told by its black cell and its white, not by the mark's colour alone. What the bar no longer holds has its place elsewhere: the Pane is shown and hidden from the palette (J-PANE-TOGGLE), the reviewer's name and the release notice stand in Settings' header row (D-SET-1). a bar at the top; a bar that moves or changes size with a mode; a mode shown by colour alone; a count of zero; the count in the accent; a Pane switch, a reviewer's name, a release notice or a Commands button in the bar; a badge or dot on Settings; a word beside or under Settings' icon; a count on a bright filled plate or touching the icon; a shadow over the bar; the mark in any status colour or used anywhere else; separators between the modes; a mode's word beside its icon in one line; the accent on the bar; a second toolbar; page-like navigation; a key cap in the bar; a switch for the Detail, which a node opens and Esc closes.
D-PANE-1
Verify's Pane is one --surface-panel column, top to bottom: the Search field (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group) and the Branches; the waiting reviews are Review's (D-REV-7) and the bar counts them (D-BAR-1). The Pane and the Main area meet at a 1px --line-panel divider that takes a drag. Hidden from the palette's Show or hide the Pane (J-PANE-TOGGLE), the Pane and its divider are gone and the Main area takes the window's width, its view as it was; hidden while focus is in it, focus moves to the Main area, the view unchanged. Shown again, the Pane has the width it had, its search, its selection and its expanded branches as they were (J-PANE-TOGGLE). The Search's query filters in place: every term must match, and the Branches keep only the branches it matches, in their own place and with their own rows; the starred branches stay first, then the others, each group in the query's order (sort:recent, the branch with the most recent Run first and branches without Runs last; sort:name, by project and branch). Focused with suggestions, the Search drops a list under the field on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: --row-dense rows in --mono at --text-small (keys, projects, branches, sort orders), the active one on --selection; a click on one completes the key or value in the field (J-PANE-SUGGEST). Right after a key is taken, the same list shows that key's values, none of them active yet; with the field unfocused or empty, no list shows. headings such as Scope or Runs; a list of waiting reviews in Verify's Pane; a gap instead of the divider; suggestions that widen the Pane.
D-PANE-2
A branch row: a --row-pane head, chosen by a click anywhere on it across the Pane's width (J-PANE-ROWS); folded, the head and its newest Run's line under it are one row, one hit and focus area: a click anywhere on either shows the branch's newest Run, the pointer lights both together and the keyboard's ring surrounds both, the two lines sharing one --row-pane row's height; the chevron, which only expands or folds the branch, and the star are targets of their own; the pointer over it gives the whole row --surface-group from edge to edge, and the keyboard on it a 2px --focus ring inside the whole row; the star stays its own button at the row's end, with the chevron (the fold icon in a 20px box, the unfold icon while expanded), the name at --text-title as project / in --ink-2 and the branch bold, cut with an ellipsis, and the star at the end (the star, its own button with a 24 × 24px hit area around its --icon glyph, in --ink-2 until on, then the filled star in --ink); folded, a 12px line under the name with an 8px dot in the status colour, the status word in the same colour at the row's weight and the age right-aligned in tabular figures (a running Run its time so far, Running in --running); a branch without Runs has no such line. Rows are separated by --line-group. a card per branch; the project louder than the branch; an age in proportional figures.
D-PANE-3
An expanded branch's timeline: entries of at most two 12px lines, each chosen by a click anywhere on it, across the Pane's width and exactly --row-pane high, as every Pane row is (J-PANE-ROWS) (status word in its colour at the row's weight and age right; under it one line in --ink-2, the comment the Run was requested with, cut to one line, or else its Run ID; dots of 8px joined by a 1px --line-group line; newest first, Runs only (a branch's Current is Chat's, D-CUR-10), it starts, while a Run is in progress on the branch, with that Run (its dot in --running, Running and its time so far in tabular figures where an age stands); the entry shown in the Main area is the darker --selection row across the Pane, its words brighter (D-SYS-13), its indent and the timeline's line kept inside the row; under the pointer an entry takes --surface-group across the whole row, and the keyboard on it a 2px --focus ring inside it; the pointer never lights the shown entry. While older Runs exist, Older runs ends the timeline as a Pane row of its own, across the Pane and --row-pane high as every Pane row is (J-PANE-ROWS), its words in --ink-2 at --text-small indented to the entries' text, lit on --surface-group under the pointer and ringed by the keyboard's focus, and brings them in below the oldest. pills on every entry; a selection or hover that starts at the timeline's indent; a status colour as the row; a Run's ID and its comment both; a Current entry or ring in Verify; a pager instead of Older runs.
D-MAIN-1
Chat and Verify open their Main area with one stage header, the same row in both: 32px high on --surface-panel, closed below by --line-panel, its words at --text-small, and controls only. Three places: at the left the way back when the view has one, a reference chip (D-REF-1, D-MAIN-4); in the middle, centred on the Main area, the view tabs when the mode has more than one view (Chat's Conversation and Graph, D-CHAT-10; Verify has none); at the right the tools of the view shown, ending at the row's --pad. A tool is borderless and faceless, a word, an icon with its word, or an icon alone with its accessible name and native tooltip: its ink --ink-2, stepping to --ink under the pointer, --ink-bright while what it opens is open, --ink-3 while there is nothing to act on, at weight 400; the keyboard's ring is the 2px --focus ring inside it (D-SYS-7). Groups of tools stand apart at a 1px --line-group separator 16px high. A graph's tools (D-GRAPH-4) are Find, the zoom and Fit, on a Current then its own (D-CUR-3), and on a Run then Run details, a word, --ink-bright while the Run's Detail is open (D-INS-7), and Open in Chat, led by the open icon, which shows the branch's Current in Chat (J-OPEN-CHAT); Conversation's one tool is Find (D-CHAT-11). Find, the find icon alone, turns into its field in its own place when clicked, up to 240px wide, the tools left of it moving left (D-OVL-4, D-CHAT-11). Nothing in the row names or rates what is shown: the Run's ID, verdict and age and the session's title, branch and state are the Pane's selected row (D-PANE-3, D-CHAT-1), and with the Pane hidden the window's title says them (J-PANE-TOGGLE); a graph's state and what went wrong are the stage's status line (D-MAIN-2). With nothing chosen the row stands empty at its height. The Review mode's header is its own (D-REV-1). Which panel has focus is not marked by a line or a colour of the panel: keyboard focus is the focused control's ring (D-SYS-7). an ID, a title, a branch, a verdict, a count, a time or a notice in the stage header; buttons with a border or a face in it; a second row of tools under it; tools that move between Chat and Verify; a tool strip over the canvas; an accent line along a focused panel's top; a large title.
D-MAIN-2
What the shown graph's state says stands in the stage's status line: one line on the canvas at its top left, --pad in and 8px down, on no face, its words at --text-small in --ink-2 with tabular figures: on a Current its scan state (D-CUR-1); on either graph a notice in --red, a few words that say what went wrong and how many (2 Runs cannot be read), cut with an ellipsis at the canvas's width. A click on the notice shows it in full in the same place: raised one step (D-SYS-2), --surface-raised inside a --line-panel border of --radius-control, 460px wide, its text wrapped and at most 96px high, scrolling inside itself, over the graph, which does not move; another click or Esc folds it to its few words. Fit and the camera's first view keep the top 32px of the canvas for this line. A Run's tally, its duration, its comment in full, what needs attention and what it waits for are in the Run's Detail (D-INS-7), which the stage header's Run details opens; the Pane's timeline shows the Run's status and its comment cut to one line (D-PANE-3). a coloured banner; a row of comment or attention under the header; the status line in the stage header; a face behind the status line while folded; an expanded notice that moves the graph.
D-MAIN-3
The graph's place is kept whatever the Main area has to say about it: with nothing chosen (a launch without arguments), while a Run loads, when a Run cannot be read, or with an empty graph, one --ink-2 line says so at the top of the graph's place (Choose a branch in the Pane., or, with the Pane hidden, Show the Pane from the command palette to choose a branch., Loading the Run…, This Run cannot be read; choose another.; for a branch without Runs, No Runs on this branch yet. followed by Open in Chat, a text reference in --ink-2 led by the open icon (D-REF-1, J-OPEN-CHAT), on the same line) and the area below stays empty on --surface-panel. Once a Run is chosen, the stage header holds the graph's tools whatever its graph's state: while the Run loads or cannot be read there is nothing to act on, and the tools are drawn unavailable in --ink-3, not hidden; on a Run read whole whose graph is empty they stay usable as on any graph: Find opens its field, there saying how to star an Artifact (D-OVL-4), and the zoom and Fit act on the empty canvas. The stage header holds no tools only while nothing is chosen. What went wrong goes to the status line's notice (D-MAIN-2), a few words that a click shows in full: for the chosen Run that cannot be read, why it cannot be read (Run cannot be read, its full reason on a click), while the graph's place keeps its line; for other Runs, how many cannot be read. an empty-state illustration; a dialog for a Run that cannot be read; the reason a Run cannot be read written in the graph's place instead of the notice; the Pane changing size with the Main area's state; the tools removed while a Run loads or cannot be read.
D-MAIN-4
A Run a Chat session started carries its way back at the stage header's left (D-MAIN-1): a reference chip (D-REF-1), the back icon and Chat · {session title}, the title cut with an ellipsis at the chip's 320px; the chip never wraps and never pushes the tools. A Run started from the CLI or an MCP client shows no chip; its Detail's Provenance says what started it (D-INS-7). the session's ID instead of its title; the way back as a sentence or a second row; a chip on a Current.
D-REF-1
A reference that shows another mode's exact target, in a header row or a panel, is a chip 20px high: a --line-group border of --radius-chip, its icon and words in --ink-2 at --text-small, IDs in --mono, one line cut with an ellipsis. A way back stands first in its header row, at its left, led by the back icon: from a Run to its Chat session (Chat · {session title}, D-MAIN-4), from a review to its Run and Artifact (Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}, D-REV-8). A reference inside a list or a card, or among the stage header's tools, is a text button in --ink-2 led by the open icon, naming the mode it opens: Open in Verify (D-CHAT-5), Open in Review (D-INS-12), Open in Chat (D-MAIN-1). Hovering steps the chip's border up to --line-input and opens nothing. a reference in a status colour; a reference that does not name its target; an underlined URL; a reference that opens on hover.
D-GRAPH-1
The graph canvas is the working surface, --surface-app, filling the Main area under the stage header (D-MAIN-1). On it rest the graph, over its grid (D-GRAPH-6), and four things in fixed places: the status line at its top left (D-MAIN-2); Find's list under the find field while it is open (D-OVL-4); the Navigator at its bottom right only while part of the graph lies outside the view (D-GRAPH-5); and, in Chat's Graph view, the floating chat at its bottom centre (D-CUR-10). The zoom is among the stage header's tools, and what a graph's marks mean is in Help (D-OVL-5). a white canvas inside a dark panel; a legend, other controls or a hint floating on the canvas.
D-GRAPH-4
A graph's tools stand at the right of the stage header (D-MAIN-1), a Run's and a Current's alike, from the left: Find, the find icon alone, which turns into the find field in its place (D-OVL-4); a separator; the zoom, Zoom out (the minus icon), the zoom in percent in --ink with tabular figures, 44px wide, and Zoom in (the add icon); a separator; Fit, the fit icon and its word, which is Fit View; on a Current then its own (D-CUR-3), on a Run Run details and Open in Chat. Each is a stage tool, borderless, by luminance alone. The Detail docks under the stage header, so the tools stay whole and usable with the Detail open. controls floating on the canvas; a bordered zoom group; a legend, a hint or a key cap among the tools; a tool strip between the header and the canvas.
D-NODE-5
Ports tell an edge's direction by shape and side, and carry no status: a node, a whole's box or a minimized whole that edges reach shows one input port, a triangle pointing into it, at the middle of its left side; one that edges leave shows one output port, a square, at the middle of its right side. A port is --port wide and high, straddles the outline, is filled in the edges' neutral --edge and takes no pointer; a side that no drawn edge meets has no port, and many edges on one side share its one port. The neighbours drawing in the Detail draws the same ports. a port per edge; the same shape on both sides; a port in a status colour; arrowheads on the edges instead; a port that can be dragged or clicked.
D-GRAPH-2
A graph too large to draw at once (J-GRAPH-LARGE) draws what the camera brings into view, at one of two levels chosen by the zoom alone, by whether names can be read, never by how many Artifacts are in view. Where a node's name would be too small to read, the node is one block filled with its status's colour, without outline, icon, name, tally or ports; a whole is its box's outline in its status's colour; an edge is one device pixel of --edge, or of its Artifact's colour when it leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked one (D-NODE-4). From the zoom at which names read, each node is painted as its element draws it (the --surface-panel face, the --line-node outline, the icon and the strip in its status's colour, its name, its tally, its ports), a whole as its neutral box with its title row, and edges at their regular stroke. At both levels the opened Artifact keeps its --current-mark ring (D-NODE-2). In the picture a click on a painted node, block or whole opens its Detail, which names it, and a click on a painted Minimize or Expand button acts as on any graph (J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE); a drag that starts on a block pans the camera, on a Current too, and never moves the Artifact: a node is dragged only where it is drawn as a node (D-CUR-4); panning at a zoom where names do not read draws the Artifacts it brings into view as blocks and whole outlines too; only zooming in until names read draws them as elements again, a whole's title row, name and Minimize button included, whole in every part of D-NODE-1 to D-NODE-4, and panning at that zoom draws what it brings into view as elements. a second palette for the picture; blocks where names would read; neutral boxes too small to tell apart; a painted button that takes no click; a block that can be dragged to move its Artifact.
D-GRAPH-3
A graph first shows whole and never enlarged: the camera fits every node into the canvas and stops at the nodes' own size, so a small graph sits at 168 × 40–44px nodes with 13px names in the middle of the canvas, the working surface empty around it; Fit View does the same. Only the person's own zooming goes beyond that size. a few nodes blown up to fill the canvas; a node's name larger than the UI's text when the graph first shows.
D-GRAPH-5
The Navigator shows whenever part of a graph lies outside the view, whatever the graph's size, and goes when all of it is in view: a panel about 172px wide at the bottom right of the canvas's free area, 8px in, left of the Detail when one is open and, in Chat's Graph view, above the band the floating chat keeps (D-CUR-10). It is anchored to that corner: it does not move with the camera, is not opened or closed by a person, and is raised one step as what floats is (D-SYS-2), on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border, --radius-control corners and at most the faint shadow. It draws the whole graph in small, each Artifact a block of its status's colour (the blocks of D-GRAPH-2), a whole included, as a fainter block of its colour behind its parts' blocks, outlined in that colour, and frames the part in view with a 1px --outline-selected line over --selection. A click or a drag in it moves the camera there (J-GRAPH-NAVIGATOR); hovering it opens nothing. a Navigator shown for a graph that is all in view; one that covers the Detail or the floating chat; names or edges drawn in it; a second palette.
D-GRAPH-6
The canvas carries a faint grid: 1px lines of --line-group at 40% every --grid, moving and scaling with the camera, under the edges and the nodes. It is left out wherever the zoom makes its cells smaller than 8px on screen, as in the picture of a large graph zoomed far out. a grid as strong as a group line; dots or crosses; a grid that stays still while the graph pans; a grid on a panel.
D-GRAPH-7
A Run's graph is laid out from the branch's definitions as they are now: wherever they save places ([annotations.style], written on a Current, D-CUR-4), the Run's graph draws its Artifacts at those places, at the top level and inside each whole's box, the same places as the branch's Current, also after a restart; an Artifact the definitions keep no place for (one added later, or one this Run has but the definitions do not place) goes into free space of its box, and a box or top level without saved places is laid out automatically. The Run's graph only shows these places: it has no Reset layout, no New Artifact, no drop target and no lifted node, and a drag on it pans. a Run laid out apart from its branch's saved places; an Artifact without a place drawn over another; a layout control or a draggable node on a Run's graph.
D-NODE-1
A node is 168 × 40–44px: a --surface-panel face, a 1px --line-node outline, --radius-control corners. Its status shows in two places and nowhere else, both in the status's one colour (D-SYS-6): the status's icon (--icon, D-SYS-8) before the name, and a strip --strip high along its bottom, inside the outline. After the icon comes the name at 13px medium in --ink, cut with an ellipsis; then, when the Artifact has evals, its tally at --text-label in --ink-2 with tabular figures: how many of its evals are GREEN of how many it has (2/3), on a Current how many would be reused; an Artifact still being checked shows none. Help pairs each status's icon with its word (D-OVL-5), as does every list of evals that names a status (an eval row, a status line; an eval chip shows the icon beside the eval's name without a status word, D-INS-3); the Pane is the exception: its branches and timelines say a Run's status by an 8px dot in its colour and its word, without the icon (D-PANE-2, D-PANE-3). an outline or a face in the status colour; a plain dot where the icon goes; a name or a tally in the status colour; a colour for a status that a list does not use for it.
D-NODE-2
Selection and focus change the outline, the face and the strip unchanged, and show never more than two marks at once: the opened Artifact's node keeps its own outline and is ringed by a 1px --current-mark line 2px outside it, a gap of the canvas between, so the ring never touches the status strip along the node's bottom; the node under the pointer has its outline stepped up to --ink-3; the 2px --focus ring sits 5px outside, beyond the selection's ring, and shows only while the keyboard moves focus, so a node opened by a click carries the one outline. The opened node and the Detail name the same Artifact; when the opened Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, that whole carries the outline (every copy of a shared part carries it too). the accent as the selection; a filled face for selection; a focus ring left on a node after a click; three rings on one node.
D-NODE-3
A whole is a box with a 1px --line-node outline and a face of --surface-panel at 40%; along the box's top edge, across the box, runs a --strip strip in its status's colour, and right under it its title row (28–32px) holds its status's icon, its name semibold, its tally and the Minimize button (18–20px, --line-group border, the minus icon) at the right, on a Current led by Reset layout while the box is placed by hand (D-CUR-6); the title row is not closed below by a strip, the parts' area following it. A part of several wholes is drawn as a copy inside each expanded whole that holds it, each copy with the part's own edges, and stays drawn inside those not minimized. A whole that is itself a part of another is a box inside that whole's box, laid out with the other parts there, as deep as the parts go; edges to and from a whole meet its box's edge. Minimized, it is a node of a node's size and anatomy with its parts' count in --ink-2 under its name, a second --line-node outline drawn behind it, and the same button at its right now reading Expand (the plus icon); either toggle lays the graph out again with the whole's top left corner kept where it was on the screen, opening and closing no Detail, and the whole stays minimized, or expanded, on every graph of its branch, its Runs' and its Current, until it is toggled again or the app quits; the edges of the Artifacts it hides meet that node instead, those between it and the same Artifact drawn once in the light that comes first, and edges among the Artifacts it hides are not drawn. the whole's strip under its title row or at the box's bottom; two edges between a minimized whole and the same Artifact; an edge drawn to an Artifact a minimized whole hides; a nested whole drawn outside its whole's box; a whole's outline or face in its status colour; the button inside the parts' area; a minimized whole without its Expand button.
D-NODE-6
An Artifact's comment is text and nothing else, on both graphs: --text-small in --ink-2, starting 4px under its node, or under a whole's title row inside its box at the title's left inset, under a minimized whole, and under every copy of a shared part; it wraps at the width of what it is under (a node's 168px, a box's inner width), keeps its line breaks and shows at most three lines, the third ending in an ellipsis when there is more. It moves with its node, and the space it takes counts in the layout, so a box fits around its parts with their comments. Where Artifacts are painted as blocks (D-GRAPH-2) and inside a minimized whole, no comment is drawn. Read aloud after its node, it is not a Tab stop of its own. On a Run's graph a comment takes no edit: a click on it opens its Artifact's Detail as a click on its node does, and a drag that starts on it pans the camera, the comment staying where it is and no comment field opening (J-COMMENT). a card, outline, background, colour, icon or count for a comment; a comment that opens or grows on hover; Markdown rendered; an author or a time; a comment overlapping a node or an edge.
D-NODE-4
Edges are 1.25–1.5px strokes in the neutral --edge, meeting nodes at their sides and boxes at their edges. One kind of edge is coloured: an edge that leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked Artifact is drawn in that Artifact's status colour, so what holds a blocked Artifact can be traced upstream by eye; every other edge, from a GREEN, in progress or waiting Artifact, is neutral. A selected node gets no highlighted edges. an edge in the colour of GREEN, in progress or waiting; thick or animated edges; arrowheads larger than the node's icon.
