Monitor: the one screen with a Run shown

The window after artifactize-desktop starts: the global bar, the Pane on the left (search, branches, the Waiting stack) and the Main area with the shown Run's headline and graph. Nothing is selected on the graph yet; the Detail is closed. Started without arguments, the Main area shows Nothing chosen instead (D-MAIN-3) until a branch or a waiting review is chosen in the Pane.

  1. 1
    Global barBrand left; reviewer, update notice, Settings and Commands right. One line below it separates it from the work.
  2. 2
    SearchA field one step below the Pane, monospace query, its starting sort:recent. Focused with suggestions, a raised list of key and value completions drops under it, the active one on the selection background.
  3. 3
    BranchesChevron, project / quiet and branch bold, star at the line's end; folded, the newest Run's dot, status word and age; expanded, the timeline with the Current's ring and the shown entry's pill, and Older runs at its end while older Runs exist.
  4. 4
    Waiting stackGrows upward from a thin labelled bar; each row an eval, where it waits, for how long and who claimed it.
  5. 5
    Main area headerThe view's title in 12px; a notice takes the room to the right in one red line; actions at the end.
  6. 6
    Run headlineThe verdict's glyph and word in its colour, the headline in secondary ink with tabular figures, the Run's comment in full, then what needs attention.
  7. 7
    NodeA light and a name on a neutral face; the border takes the light's colour. Hover is a thin neutral outline.
  8. 8
    WholeA box in its light's colour around its parts, its name and light in a 24px title row, the Minimize button at the right.
  9. 9
    Canvas furnitureLegend top-left, zoom controls and the key hint bottom-left, all quiet overlays on the working surface.

Rules

D-BAR-1
One global bar of --bar-height on --surface-bar, closed below by --line-panel: the brand (mark and name, semibold) at the left; at the right, in this order, the reviewer in --ink-2, the update notice, Settings and Commands with its Ctrl+K key cap. a shadow under the bar; a second toolbar; page-like navigation.
D-PANE-1
The Pane is one --surface-panel column, top to bottom: the Search field (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group), the Branches, and the Waiting stack opened above by --line-group. The Pane and the Main area meet at a 1px --line-panel divider that takes a drag. Focused with suggestions, the Search drops a list under the field on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: --row-dense rows in --mono at --text-small (keys, projects, branches, sort orders), the active one on --selection; Tab or Enter takes it, Esc closes it. headings such as Scope or Runs; a screen switch; a gap instead of the divider; suggestions that widen the Pane.
D-PANE-2
A branch row: a 28px head with the chevron (20px), the name at --text-title as project / in --ink-2 and the branch semibold, cut with an ellipsis, and the star at the end (faint until on, then --light-active); folded, a 12px line under the name with an 8px dot in the status colour, the status word semibold in the same colour and the age right-aligned in tabular figures. Rows are separated by --line-group. a card per branch; the project louder than the branch; an age in proportional figures.
D-PANE-3
An expanded branch's timeline: entries of two 12px lines (status word semibold and age right; Run ID, then a comment cut to one line, in --ink-2), dots of 8px joined by a 1px --line-group line, the Current a ring, the entry shown in the Main area in a --surface-group pill of --radius-control; the selected entry has the --selection background and a 2px inset bar while the keyboard is in the Pane. While older Runs exist, Older runs ends the timeline as a text button in --accent at --text-small, indented to the entries' text, and brings them in below the oldest. pills on every entry; a status colour as the pill; a pager instead of Older runs.
D-PANE-4
The Waiting stack: an 11px uppercase label WAITING (n) in --ink-2, then 12px rows of the eval (its glyph in --human), the project and branch, and age · claimant in --ink-2; selected rows use --selection. a bold heading; the stack taller than half the Pane.
D-MAIN-1
The Main area header is a 32px row: the view's title at --text-small semibold in --ink-2 (--ink while the Main area has focus), a notice in --red as one cut line taking the remaining room, actions at the end as small buttons with key caps; a click on the notice shows it in full, wrapped within the header, what is below neither moved nor hidden; another click shows it as one line again. A focused panel shows a 2px --accent line at its top. a large title; a notice that moves the graph; a notice that cannot be read whole.
D-MAIN-2
A Run shows, above its graph, its headline row (verdict glyph and word bold in the status colour; the headline in --ink-2 with tabular figures), its comment in full (wrapping, in --ink), and what needs attention as 12px lines each led by the glyph in its status colour. a coloured banner; the attention list inside the graph; the comment cut short here.
D-MAIN-3
The graph's place is kept whatever the Main area has to say about it: with nothing chosen (a launch without arguments), while a Run loads, when a Run cannot be read, or with an empty graph, one --ink-2 line at the top of the Main area's body says so (Choose a branch or a waiting review in the Pane., Loading the Run…, This Run cannot be read; choose another.) and the area below stays empty on --surface-panel; a notice about other Runs still goes to the header (D-MAIN-1). an empty-state illustration; a dialog for a Run that cannot be read; the Pane changing size with the Main area's state.
D-GRAPH-1
The graph canvas is the working surface, --surface-app, opened by a --line-group line under the header; its furniture floats quietly: the legend top-left and the key hint bottom-left at 11px in --ink-2 on --surface-panel at 85%, the zoom controls bottom-left as one bordered group of 24px squares. a white canvas inside a dark panel; furniture that takes the pointer from the graph.
D-NODE-1
A node is 168 × 40–44px: a --surface-panel face, a 1px border in its light's colour, --radius-control corners; inside, an 8–10px dot in the light's colour, then the name at 13px medium cut with an ellipsis. The legend's swatch, a node's border and dot and an edge's stroke share one light colour per verdict. a face tinted by the verdict; a border thicker than 1.5px; a name in the status colour.
D-NODE-2
Selection and focus are outlines, the face unchanged: the opened Artifact's node has a 2px --outline-selected outline outside its border; the node under the pointer a 1px --ink-3 outline; the focused node a 2px --focus ring. The opened node and the Detail name the same Artifact; when the opened Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, that whole carries the outline (every copy of a shared part carries it too). the accent as the selection; a filled face for selection.
D-NODE-3
A whole is a box with a 1px border in its light's colour and a face of --surface-panel at 40%, its title row (28–32px) holding the dot, the name semibold and the Minimize button (18–20px, --line-group border) at the right; minimized, it is a node of a node's size with its parts' count in --ink-2 under its name, a second edge drawn behind it, and the same button at its right now reading Expand (+). a whole filled like a node; the button inside the parts' area; a minimized whole without its Expand button.
D-NODE-4
Edges are 1.25–1.5px strokes in their light's colour, meeting nodes at their sides and boxes at their edges; a selected node gets no highlighted edges. thick or animated edges; arrowheads larger than the node's dot.
