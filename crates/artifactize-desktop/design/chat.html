Chat: a session with Artifactize's own agent

Chat mode, laid out as agent chat applications are: its Pane holds New chat and the sessions, grouped by project, one line each; its Main area holds the chosen session's conversation in a column of the reading measure and the composer under it. The agent's text reads as text, what it did as quiet one-line rows, and each verify it starts as a Run reference that opens the Run in Verify. The model selector sits at the composer's bottom left, its menu opening upward; Send, or Stop while a turn runs, at its bottom right.

  2. 2

Rules

D-CHAT-1
Chat's Pane is one --surface-panel column, as an agent chat application's sidebar: its head, a row closed below by --line-group, holds New chat, a button across the Pane's width with the add icon; under it the sessions, grouped by project, the groups and their sessions newest first, each group under its project's name as a group label (D-SYS-5), each session one --row line: its title in --ink, cut with an ellipsis, and its age at the right in --ink-3 with tabular figures. The shown session has --selection and, while the keyboard is in the list, the 2px inset bar (D-SYS-7). a second line under each session; the branch or the model in the list; a heading over the Pane; a card per session; a preview on hover.
D-CHAT-2
The Main area's header row (D-MAIN-1) names the session: its title as the view's name, then its branch in --mono --ink-2 as its headline. Under it, closed above by --line-panel, the conversation stands on --surface-app in a column of --measure centred in the area, newest at the bottom, scrolling on its own and following its newest line unless scrolled up. With no session shown, one --ink-2 line says how to start one (D-MAIN-3). the conversation stretched to the window's width; avatars; a time on every message; the model or the provider in the header.
D-CHAT-3
A person's message is a block at the column's right, at most 80% of its width, on --surface-group with --radius-chip and no border, its text as typed. The agent's turn runs at the column's left as plain text at --text-body in --ink, without a box, a name or an icon before it; a stopped turn ends with Stopped in --ink-2, a failed one with why in --red; a turn that ended well ends with its last words. a bubble around the agent's text; the model, the time or a token count under every turn; a sentence saying the turn went well.
D-CHAT-4
What the agent did is one quiet line per tool between its words, without a box or a background: an icon of what it does in --ink-3 (the file icon for a read, the folder icon for a listing or a search, the edit icon for an edit or a write, the command icon for a command, the Verify icon for Verify's other tools), then at --text-small in --ink-2 a verb (Read, Searched, Edited, Ran, …, in the present tense while it runs) and what it acted on in --mono, cut with an ellipsis; an edit adds the lines it added and removed, +N −M, in the same ink. While it runs, the in-progress icon in --running takes its icon's place. A tool that failed adds why in --red on the same line. A verify the agent started is not such a line but its Run reference (D-CHAT-5). a box, a background or a border per tool; a tick for a tool that went well; a time per tool; added and removed lines in GREEN and RED; a tool's output in the conversation.
D-CHAT-5
A Run reference is one row of 32px inside a --line-group border of --radius-control on --surface-panel: the Run's status icon in its colour, the Run ID in --mono, its status word bold in its colour (in progress, then its verdict, changing in place), what needs attention in --ink-2 (the eval that failed or waits for a person, cut with an ellipsis), and at the right Open in Verify, a text reference in --accent led by the open icon (D-REF-1). a coloured card or stripe for the Run's status; the word Verify repeated in the row; Open in Verify as a primary button.
D-CHAT-6
The composer stands under the conversation in the same --measure column, 12px above the area's bottom: one field on --surface-input with a --line-input border of --radius-chip, its text growing to eight lines, its placeholder Ask the agent to work on this branch… in --ink-3; under the text, inside the field, one row: the model selector at the left, the session's model in --ink with the unfold icon, borderless; at the right Send, the one primary control, an icon button of --control with the send icon and its accessible name, which becomes Stop, a quiet icon button with the stop icon, while a turn runs. Keyboard focus is the field's 2px ring (D-SYS-7). the selector above the field or at its right; the provider's name beside the model; Send at the left; a key cap; a disabled composer while a turn runs.
D-CHAT-7
The model menu opens upward from the selector, its bottom left at the selector's top left, raised (D-SYS-2): --surface-raised, a --line-panel border, --radius-control, the faint shadow. Each provider Settings lists is a group: its name as a group label, then its models as --row rows by name; the session's model has --selection and semibold; a provider that lists none says why in --red at --text-small in its group. After a --line-group line, Connect provider with the add icon ends the menu. Taller than the room above the selector, the menu scrolls inside itself. a menu that opens downward over the composer or on hover; a model chosen by colour alone; IDs or a line explaining the harness in the menu.
D-CHAT-8
The New chat dialog is a dialog as the overlays draw one (D-OVL-1): its title, the Branch field, a choice on --surface-input inside --line-input, then Model, labelled as a form's field is, its value plain text, not a field: the provider's name and the model in --mono at --text-small; while the providers' models are being read it says Reading the providers' models… in --ink-2, and once they are read with none listed, No provider lists a model. Connect one in Settings. in --ink-2. Cancel and the primary Create end its last row, Create disabled until the model is known. Create offered before the model is known; the no-model sentence while the models are still being read; a model choice in the dialog (the composer's selector changes it).
