Chat: a session with Artifactize's own agent

Chat mode: its Pane lists the sessions, newest first, with New at its top; its Main area shows the chosen session: one header row with its title and its branch, then the conversation in a column of the reading measure and, under it, the composer. A turn streams in as the agent works: its text, one row per tool it uses and, for each verify it starts, a Run reference that opens that Run in Verify. The provider and model selector sits at the composer's bottom left, its menu opening upward; Send, or Stop while a turn runs, at its bottom right.

  1. 1
    Chats and NewThe Pane's head: its name, Chats, and at its right one small button, New, which opens the dialog that asks for the session's branch.
  2. 2
    SessionsNewest first, each its title and, under it, its project, branch and age; the shown one on the selection. A long title is cut with an ellipsis inside the Pane.
  3. 3
    Header rowThe session's title, then its project and branch as its headline in the quieter ink: the branch whose worktree the agent works in.
  4. 4
    A messageWhat the person sent, on the group surface at the column's right; the agent's text runs at the left without a box.
  5. 5
    Tool rowsOne dense row per tool the agent used: its icon, its name and what it acts on in monospace, how long it took at the right; an edit adds the lines it added and removed. A running tool shows the in-progress icon in its place.
  6. 6
    Run referenceEach verify the agent starts: the Run's status icon, Verify, the Run ID, its status word, and what failed or waits for a person, then Open in Verify. Its status changes in place as the Run goes on.
  7. 7
    A failed toolThe reason, in red, on a line of its own under the tool's name; the turn goes on.
  8. 8
    ComposerA field one step below the panel, as wide as the conversation, growing with what is typed up to eight lines; its last row holds the provider and model selector at the left and Send at the right.
  9. 9
    SendThe one primary control of Chat: an icon button with its accessible name. While a turn runs it is Stop.
  10. 10
    Bottom barChat is on; Review still counts the Human reviews waiting for a person.
  1. 11
    Model menuRaised, opening upward from the selector and as wide as needed for a model's name and ID; a provider's name as a group label, its models as rows, the session's model on the selection. A click on a row takes it for the next turn.
  2. 12
    Connect providerAfter a group line, the last row: it opens Settings on its Providers tab. A provider that lists no models says why in red under its name.
  3. 13
    StopWhile a turn runs, Send's place holds Stop, a quiet icon button with the stop icon; the composer stays usable.
  4. 14
    In progressA tool still running shows the in-progress icon in its row's icon place and no time yet.
  1. 15
    No session yetThe Main area's one line says how to start one, in the quieter ink, as an empty place may.
  2. 16
    New chatA dialog of the app's kind with one field, the branch, which suggests the live branches as the search does, the branch selected in Verify first; Create starts the session there.

Rules

D-CHAT-1
Chat's Pane is one --surface-panel column: its head, a 40px row closed below by --line-group, holds Chats semibold at --text-small and, at its right, New, a small button with the add icon; under it the sessions, newest first, each a two-line row: the title in --ink at --text-body, cut with an ellipsis, and under it project · branch · age in --ink-2 at --text-small with tabular figures. The shown session's row has --selection and, while the keyboard is in the list, the 2px inset bar (D-SYS-7). a card per session; a session's last message under its title; a preview on hover.
D-CHAT-2
The Main area's header row (D-MAIN-1) names the session: its title as the view's name, then as its headline {project} / {branch} in --ink-2. Under it, closed above by --line-panel, the conversation stands on --surface-app, in a column of --measure centred in the area, newest at the bottom: it scrolls on its own and follows its newest line unless scrolled up. With no session shown, one --ink-2 line says how to start one (D-MAIN-3). the conversation stretched to the window's width; the session's ID as its title; avatars; timestamps on every message.
D-CHAT-3
A person's message is a block at the column's right, at most 80% of its width, on --surface-group with --radius-chip and no border, its text as typed. The agent's turn runs at the column's left without a box: its text at --text-body in --ink, and its last row, once it ends, the provider, the model and how long the turn took in --ink-2 at --text-small, led by the Chat icon; a stopped turn ends Stopped there, a failed one with why in --red. a bubble around the agent's text; the model named on every row; a sentence saying the turn went well.
D-CHAT-4
A tool the agent used is one --row-dense row on --surface-input with --radius-control: an icon of what it does in --ink-2 (the file icon to read, the folder icon to list, find or search, the edit icon to edit or write, the command icon to run a command, the Verify icon for Verify's tools), the tool's name in --mono --ink, what it acts on in --mono --ink-2 cut with an ellipsis (an edit adds +N −M, the lines it added and removed, in the same ink), and at the right how long it took in tabular figures. While it runs the in-progress icon in --running takes its icon's place and no time shows; a failed tool's reason follows on a line of its own under its name, in --red. Rows of one turn stack with 4px between them. a tool's output dumped into the conversation; a tick for a tool that went well; added and removed lines in GREEN and RED; a row per tool that folds open on hover.
D-CHAT-5
A Run reference is a row of 32px inside a --line-group border of --radius-control on --surface-panel: the Run's status icon in its colour, Verify, the Run ID in --mono, its status word bold in its colour (in progress, then its verdict, changing in place), what needs attention (each eval that failed or waits for a person, its status's icon and its name, cut with an ellipsis), and at the right Open in Verify, a text reference in --accent led by the open icon (D-REF-1). A Run that cannot be read says so in --red in place of its status. a coloured card or stripe for the Run's status; a Run reference without its Run ID; Open in Verify as a primary button.
D-CHAT-6
The composer stands under the conversation in the same --measure column, 12px above the area's bottom: a field on --surface-input with a --line-input border of --radius-chip, its text growing to eight lines, its placeholder Ask the agent to work on this branch… in --ink-3, then one row: the provider and model selector at the left, a quiet button of --control height with the provider's name in --ink-2, the model's in --ink and the unfold icon; and at the right Send, the one primary control, an icon button of --control with the send icon and its accessible name, which becomes Stop, a quiet icon button with the stop icon, while a turn runs. Keyboard focus is the field's 2px ring (D-SYS-7). the selector above the field or at its right; Send at the left; a Send button with a key cap; a disabled composer while a turn runs.
D-CHAT-7
The model menu opens upward from the selector, its bottom left at the selector's top left, raised (D-SYS-2): --surface-raised, a --line-panel border, --radius-control, the faint shadow. Each provider Settings lists is a group: its name as a group label (D-SYS-5), then its models as --row rows, each the model's name in --ink and its ID in --mono --ink-2 at the right; the session's model has --selection and semibold; a provider that lists none says why in --red at --text-small in its group. After a --line-group line, Connect provider with the add icon ends the menu. Taller than the room above the selector, the menu scrolls inside itself. a menu that opens downward over the composer; a menu that opens on hover; a model chosen by colour alone; a line explaining the harness.
