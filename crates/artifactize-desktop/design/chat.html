Chat: a session with Artifactize's own agent

Chat mode, laid out as agent chat applications are: its Pane holds New chat, the search and the sessions, grouped by project, one line each, a session's state where its age is; its Main area holds the chosen session's conversation in a column of the reading measure, kept where it is read, and the composer under it. The agent's text reads as Markdown, what it did as quiet one-line rows, routine ones folded into a summary, each with its details a click away, and each verify it starts as a Run reference that opens the Run in Verify. The model selector sits at the composer's bottom left, its menu opening upward; Send, or Stop while a turn runs, at its bottom right.

Rules

D-CHAT-1
Chat's Pane is one --surface-panel column, as an agent chat application's sidebar: its head, a row closed below by --line-group, holds New chat, a button across the Pane's width with the add icon; under it the search field, a --control field on --surface-input across the Pane's width, its placeholder Search chats in --ink-2; under it the sessions, grouped by project and branch, the groups and their sessions newest first, each group under {project} / {branch} as a group label (D-SYS-5), each session one --row-pane row, chosen by a click anywhere on it (J-PANE-ROWS): its title in --ink, cut with an ellipsis, and at the right its age in --ink-2 with tabular figures, or in the age's place its state as one word at --text-small, at the row's weight (J-CHAT): running in --running, then until the session is shown how a turn ended while it was not, finished in --ink, failed in --red, stopped in --ink-2 or interrupted in --ink-2. The pointer over a session gives its whole row --surface-group, and the keyboard on it a 2px --focus ring inside the whole row. While the search matches nothing, one --ink-2 line, No chat matches., takes the list's place. A session's title is --ink-2; the shown session is the darker --selection row with its title in --ink (D-SYS-13), and hover never lights it. a second line under each session; the branch on a session's line or the model in the list; a heading over the Pane; a card per session; a preview on hover; a dot, badge or count beside a session; a Human review's waiting shown in the Pane (the bar's Review count says it).
D-CHAT-2
The Main area's header row (D-MAIN-1) names the session: its title as the view's name, then its branch in --mono --ink-2 as its headline, then the view switch (D-CHAT-10) and, in Graph view, the Current's scan state and Refresh (D-CUR-1). Under it, closed above by --line-panel, the conversation stands on --surface-app in a column of --measure centred in the area, newest at the bottom, scrolling on its own and following its newest line only while it is read at its end (J-CHAT-READ): scrolled up, what is read stays put as the turn streams and Run references change in place. Away from the end, Scroll to end stands centred at the conversation's bottom, 8px above the composer, raised (D-SYS-2): --surface-raised, a --line-panel border, --radius-control, the faint shadow, --control high, the unfold icon and its name at --text-small; at the end it is not drawn. With no session shown, one --ink-2 line says how to start one (D-MAIN-3). the conversation stretched to the window's width; avatars; a time on every message; the model or the provider in the header; a jump to the end while scrolled up; a count of new lines on Scroll to end.
D-CHAT-3
A person's message is a block at the column's right, at most 80% of its width, on --surface-group with --radius-chip and no border, its text exactly as typed, never read as Markdown. The agent's turn runs at the column's left at --text-body in --ink, without a box, a name or an icon before it, its text read as Markdown (J-CHAT-SEND): paragraphs; lists with their markers, indented 20px; links as the app's links (--accent, underlined), opening in the browser; inline code in --mono at --text-small on --surface-group with --radius-control; a code block on --surface-panel inside a --line-group border of --radius-control, its lines in --mono at --text-small, kept as written and scrolling sideways on their own, Copy a small quiet button at its top right; anything else, headings and HTML included, as its text. A stopped turn ends with Stopped in --ink-2, an interrupted one with Interrupted and why in --ink-2, a failed one with why in --red; a turn that ended well ends with its last words. a bubble around the agent's text; the model, the time or a token count under every turn; a sentence saying the turn went well; HTML from the answer rendered; images or embedded pages from the answer; colour themes for code.
D-CHAT-4
What the agent did is one quiet line per tool between its words, without a box or a background: an icon of what it does in --ink-3 (the file icon for a read, the folder icon for a listing or a search, the edit icon for an edit or a write, the command icon for a command, the Verify icon for Verify's other tools), then at --text-small in --ink-2 a verb (Read, Searched, Edited, Ran, …, in the present tense while it runs) and what it acted on in --mono, cut with an ellipsis; an edit adds the lines it added and removed, +N −M, in the same ink. While it runs, the in-progress icon in --running takes its icon's place. A tool that failed adds why in --red on the same line. A verify the agent started is not such a line but its Run reference (D-CHAT-5). Tools in a row that went well, with no words between them, are one summary line of the same anatomy: the fold icon in --ink-3 in the tool's icon's place, then what they did, counted, verbs joined by · (Read 3 files · searched 1 folder · edited 1 file); unfolded, the unfold icon takes its place and their lines follow under it, indented 22px. A running or failed tool is never in a summary, nor is a Run reference; a summary ends where one stands. A click on a tool's line unfolds its details under it, indented 22px, at --text-small in --ink-2: a key/value grid (Asked with, Exit code for a command), then the end of its output, as much as the library keeps, in --mono in --ink on --surface-input with --radius-control, its first line saying how many earlier lines were left out (… 38 earlier lines); a second click folds them. a box, a background or a border per tool; a tick for a tool that went well; a time per tool; added and removed lines in GREEN and RED; a tool's output shown before its line is clicked; a running or failed tool hidden in a summary; a terminal drawn in the conversation.
D-CHAT-5
A Run reference is one row of 32px inside a --line-group border of --radius-control on --surface-panel: the Run's status icon in its colour, the Run ID in --mono, its status word in its colour at the row's weight (in progress, then its verdict, changing in place), what needs attention in --ink-2 (the eval that failed or waits for a person, cut with an ellipsis), and at the right Open in Verify, a text reference in --ink-2 led by the open icon (D-REF-1). a coloured card or stripe for the Run's status; the word Verify repeated in the row; Open in Verify as a primary button.
D-CHAT-6
The composer stands under the conversation in the same --measure column, 12px above the area's bottom: one field on --surface-input with a border of --radius-chip a 1px --line-input edge, so even empty it reads as a field; its state shows: empty, the placeholder; holding a draft, its text; being typed in, the 1px --current-mark editing outline and the caret; keyboard focus, the 2px --focus ring outside it, apart from those (D-SYS-13); its text growing to eight lines, its placeholder Ask the agent to work on this branch… in --ink-2; under the text, inside the field, one row: the model selector at the left, a control of --control height inside a 1px --line-group border of --radius-control: the session's model in --ink, then, for a model with reasoning efforts, · {effort} in --ink-2, with the unfold icon; at the right Send, the one primary control, an icon button of --control with the send icon and its accessible name, the secondary face with a 1px --line-primary outline (D-SYS-13), which becomes Stop, a quiet icon button with the stop icon, while a turn runs; once Stop is clicked, Stopping… in --ink-2 at --text-small, led by the in-progress icon in --running, takes its place until the turn has stopped (J-CHAT-STOP). A message the library did not accept leaves one line above the field, inside the column: why in --red at --text-small, cut with an ellipsis, then Copy and Restore as small quiet buttons; Restore is drawn disabled while the field holds text (J-CHAT-SEND). Keyboard focus is the field's 2px ring (D-SYS-7). a borderless selector that reads as text; the selector above the field or at its right; the provider's name beside the model; Send at the left; a key cap; a disabled composer while a turn runs; Stop clickable again while stopping; a refused message put back over newer text; a refused message shown in the conversation as sent.
D-CHAT-7
The model menu opens upward from the selector, its bottom left at the selector's top left, raised (D-SYS-2): --surface-raised, a --line-panel border, --radius-control, the faint shadow. Its first row is the filter, a --control field on --surface-input, its placeholder Filter models, focused when the menu opens; typed in, the groups keep only the models that match, a provider with none left drops out, and with nothing left one --ink-2 line, No model matches., stands in their place. The menu closes on a click outside it and on Esc, which does nothing else (J-CHAT-MODEL). Each provider Settings lists is a group: its name as a group label, then its models as --row rows by name; the session's model is the darker --selection row, its name in --ink and the others' in --ink-2, at one weight; a model with reasoning efforts, as the library lists them, has them at its row's right as small text choices at --text-small in --ink-2, each effort in its own column, the same for every row: one column per effort any listed model offers, in the order the library lists them (low, medium, high, xhigh in the figure), a column left empty where a model lacks that effort, so an effort stands in one place down the menu, the session's effort on the session's model marked in --ink over a 2px --current-mark underline, at the others' weight; a click on an effort sets the model with it, a click on the model's name sets it with the session's effort when it has it, else with the model's default effort (J-CHAT-MODEL); a provider that is only not set up yet (no key, Antigravity not signed in; its Google account's models once it is, J-SET-ANTIGRAVITY) has no group: such providers are named together in one --ink-2 line at --text-small under Connect provider, Not set up: {providers}; a provider whose listing failed keeps its group, with why in --red at --text-small. Each effort is a cell of its column, 52px wide and as tall as the row, which the pointer lights whole on --surface-group. After a --line-group line, Connect provider with the add icon, then the providers not set up, end the menu. Taller than the room above the selector, the menu scrolls inside itself. a menu that opens downward over the composer or on hover; a model chosen by colour alone; IDs or a line explaining the harness in the menu; efforts in a second menu or a separate control; efforts shown for a model that has none; an effort that moves from row to row; a provider that is only not set up drawn in red.
D-CHAT-8
The New chat dialog is a dialog as the overlays draw one (D-OVL-1): its title, the Branch field, a choice on --surface-input inside --line-input, then Model, labelled as a form's field is, its value plain text, not a field: the provider's name, the model and, for a model with reasoning efforts, its effort, in --mono at --text-small; while the providers' models are being read it says Reading the providers' models… in --ink-2, and once they are read with none listed, No provider lists a model. Connect one in Settings. in --ink-2. Cancel and the primary Create end its last row, Create disabled until the model is known. Create closes the dialog and shows the new session: selected in the session list, its conversation empty, the composer focused (J-CHAT-NEW). Create offered before the model is known; the no-model sentence while the models are still being read; a model choice in the dialog (the composer's selector changes it).
D-CHAT-10
The view switch stands in the session's header row after its branch, 12px from it: Conversation and Graph as two text segments at --text-small in one group 22px high, of --radius-control, as a video editor's viewer-mode toggles are; inside a 1px --line-input edge with a divider between the two; since it swaps what the area shows, it is drawn as tabs are: the shown view's word --ink-bright over a 2px --current-mark underline, the other's --ink-off, on the same surface and at the same weight. A click shows that view; each session keeps its own (J-CHAT-GRAPH). Graph is the Current page's (D-CUR-10). the switch as tabs under the header or as icons alone; a coloured or bold shown segment; a third view.
D-CHAT-9
A session whose branch cannot be worked on now keeps its place in the Pane and its conversation; above its composer, one line in --ink-2 at --text-small says why, as the library says it, and the composer reads as off: its field and Send disabled, the whole box at reduced opacity. the session hidden or removed from the Pane; a red banner; a composer that takes text it cannot send.
