Detail: the inspector of the selected Artifact

A click on a node opens its Detail: a panel docked at the graph's right edge, meeting the graph at a line, with the graph still visible on its left. The selection drives it: the outlined node and the Detail's title name the same Artifact. Its tabs read as a path, the Artifact's own chip then one chip per eval; the first figure shows the Artifact's tab, the second an eval's Summary.

  1. 1
    SelectionThe opened node keeps its face and its strip; its own outline turns neutral-bright and 2px. It is the Artifact the Detail is titled with.
  2. 2
    Dock headerThe title (the whole name, wrapping), the star beside it, Close with its key cap. The 2px accent line at the top says the Detail has focus. The Detail starts under the graph's tool strip, which stays in reach.
  3. 3
    Chip pathThe Artifact's chip, a /, then one chip per eval: rounded chips of 22px, the chosen one filled; eval chips lead with the verdict's icon in its colour; many chips wrap.
  4. 4
    NeighboursThe Artifact between the Artifacts its evals use and those whose evals use it, drawn with the graph's nodes; a click on a neighbour opens it.
  5. 5
    Eval rowsDense rows of four columns: the verdict's icon, the name, the status words in the status colour, and time and tokens pinned to the panel's right edge in secondary ink with tabular figures.
  6. 6
    SectionsEach a header row: the fold icon, the section's name in ink, a short summary at the right; a click folds it to that row. An empty Tool log is one quiet line; Debug starts folded.
  1. 7
    View switchPlain text a level below the chips: the chosen view dark and bold, the other light; no chip, no fill.
  2. 8
    Status lineThe verdict's icon and word bold in the status colour; what it took and spent beside it in neutral secondary ink.
  3. 9
    Header → label → valueThree levels: a section's header row, then its facts as a key/value grid, keys right-aligned in secondary ink, values in tabular figures. A folded section keeps its row and its summary (Fingerprint here).
  1. 10
    Run DetailThe same dock, titled with the Run's ID; it has no chip path, since a Run has no evals of its own to tab through.
  2. 11
    SectionsOutcome, Waits for (its pending evals folded behind a quiet line), What and Provenance as key/value grids under section header rows; Technical is folded last, its row's summary naming its keys.
  1. 12
    A Current's chipsEach eval chip leads with what verify would do, in the plan's colour, the same as its node's: reused, would run, waiting, blocked, or still being checked.
  2. 13
    A Current's SummaryThe plan line replaces the verdict line: what would happen and why, in the plan's colour and secondary ink; then the result it would reuse, when there is one; no log.

Rules

D-INS-1
The Detail is the inspector: a panel docked at the Main area's right edge over the graph's canvas, from the canvas's top to the area's bottom, below the area's header row, below what a Run shows under it (its comment and attention) and below the graph's tool strip (all stay readable and usable), with no gap at its top, right or bottom, never beside the Pane. It is a --surface-panel panel meeting the graph at lines, --line-panel at its left and the tool strip's --line-group at its top, with square corners and no shadow; its left edge takes a drag and its width is remembered; part of the graph stays visible on its left. a floating card (a gap, a radius or a shadow around the Detail); the Detail pushing the graph aside or moving its camera.
D-INS-2
The Detail's header is the panel header row (D-MAIN-1): the title at --text-small semibold, wrapping to show a long name whole, naming what the tab shows with the Artifact's name whole in it (Artifact {name} on the Artifact's tab, {Artifact}/{eval} · {eval title} on every eval's tab, a Human eval's included, Run {id} on a Run's), the star beside it on an Artifact's tabs, Close with its Esc key cap at the end; the chip path below keeps the Artifact's name on every tab; focus shows as the 2px --accent top line. a cut title; a second close control.
D-INS-3
The chip row sits under the header, closed below by --line-group: chips of --row-dense height, --radius-chip, --text-small, a 1px --line-group border and --ink-2 text; the chosen chip is filled with --surface-group in --ink semibold; the Artifact's chip leads with the Artifact mark and cuts only its name; each eval chip leads with its verdict's icon in the status colour (on a Current, with what verify would do, D-INS-8); the / stays with the Artifact's chip and the rest wrap. pills in the accent; a chip without its icon.
D-INS-4
The Artifact's tab is sections, top to bottom, each under its header row (D-INS-9): Neighbours (the drawing, at least 72px, non-interactive camera; its summary how many Artifacts it uses and how many use it), Evals (the eval rows; its summary how many are GREEN of how many, the node's tally), Tools, Tool log, Path, then Debug, folded. An eval row is --row-dense in four columns: the verdict's icon in the status colour, the name, the status words in the status colour, and the last column (time, tokens) pinned to the panel's right edge in --ink-2 tabular; on a Current the words say would run, would be reused or checking. A Tool log without runs is one --ink-2 line, not an empty box. accent-coloured section headings; times trailing the status words instead of aligning at the right; an empty log box; a tool button on the graph's nodes.
D-INS-5
An eval's tab: the view switch as plain text (the chosen view --ink bold, the other --ink-2, no radius, no fill), the status line (the verdict's icon and word bold in the status colour; what it took and spent, or saved, in --ink-2), the instruction, then Result, Execution options, Execution metadata and Fingerprint each a section under its header row (D-INS-9), facts in a key/value grid with keys right-aligned in --ink-2 and values in tabular figures; a list as numbered items; Debug folded last. A log replaces the body below the switch in --mono at --text-small, wrapping and scrolling in its own box. a JSON dump as the Summary; costs in the status colour.
D-INS-7
A click on the Run's headline opens the Run's Detail in the same dock (D-INS-1, D-INS-2), titled with the Run's ID and without a chip row: its sections Outcome, Waits for, What and Provenance each under a section header row (D-INS-9), their facts as key/value grids (keys right-aligned in --ink-2, values in tabular figures), the pending evals of Waits for folded behind a --ink-2 line led by the fold icon (n pending evals) that a click unfolds, and Technical folded last, a section whose header row names its keys as its summary. tabs on a Run's Detail; the Run's graph redrawn inside it.
D-INS-8
Over a Current the Detail keeps its anatomy and changes what the marks say: each eval chip and each eval row lead with what verify would do with that eval (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, or still being checked) as that plan's icon and the matching words in its one colour, the legend's (would run --running, reused --reused, waiting --queued, blocked --blocked, checking --checking); an eval's tab shows, in place of the verdict line, the plan (what would happen, bold in its colour) with its reason beside it in --ink-2, then the instruction, the result it would reuse as a section under its header row when there is one (the plan line already says why an eval would run), then the execution options and Debug; there is no log and no Human review. A node still being checked recolours its chips and rows in place as the scan learns. a Run's verdict words on a Current; a log switch that has nothing to show.
D-INS-9
The inspector reads in three levels: a section's header row, a fact's label, its value. A section's header row is --row high, opened above by --line-group: the fold icon in --ink-2 (the unfold icon while the section is unfolded), the section's name at --text-small semibold in --ink, in sentence case without letter-spacing, and at the row's right end a short summary in --ink-2 with tabular figures, cut with an ellipsis (a count, a state, a value that can stand for the folded content). The whole row takes the click that folds or unfolds its section, and keyboard focus as a control (D-SYS-7); folded, a section is its row alone. Debug, and a Run's Technical, start folded; a section keeps the state a person gave it. A Human review's Tools, Tool log and Result, and the sections of Settings, stand under the same row without the fold icon: they do not fold. 11px uppercase labels as section headings; a section that cannot be told folded from empty; a box around a section; a fold control apart from the row.
D-INS-6
Selection and inspector agree: the node with the --outline-selected outline, every copy of a shared part included, is the Artifact the Detail is titled with, or, when that Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, the minimized whole that hides it while the Detail still names the Artifact; opening a neighbour or another node moves both; closing the Detail removes the outline. The Find field's choice opens the Detail and moves only the camera. a Detail left open for a node no longer highlighted.
