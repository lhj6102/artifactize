Detail: the inspector of the selected Artifact

A click on a node opens its Detail: a panel docked at the graph's right edge, meeting the graph at a line, with the graph still visible on its left. The selection drives it: the outlined node and the Detail's title name the same Artifact. The Artifact's tab lists its evals, and a click on one opens that eval's tab, where chips read as a path back; the first figure shows the Artifact's tab, the second an eval's Summary.

  1. 1
    SelectionThe opened node keeps its face and its strip; its own outline turns neutral-bright and 2px. It is the Artifact the Detail is titled with.
  2. 2
    Dock headerThe title (the whole name, wrapping), the star beside it, Close. The 2px accent line at the top says the Detail has focus. The Detail starts under the graph's tool strip, which stays in reach. The outlined node and this title are the two places that name the Artifact.
  3. 4
    NeighboursFolded until asked for: the graph beside the Detail already shows them. Unfolded, it draws the Artifact between the Artifacts its evals use and those whose evals use it, with the graph's nodes; a click on a neighbour opens it.
  4. 5
    Eval rowsThe tab's navigation: a click on a row opens that eval's tab. Dense rows of four columns: the verdict's icon, the name, the status words in the status colour, and time and tokens pinned to the panel's right edge in secondary ink with tabular figures.
  5. 6
    SectionsEach a header row: the fold icon and the section's name in ink, nothing else; a click folds it to that row, and what it holds shows once it is unfolded. The Tool log joins them with the first run of a tool.
  1. 3
    Chip pathOnly on an eval's tab: the Artifact's chip, which leads back to its tab, a /, then one chip per eval: rounded chips of 22px, the chosen one filled; eval chips lead with the verdict's icon in its colour; many chips wrap.
  2. 7
    View switchPlain text a level below the chips: the chosen view dark and bold, the other light; no chip, no fill.
  3. 8
    Status lineThe verdict's icon and word bold in the status colour; what it took and spent and how it ended beside it in neutral secondary ink, said here once.
  4. 9
    Header → label → valueThree levels: a section's header row, then its facts as a key/value grid, keys right-aligned in secondary ink, values in tabular figures. The Result is unfolded; the sections read now and then are folded to their names, and their values show once unfolded.
  1. 10
    Run DetailThe same dock, titled with the Run's ID; it has no chip path, since a Run has no evals of its own to tab through. It holds what the header row leaves out: the tally with its words, the duration and the comment in full.
  2. 11
    SectionsOutcome and Waits for (what the Run waits for, then its pending evals) are unfolded, their facts as key/value grids; What, Provenance and, last, Technical are folded to their names.
  1. 12
    A Current's chipsEach eval chip leads with what verify would do, in the plan's colour, the same as its node's: reused, would run, waiting, blocked, or still being checked.
  2. 13
    A Current's SummaryThe plan line replaces the verdict line: what would happen and why, in the plan's colour and secondary ink; then the result it would reuse, when there is one; no log.

Rules

D-INS-1
The Detail is the inspector: a panel docked at the Main area's right edge over the graph's canvas, from the canvas's top to the area's bottom, below the area's header row and below the graph's tool strip (both stay readable and usable), with no gap at its top, right or bottom, never beside the Pane. It is a --surface-panel panel meeting the graph at lines, --line-panel at its left and the tool strip's --line-group at its top, with square corners and no shadow; its left edge takes a drag and its width is remembered; part of the graph stays visible on its left. a floating card (a gap, a radius or a shadow around the Detail); the Detail pushing the graph aside or moving its camera.
D-INS-2
The Detail's header is the panel header row (D-MAIN-1): the title at --text-small semibold, wrapping to show a long name whole, naming what the tab shows with the Artifact's name whole in it (Artifact {name} on the Artifact's tab, {Artifact}/{eval} · {eval title} on every eval's tab, a Human eval's included, the Run's ID on a Run's), the star beside it on an Artifact's tabs, Close at the end (Esc does the same); focus shows as the 2px --accent top line. The outlined node and this title are the two places that name the opened Artifact (D-INS-6). a cut title; a second close control; a key cap on Close.
D-INS-3
The chip row shows only on an eval's tab, under the header, closed below by --line-group; the Artifact's own tab has none, since its Evals rows lead to every eval (D-INS-4). It reads as a path: the Artifact's chip (the Artifact mark and its name, only the name cut), which leads back to the Artifact's tab, a /, then one chip per eval. Chips are of --row-dense height, --radius-chip, --text-small, with a 1px --line-group border and --ink-2 text; the chosen chip is filled with --surface-group in --ink semibold; each eval chip leads with its verdict's icon in the status colour (on a Current, with what verify would do, D-INS-8); the / stays with the Artifact's chip and the rest wrap. pills in the accent; a chip without its icon; a chip row above the Artifact's own list of evals.
D-INS-4
The Artifact's tab is sections, top to bottom, each under its header row (D-INS-9): Evals (the eval rows, each of which opens that eval's tab), Neighbours (folded until asked for; unfolded, the drawing, at least 72px, with a camera that does not move), Tools, Tool log (only once a tool has run: an Artifact without runs has no Tool log section), Path, then Debug, folded. An eval row is --row-dense in four columns: the verdict's icon in the status colour, the name, the status words in the status colour, and the last column (time, tokens) pinned to the panel's right edge in --ink-2 tabular; on a Current the words say would run, would be reused or checking. accent-coloured section headings; times trailing the status words instead of aligning at the right; a section, a sentence or an empty box for a Tool log without runs; the Artifact's name or its tally repeated in the tab's body; a tool button on the graph's nodes.
D-INS-5
An eval's tab: the view switch as plain text (the chosen view --ink bold, the other --ink-2, no radius, no fill), the status line (the verdict's icon and word bold in the status colour; what it took and spent, or saved, and how it ended, in --ink-2), the instruction, then Result, unfolded, a list as numbered items; then Execution options, Execution metadata and Fingerprint, each folded to its name (D-INS-9), their facts, once unfolded, in a key/value grid with keys right-aligned in --ink-2 and values in tabular figures; Debug folded last. What the status line says (the duration, the exit) is not said again below it. A log replaces the body below the switch in --mono at --text-small, wrapping and scrolling in its own box. a JSON dump as the Summary; costs in the status colour; the exit code or the duration repeated in the Result or the metadata.
D-INS-7
A click on the Run's headline opens the Run's Detail in the same dock (D-INS-1, D-INS-2), titled with the Run's ID and without a chip row. It holds what the header row leaves out. Outcome, unfolded: the status, its icon and word said once, the evals that failed by name, the tally (each status's icon in its colour, its count and its word), what the Run took, and its comment in full. Waits for, unfolded: what the Run waits for, then its pending evals in --ink-2. Then What, Provenance and, last, Technical, folded to their names. Facts are key/value grids (keys right-aligned in --ink-2, values in tabular figures), and every section folds and unfolds by its header row (D-INS-9). tabs on a Run's Detail; the Run's graph redrawn inside it; a sentence that says the status again (NOT SATISFIED at Run end beside RED).
D-INS-8
Over a Current the Detail keeps its anatomy and changes what the marks say: each eval chip and each eval row lead with what verify would do with that eval (reused, would run, waiting, blocked, or still being checked) as that plan's icon and the matching words in its one colour, the node's (would run --running, reused --reused, waiting --queued, blocked --blocked, checking --checking); an eval's tab shows, in place of the verdict line, the plan (what would happen, bold in its colour) with its reason beside it in --ink-2, then the instruction, the result it would reuse as a section under its header row when there is one (the plan line already says why an eval would run), then the execution options and Debug, both folded; there is no log and no Human review. A node still being checked recolours its chips and rows in place as the scan learns. a Run's verdict words on a Current; a log switch that has nothing to show.
D-INS-9
The inspector reads in three levels: a section's header row, a fact's label, its value. A section's header row is --row high, opened above by --line-group: the fold icon in --ink-2 (the unfold icon while the section is unfolded), the section's name at --text-small semibold in --ink, in sentence case without letter-spacing, and nothing else: no count, value or list of what it holds stands at the row's end, folded or unfolded; what a section holds shows when it is unfolded. The whole row takes the click that folds or unfolds its section, and keyboard focus as a control (D-SYS-7); folded, a section is its row alone. A section starts unfolded when it is what its tab is opened for (an Artifact's Evals, Tools and Path, an eval's Result, a Run's Outcome and Waits for) and folded when it is read now and then (Neighbours, Execution options, Execution metadata, Fingerprint, What, Provenance, Debug and a Run's Technical); a section keeps the state a person gave it. A section with nothing to hold is not shown (an Artifact's Tool log before its first run). A Human review's Tools, Tool log and Result, and the sections of Settings, stand under the same row without the fold icon: they do not fold. 11px uppercase labels as section headings; a section that cannot be told folded from empty; a summary, a count or a table of contents at a row's end; a box around a section; a fold control apart from the row.
D-INS-6
Selection and inspector agree: the node with the --outline-selected outline, every copy of a shared part included, is the Artifact the Detail is titled with, or, when that Artifact is hidden inside a minimized whole, the minimized whole that hides it while the Detail still names the Artifact; opening a neighbour or another node moves both; closing the Detail removes the outline. The Find field's choice opens the Detail and moves only the camera. a Detail left open for a node no longer highlighted.
