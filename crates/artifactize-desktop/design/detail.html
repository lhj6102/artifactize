Detail: the inspector of the selected Artifact

A click on a node opens its Detail, docked over the graph's right side with a gap. The selection drives it: the outlined node and the Detail's title name the same Artifact. Its tabs read as a path, the Artifact's own chip then one chip per eval; the first figure shows the Artifact's tab, the second an eval's Summary.

  1. 1
    SelectionThe opened node keeps its face and border and gains a neutral 2px outline. It is the Artifact the Detail is titled with.
  2. 2
    Dock headerThe title (the whole name, wrapping), the star beside it, Close with its key cap. The 2px accent line at the top says the Detail has focus.
  3. 3
    Chip path[mark] cli / ✓ lint ✗ tests ✓ matches-docs: rounded chips of 22px, the chosen one filled; eval chips lead with the verdict glyph in its colour; many chips wrap.
  4. 4
    NeighboursThe Artifact between the Artifacts its evals use and those whose evals use it, drawn with the graph's nodes; a click on a neighbour opens it.
  5. 5
    Eval rowsDense rows: glyph, name, status words in the status colour, the right column (time, tokens) in secondary ink with tabular figures.
  6. 6
    SectionsTools, Tool log and Path under 11px uppercase labels in secondary ink, each section opened above by a group line; the Tool log in an input-surface box; the rest folds under Debug.
  1. 7

Rules

D-INS-1
The Detail docks over the graph toward its right edge with an 8–12px gap on three sides, never beside the Pane: a --surface-panel panel with a 1px --line-panel border, --radius-panel corners and a faint shadow (--scrim, 2px offset, 8px blur); its left edge takes a drag and its width is remembered; part of the graph stays visible on its left. a heavy shadow or large radius; the Detail pushing the graph aside.
D-INS-2
The Detail's header is the panel header row (D-MAIN-1): the title at --text-small semibold, wrapping to show a long name whole, the star beside it, Close with its Esc key cap at the end; focus shows as the 2px --accent top line. a cut title; a second close control.
D-INS-3
The chip row sits under the header, closed below by --line-group: chips of --row-dense height, --radius-chip, --text-small, a 1px --line-group border and --ink-2 text; the chosen chip is filled with --surface-group in --ink semibold; the Artifact's chip leads with the Artifact mark and cuts only its name; each eval chip leads with its verdict glyph in the status colour; the / stays with the Artifact's chip and the rest wrap. pills in the accent; a chip without its glyph.
D-INS-4
The Artifact's tab, top to bottom: the neighbours drawing (at least 72px, non-interactive camera), the eval rows (--row-dense: glyph in the status colour, name, status words in the status colour, the right column in --ink-2 tabular), then Tools, Tool log and Path under section labels, then Debug folded. Section labels are 11px uppercase --ink-2; each section after the first is opened above by --line-group. accent-coloured section headings; a tool button on the graph's nodes.
D-INS-5
An eval's tab: the view switch as plain text (the chosen view --ink bold, the other --ink-2, no radius, no fill), the status line (glyph and word bold in the status colour; what it took and spent, or saved, in --ink-2), the instruction, then Result, Execution options, Execution metadata and Fingerprint each under a section label, facts in a key/value grid with keys right-aligned in --ink-2 and values in tabular figures; a list as numbered items; Debug folded last. A log replaces the body below the switch in --mono at --text-small, wrapping and scrolling in its own box. a JSON dump as the Summary; costs in the status colour.
D-INS-6
Selection and inspector agree: the node with the --outline-selected outline, every copy of a shared part included, is the Artifact the Detail is titled with; opening a neighbour or another node moves both; closing the Detail removes the outline. The Find field's choice opens the Detail and moves only the camera. a Detail left open for a node no longer highlighted.
