Settings: a modal over the screen

Ctrl+, or the bar's Settings button opens Settings over the screen; Esc, Close or the button again closes it with the screen beneath as it was. Three tabs: Providers (a list beside the chosen provider's setup), Remote (the shared review store) and Limits (the sign-off idle grace).

Rules

D-SET-1
Settings is a --surface-raised dialog with a 1px --line-panel border and --radius-chip corners over a --scrim, at most 900px wide and the window's height less 80px, 12–16px padding; the bar stays above the scrim. Its header row holds the title at 15px, the tabs, and Close with its Esc key cap at the end; one --ink-2 line under it says these are the CLI's settings. a full-screen page; a 20px radius.
D-SET-2
The tabs are text: --ink-2, the active one --ink semibold with a 2px --accent line below; they are a tab list (←, → move, the active one focusable). pill tabs; the active tab filled.
D-SET-3
Providers is two columns: a 180px list of --row rows (built-in providers in their order, custom providers by name, + Custom provider last), the current one on --selection and semibold; the chosen provider's setup at the right in sections separated by --line-group, each a 14px title, a --ink-2 description, facts as a key/value grid (keys in --ink-2) and actions as 24px buttons in one row; a key field is a --surface-input field and never shows a value. cards per provider; a password field pre-filled.
D-SET-4
Remote and Limits use the same section anatomy: fields on --surface-input in a labelled grid, one row of buttons, and the status or result as one line in its tone (--green done, --yellow warning, --red failed). a status in a coloured box.
