Settings: a modal over the screen

Ctrl+, or the bar's Settings button opens Settings over the screen; Esc, Close or the button again closes it with the screen beneath as it was. Three tabs: Providers (a list beside the chosen provider's setup), Remote (the shared review store) and Limits (the sign-off idle grace).

    DialogA raised surface with a panel line over a scrim; the bar stays above the scrim so its Settings button closes the dialog again.
  2. 2
    Text tabsProviders, Remote, Limits as text with a 2px accent line under the active one; ← → move between them.
  3. 3

Rules

D-SET-1
Settings is a --surface-raised dialog with a 1px --line-panel border and --radius-chip corners over a --scrim, at most 900px wide and the window's height less 80px, 12–16px padding; the bar stays above the scrim. Its header row holds the title at --text-title semibold, the tabs, and Close at the end (Esc does the same); the tab's content follows right under it. a full-screen page; a 20px radius; a line that explains the dialog; a key cap on Close.
D-SET-2
The tabs are text: --ink-2, the active one --ink semibold with a 2px --accent line below; they are a tab list (←, → move, the active one focusable). pill tabs; the active tab filled.
D-SET-3
Providers is two columns: a 180px list of --row rows (built-in providers in their order, custom providers by name, + Custom provider last), the current one on --selection and semibold; the chosen provider's setup at the right in sections separated by --line-group, each under a section header row of the inspector's anatomy without the fold icon (D-INS-9: --row high, the name at --text-small semibold), then, only where the name cannot say what the section is, one --ink-2 line of description, facts as a key/value grid (keys in --ink-2) and actions as 24px buttons in one row; a key field is a --surface-input field and never shows a value. cards per provider; a password field pre-filled; a description that repeats a section's name or its button.
D-SET-4
Remote and Limits use the same section anatomy: fields on --surface-input in a labelled grid, one row of buttons, and the status or result as one line in its tone (--green done, --yellow warning, --red failed). a status in a coloured box.
D-SET-5
A provider's setup states its account as a fact of the grid: Codex signed in or out; Claude Agent as Claude Code reports it (claude not found, not signed in, signed in with its login method and plan, or the error it failed with) with Check again beside it; an API-key provider's key as set from the environment (naming the variable), saved here, or not set, never its value. Its actions are one row of 24px buttons (Sign in with Codex / Sign out of Codex, Sign in with Claude Code / Sign out of Claude Code, Save key / Remove saved key) and a key field is a --surface-input field that empties once saved. While a sign-in is in progress the fact reads so in --yellow and Cancel sign-in is the one button under it. What finishes a sign-in by hand, for when the browser does not return, stays under a folded row, Finish by hand (the fold icon and its name, as a section's header row, D-INS-9): unfolded, it shows the URL the sign-in printed as selectable --mono text on --surface-input, one field for what the browser could not return (for Codex the redirect URL, for Claude Code the code its page gives) and Finish sign-in beside the field. Before Claude Agent is signed in, one --ink-2 line says that the login stays Claude Code's own, which artifactize never reads, copies or stores; once signed in, the facts stand alone. The error a sign-in or sign-out ends with is a fact of the same grid in --red, as a status Claude Code fails to report is. Models is its own section: List models, then one --row row per model, its name in --ink and its ID beside it in --mono and --ink-2. a modal per sign-in; the sign-in's URL and paste field shown before they are asked for; a table of IDs beside names; a saved key echoed back.
D-SET-6
+ Custom provider opens an empty form in the setup column, and a listed custom provider opens its filled form: Name, Kind (a choice between an alias of a built-in provider and an endpoint), for an alias the built-in provider it uses (a choice of Codex, Claude Agent, OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini; the alias then runs with that provider's own sign-in or key, set up under that provider), and for an endpoint API (OpenAI Responses, OpenAI Chat Completions, Anthropic Messages or Gemini), Base URL and an optional Key, as a labelled grid of --surface-input fields; Save is the primary button, Remove provider sits beside it; a saved provider is listed by its name under the built-in ones. a wizard; the key shown after saving.
D-SET-7
Remote is one section of at most 640px that shows only what its state can use. Signed out: Store URL, Share level (a choice) and Token fields in a labelled grid, Sign in to the store as the primary button and Check status beside it; an error is one line in --red. Signed in: the store's URL and share level as facts of a key/value grid, with the status as its last fact, one line in its tone (signed in, the reviewer and what was pushed today); then one row of buttons, Check status, Push (dry run), Push results and, at the right, Sign out of the store; a push's result or an error replaces that status line in its own tone. The fields, the token and Sign in to the store come back once signed out. the token shown; Sign in or a token field beside a store that is signed in; a status that repeats a fact above it; a push without a dry run.
D-SET-8
Limits is one section: one --ink-2 line that says what the sign-off idle grace is, a seconds field of --control height holding the grace in force (120, the default, unless changed) with Save, the primary button, beside it; once saved, the field holds the new grace, which applies to the Runs started afterwards; only a failure to save is said, in one --red line. a slider; a unit other than seconds; the grace in force shown beside the field that holds it.
