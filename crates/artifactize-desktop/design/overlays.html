Overlays: the palette, Help, dialogs and the find field

What opens over the screen: the command palette (the bar's brand, or Ctrl+K), Help (the palette's Keyboard shortcuts), Open a request by ID, the Leave dialog (quit while claims are held or a Chat turn runs), the Update dialog (from the release notice in Settings' header), and the graph's find field (the tool strip's Find), which floats over the graph's top left rather than over the screen, and, on a Current, the New Artifact, Rename and Delete dialogs.

Rules

D-OVL-1
A dialog stays inside the work area, between the window's top and the bottom bar: taller than the room, its content scrolls under its title and above its actions, which stay in view, a 4px --line-input thumb showing there is more. It is a --surface-raised panel with a 1px --line-panel border, --radius-chip corners, 16px padding and a shadow no stronger than 8px offset, 24px blur in --scrim, over a --scrim overlay; its title is --text-title semibold; its actions are right-aligned 24px buttons, at most one primary. A dialog's buttons carry no key cap (D-SYS-12), but for the Leave dialog's two, which may show their keys as hints at the end of their labels. a 12px radius; a borderless card floating on a blur; a key cap on any other dialog's button.
D-OVL-2
The command palette, which a click on the bar's brand opens (D-BAR-1), is 640px wide near the top: its input a field as Find's is, --surface-input inside a 1px --line-input edge with the 1px --current-mark editing outline while typed in, group headings at --text-label uppercase in --ink-2, its list bounded above the bottom bar and scrolling on its own, a 4px --line-input thumb at its right showing how much is out of view; items of --row with the label left in --ink-2 and its key cap, if it has one, right, the selected item the darker --selection row with its label in --ink; typing filters the rows: only the commands whose names contain the typed text stay, in their order, each group heading kept only while one of its commands does (typing rel here would leave Release the claim alone, under Human review), and the selected row among them on --selection. It lists every command that is enabled now, each with its key cap where the app offers a key (J-PALETTE): the app's first, under App, then those of what is shown, each under its group (Graph, Human review, and Settings while Settings is open); the Graph group holds a graph's commands that can run now, Minimize or expand the whole among them while a whole's Detail is open (J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE); while a graph's Detail shows a Human review on a Human eval's tab, that review's commands are listed too, under the review's group, as they are for a review in the Main area. icons per command; the selection shown by colour alone; a command that opens the palette itself.
D-OVL-3
A key cap is the one kbd style: --mono at --text-label, --ink-2, a 1px --line-input border, --radius-control, --surface-group. It shows where keys are looked up, in the palette and in Help, and on the Leave dialog's two buttons (D-OVL-1); nowhere else, other dialogs' buttons included (D-SYS-12). On the primary button, the secondary face with its --line-primary outline, the key cap is drawn as on any button. a second kbd style; a key cap on any control other than the Leave dialog's two buttons.
D-OVL-4
The find field is opened by the tool strip's Find button (D-GRAPH-4, J-GRAPH-FIND). It floats over the canvas's top left (8px in, under the strip), 380px wide on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: a --mono input, empty and focused when it opens, then result rows of --row-dense in --mono at --text-small led by the verdict's icon in its colour (on a Current, what verify would do), each showing the whole name, wrapping within the field's width rather than cut with an ellipsis, the chosen row the darker --selection row with its name in --ink, the others in --ink-2, a hovered row on --surface-group. It has four states. Nothing typed, with starred Artifacts on the graph: those Artifacts as rows, as if found, in the graph's order. Text typed with matches: the Artifacts and evals whose names contain the text, ignoring case, as rows in the graph's order, the list no taller than the canvas below the field and scrolling inside the field, so all 50 listed rows and the line after them stay reachable, an eval as {Artifact} / {eval}, and past the first 50 one --ink-2 line under them saying how many more match. Nothing typed and nothing starred: no rows, one --ink-2 line saying how to star one. Text typed and nothing matching: no rows, one --ink-2 line saying that none does. Typing moves nothing on the graph. No row shows as chosen when the list appears. A click on a row closes the field, opens and focuses the Detail on that Artifact's tab or that eval's tab, and moves the camera so the Artifact sits in the middle of the graph left of the Detail, zoomed in only as far as its name needs to be readable (J-GRAPH-FIND). A part of several wholes is listed once, and the camera goes to its copy in the first of its wholes. An Artifact hidden in a minimized whole, or an eval of one, is listed as any other; chosen, the Detail opens on that Artifact or eval, and the camera centres the minimized whole that hides it, which takes the selection's outline (J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE), the whole staying minimized. A click outside closes the field and does nothing else: the click neither acts on the graph nor opens, switches or closes the Detail, and the graph, its camera and the Detail stay as they were. a modal find; a camera that moves while typing or choosing a row; an empty list with no line under it; the starred list and the how-to-star line together; a no-match line that reads like the how-to-star line.
D-OVL-6
Open a request by ID (from the palette) is a dialog of D-OVL-1 at most 480px wide: its title, one --surface-input text field taking the request ID with req-… in --ink-2 as its placeholder, and the actions Cancel and the primary Open, without key caps; what opens is shown in Review as a reference opens it (D-REV-8): a waiting Human review, or one this reviewer holds, actionable (D-REV-1), its pill REVIEW (yours) for the one held; a Human review another reviewer holds as that review, read-only, TAKEN with the claimant named (D-REV-1), as any other reference opens it; a submitted Human review as that review with its completed result; any other request as the Request view, read-only (D-REV-6). a list of recent IDs; a second field.
D-OVL-5
Help lists the keys the app has for what has focus (a Pane, a Chat, a graph with its Detail, a review, Settings) as a key cap / action grid under an 11px group label, each key cap beside what it does, and Close as its one action (J-HELP); which keys there are is the app's to choose, and a Run's Help lists none that only a Current has. For a graph it adds the graph's marks under their own label, each status's icon in its colour beside its word (a Run's: GREEN, running or waiting for a Human (on a node, one mark for both), waiting, RED, ERROR, blocked by upstream, and the Human icon that the Run's headline and a Human eval's chip and row use for a sign-off that waits; a Current's: reused, would run, waiting, blocked, checking), the one place that spells them out; the Leave dialog says which claims are held and offers Stay and Release and quit (D-OVL-1); the Update dialog, opened from the release notice in Settings' header (D-SET-1), shows the running and the new version as two numbers, then the one way to update that fits how the app was installed: a command to run, as selectable --mono text on --surface-input with a --line-input border and no link; a file to download, as a --accent link that opens in the browser, followed by the command that installs it by hand when there is one; or, when the way is unknown, the release page as a link; Close closes it. It never downloads or installs anything itself. a Current's editing keys in a Run's Help; a Help page outside the app; a legend on a working screen; an update that starts on its own; a link and a command for the same thing; an Install or Update button.
D-OVL-7
The edit dialogs of a Current are dialogs of D-OVL-1 at most 400px wide, opened by the tool strip's New Artifact (D-CUR-3) and the Definition's Rename and Delete (D-INS-10). New Artifact: two fields under their names, Name (focused) and Path in --mono, then Cancel and the primary Create. A Create that succeeds writes .artifactize/{name}.artf with that path and closes the dialog; the new node is drawn on the Current, in free space when the top level is placed by hand, its Detail opens on its Artifact tab, and the camera moves to it as to a found Artifact, centred on the graph left of the Detail (D-OVL-4). Rename: titled Rename {name}, one field holding the name, then Cancel and the primary Rename. Delete: titled Delete {name}?, one sentence naming the file it removes, then Cancel and the primary Delete; a Delete that succeeds removes that file, with its place, its parts' places and its comment, and closes both the dialog and the Detail, the node going from the graph (J-NODE-DELETE). Their buttons carry no key cap; Delete is done only by its button. A refusal shows in --red at --text-small under the field it concerns, or under Delete's sentence, and the dialog stays with what was typed: a name already used or not valid, or a path not valid, says why; Delete refused while other Artifacts name it says that the file was not removed and which Artifacts name it; and a Rename or Delete whose file (the Artifact's own, or a whole's or a referrer's that the edit would change) changed on disk since the Current read it names that file, {file} changed on disk; nothing written, without a line or column, and the Current is scanned again. a folder or kind chooser in New Artifact; a sentence that explains what Rename changes; Delete in red; a refusal that closes the dialog.
