Overlays: the palette, Help, dialogs and the find field

What opens over the screen and closes with Esc: the command palette (Ctrl+K), Help (?), Open a request by ID (Ctrl+O), the Leave dialog (quit while claims are held), the Update dialog (from the bar's release notice), and the graph's find field (Ctrl+F), which floats over the graph's top left rather than over the screen.

  1. 1
    PaletteA 640px raised dialog near the top: an input closed below by a group line, groups with 11px headings, 24px items with the key cap at the right; the selected item on the selection background.
  2. 2
    Key capsEvery command that has a key shows it as a key cap, the same cap the bar and the hints use.
  3. 3
    HelpThe keys of what has focus, as a key-cap / action grid under group labels.
  4. 4
    Leave dialogSays what is held; Stay (Esc) and the primary Release and quit (u).
  5. 5
    Find fieldOpened by Ctrl+F or the tool strip's Find button, over the graph's top left: a monospace input and 22px result rows with the verdict's icon in its colour; the chosen row on the selection background.
  6. 7
    Open by IDOne text field for the request ID (placeholder req-…) and two actions, Cancel and the primary Open; Enter opens, Esc cancels.
  7. 6
    Update dialogThe running and the new version, how to update the way the app was installed, the command as selectable monospace in an input-surface box, a link to the release page.

Rules

D-OVL-1
A dialog is a --surface-raised panel with a 1px --line-panel border, --radius-chip corners, 16px padding and a shadow no stronger than 8px offset, 24px blur in --scrim, over a --scrim overlay; its title is --text-title semibold; its actions are right-aligned 24px buttons, at most one primary, each with its key cap when a key does the same. a 12px radius; a borderless card floating on a blur.
D-OVL-2
The command palette is 640px wide near the top: an input of 36px closed below by --line-group, group headings at --text-label uppercase in --ink-2, items of --row with the label left and the key cap right, the selected item on --selection; Enter runs, Esc closes. icons per command; the selection shown by colour alone.
D-OVL-3
A key cap is the one kbd style: --mono at --text-label, --ink-2, a 1px --line-input border, --radius-control, --surface-group; on the primary button it is transparent with --ink-on-accent. a second kbd style; keys written as plain text where a cap is used elsewhere.
D-OVL-4
The find field, opened by Ctrl+F or the tool strip's Find button (D-GRAPH-4), floats over the canvas's top left (8px in, under the strip), 380px wide on --surface-raised with a --line-panel border: a --mono input, then result rows of --row-dense in --mono at --text-small led by the verdict's icon in its colour, the chosen row on --selection with a 2px inset bar, a hovered row on --surface-group, and, only when there is something to say, a --ink-2 note under the rows: how many more match beyond the first 50 listed, that none does, or how to star one when no favorite is starred. a modal find; results that move the graph.
D-OVL-6
Open a request by ID (Ctrl+O or the palette) is a dialog of D-OVL-1 at most 480px wide: its title, one --surface-input text field taking the request ID with req-… in --ink-3 as its placeholder, and the actions Cancel (Esc) and the primary Open (Enter); what opens is the Review screen, actionable for a waiting Human review and read-only otherwise. a list of recent IDs; a second field.
D-OVL-5
Help lists the keys of what has focus as sections (Pane, Main area, Settings) with a key cap / action grid; the Leave dialog says which claims are held and offers Stay (Esc) and Release and quit (u); the Update dialog shows the running and the new version, the way to update as a selectable --mono command on --surface-input or a link, and never installs anything itself. a Help page outside the app; an update that starts on its own.
