Review: a Human review in the Main area

A waiting review opened from the Pane fills the Main area, its eval and Artifact named in the Main area's header row. It has one order, the instruction, the Tools, their Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements: with room, the Tool log is the view at the left and the rest a column at the right, where the Detail docks; in one column, on a Human eval's chip inside a Run's Detail or in a narrow Main area, it reads top to bottom. A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

  1. 1
    Title and stateThe eval's title at 14px and the stage pill (CLAIM while unclaimed or taken by another reviewer, REVIEW (yours) once claimed here, completed); under it the request's facts in secondary ink, naming the claimant, only the state's icon and word in its status colour.
  2. 2
    ToolsGrouped by the Artifact that declares them, the reviewed one first, each a small button led by the run icon, with its description in secondary ink.
  3. 3
    Tool log, the viewWhat the reviewer looks at gets the room: monospace on the input surface from the header row to the bottom, the run's start line and end line quiet, printed lines in ink, stderr in red behind a red rule; it follows its newest line unless scrolled up.
  4. 4
    ResultGREEN and RED as a segmented choice outlined in the chosen verdict's colour; then the form from the eval's schema: label semibold, * on a required field, Optional in grey inside an empty optional one.
  5. 5
    FooterRight under the Result, inside the column: busy state or the last notice at the left; Release at the right; Submit is the one primary button.
  6. 6
    Two columnsThe column takes the place and the width of the Detail, so what is acted on is always at the right; the two meet at a panel line.
  1. 7
    Tool log, in the columnWith one column the log is an input-surface box in its place in the order, scrolling on its own.
  2. 8
    Same order, same actionsInstruction, Tools, Tool log, Result, then the footer: nothing moves but the log.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review has one order, the instruction, the Tools, the Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements by the room it has. One column, on a Human eval's tab in the Detail and in a Main area narrower than twice the Detail's kept width: it reads top to bottom in one scrolling column, left-aligned and no wider than --measure, the Tool log a box between the Tools and the Result. Two columns, in a Main area at least twice the Detail's kept width: the Tool log is the view, filling the left from under the header row to the area's bottom (D-REV-3), and the rest is a column docked at the right, where the Detail docks and of the Detail's kept width, in the same order (title row, facts, instruction, Tools, Result, footer), scrolling on its own; the two meet at a --line-panel line, and the column's width follows the Detail's, with no drag of its own. In both, the title row holds the title at --text-title and the stage pill of --radius-chip on --surface-group, or --accent-soft in --accent while it is yours; the request's facts follow in --ink-2 with only the state's icon and word (DEFERRED) in the status colour. Opened from the Pane, the eval and its Artifact are named in the Main area's header row, as its headline in --ink-2 (D-MAIN-1). Tools, Tool log and Result each stand under a section header row without the fold icon (D-INS-9). Its states read without colour: Unclaimed shows the primary Claim to review button in the Result; Taken (another reviewer holds the claim) keeps the CLAIM pill, names the claimant in the facts line and says Claimed by {reviewer}. Read-only until released. in --ink-2 in the Result, with no Claim button and the tools disabled; Claimed shows REVIEW (yours), the GREEN / RED choice and the enabled tools; Submitted shows the completed result in place of the form. a column stretched to the window's width; the form in the wide left and the log squeezed at the right; a third column; a whole line in a status colour; the Result above the Tools; a Taken review that looks claimable.
D-REV-2
Tools are grouped by the Artifact that declares them, the reviewed one first, each group led by the Artifact mark and name semibold; each tool is a small (22px) button, the run icon then the operation's name, with its description in --ink-2 at --text-small, an error under it in --red. Before a claim the buttons are disabled and a line says so. tool buttons in the accent; a tool without its description.
D-REV-3
The Tool log is monospace on --surface-input, --mono at --text-small: in one column a box with a --line-input border; in two columns the view itself, the left of the Main area without a box, under its header row (Tool log and, as its summary, how many runs it holds and whether it follows its newest line), with --pad around its lines. Either way: each run's ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2 (a failing end in --red), printed lines in --ink, stderr in --red behind a 2px --red rule at its left, so it is told apart without colour too; lines wrap within its width; it scrolls on its own and follows its newest line unless scrolled up; its text selects. a log that grows the review; a marker typed into a printed line (the line keeps its text as printed).
D-REV-4
The Result begins with the GREEN / RED choice as two --control buttons with --line-group borders; the chosen one takes its verdict's colour as text and border, semibold, its face unchanged; each carries the key cap of the key that chooses it (g and r before a form is open, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+R while editing), as Claim to review carries c. The form's fields follow: the label semibold with * in --red when required, inputs on --surface-input with --line-input, Optional in --ink-3 inside an empty optional field; lists add with Add led by the add icon and remove with the remove icon; a failing path shows in --red under the form and the entries stay. a filled green or red button; a JSON editor.
D-REV-5
Submit is the one primary (--accent) button, right-aligned under the form, at the column's right edge, with its Ctrl+S key cap; the footer follows the Result inside the column, never pinned to the window's bottom, and holds the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel, or the last notice (--green done, --red failed), and Release with its u key cap at the right. two primary buttons; a notice as a toast; Submit or Release a window's width away from the form.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows its sections read-only in the same column, its title suffixed read-only in the Main area header. a disabled form standing in for read-only.
