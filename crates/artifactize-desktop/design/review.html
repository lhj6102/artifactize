Review: the reviews and a review's conversation

Review mode: its Pane holds the filter, a plain-text query (type:human waiting to start), and the reviews it matches, the open one selected, which names its eval; a review chosen there, or opened by a reference, fills the Main area under the stage header: the way back at its left when the review was opened from Verify, Find and Open in Verify at its right. A review opened by a reference shows whether or not the filter lists it, the filter staying as it was. A review is a conversation, drawn with Chat's parts, the same in Review and on a Human eval's tab in a Run's Detail: the request asks at the top, unboxed as the agent's text is (its title and the claim's state, the instruction, the tools, Debug folded), and grows downward with each tool's run, folded to one line, and each point; the reviewer's issues stack under it as bubbles, as Chat's messages are; at the bottom the issues box, where Chat's composer stands, holds only the issue field and Add issue, and under it, apart, the decision bar holds the claim at its left and the GREEN / RED choice and Submit at its right. Before a claim the issues box gives way to one quiet line and the decision bar holds Claim to review. A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review is a conversation drawn with Chat's parts (D-CHAT-2, D-CHAT-3, D-CHAT-4, D-CHAT-6), the same in Review and on a Human eval's tab in the Detail: the review's request asks at the top and the reviewer answers under it, with no second panel and no split. In Review's Main area it stands under the stage header (D-REV-8) on --surface-app in a column of --measure centred in the area, scrolling on its own and following its newest line only while it is read at its end, as Chat's does (D-CHAT-2); in the Detail it takes the Detail's width on the Detail's surface, under its tabs, without section header rows. Top to bottom: the head line (D-REV-11); the request, at the column's left at --text-body in --ink without a box, a bubble, a name or an icon, as the agent's text is (D-CHAT-3): the instruction as written, the tools (D-REV-2), and Debug, one folded line of Chat's tool-line anatomy (D-CHAT-4), the fold icon in --ink-3 and its name in --ink-2 at --text-small, which a click (also t) unfolds into the request's ID, its mode, its claimant and how long it has waited as Chat's details grid; then, newest last as they happen, each tool's run (D-REV-3) and each point (D-REV-9), 6px apart; then, pushed to the conversation's bottom, the issues (D-REV-10); last, the issues box (D-REV-5) and under it the decision bar (D-REV-12). Nothing else stands in the conversation: no pill, no count, no section header and no second title. two columns or an Evaluation panel beside the conversation; the request in a bubble or a card; the issues interleaved with the runs; a section header over the tools, the runs, the points or the issues; the request's ID or mode outside Debug; the conversation stretched to the window's width; a card or a box around the whole review.
D-REV-11
The head line is the conversation's first line, --row high: the eval's title in --ink-bright at --text-lead cut with an ellipsis, led by the eval ({Artifact}/{eval} · ) only while no row of the Pane names the review (opened by a reference the filter does not list); in the Detail the Detail's title says it, and the head line holds only Open in Review (D-REF-1) at its right. The claim's state is said in the Pane's row (D-REV-7) and the decision bar (D-REV-12), not here. the claim's state or Release claim in the head line; the claim's state as a block, a banner or a pill in the conversation; the title said twice.
D-REV-2
The tools stand in the request under the instruction: one row of small (22px) buttons that wraps, each the run icon then its operation's name, its description its tooltip and accessible description, grouped by the Artifact that declares them or whose type provides them, the reviewed one first, a group led by the Artifact's name in --ink-2 at --text-small only when more than one Artifact has tools. Before a claim the buttons are drawn disabled, their tooltip Claim to run the tools (for a taken review, who holds it); while any of the review's work runs (a tool, a claim, a release or a submission) they are disabled too, so one tool runs at a time. A tool's failure is said in words on its run's line (D-REV-3); no other notice says it. tool buttons in the accent; descriptions as lines under the buttons; a count beside the tools; a heading over the only group; a tool's failure as a toast or a banner.
D-REV-3
Each run of a review's tool is one line of the conversation, added after the request's last line when it starts, with the anatomy of a Chat tool's line (D-CHAT-4): --row-dense high, no box or background, the fold icon in --ink-3 (the in-progress icon in --running while it runs), then at --text-small in --ink-2 its verb (Ran for an output tool, Opened for a launch or viewer tool; Running, Opening while it runs), the operation in --mono and · {Artifact}; then its last stderr line if it printed one, in --ink behind a 2px --red rule, cut with an ellipsis; at the line's right, with tabular figures, its end and how many lines it printed (exit 0 · 8 lines; exit N in --red unless N is 0; done; launched; error: … in --red when the tool could not run), or, while it runs, its newest line in --mono, its seconds and Cancel, a small quiet button. A click on the line (also Enter on it) unfolds its lines under it, indented 22px, the fold icon becoming the unfold icon, and another folds them; a run that fails unfolds nothing by itself: its red end shows folded. Unfolded, its lines stand in a box on --surface-input inside --line-input of --radius-control, --mono at --text-small, as tall as its lines up to half the conversation's height, then scrolling on its own and following its newest line unless scrolled up: its ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2, the end line one of four: ■ exit N (its ■ in --red unless N is 0), ■ done, ■ launched once a file is handed to its desktop application or an Artifact Viewer opens, after which that application's lines keep arriving under the same run until it exits, or ■ error: … in --red; printed lines in --ink, stderr in --ink behind a 2px --red rule at its left, a warning: or error: prefix it prints in --red; lines wrap within its width and select. A submission clears the runs: their lines go. one Tool log box for every run; a run's lines open by default; a log box taller than its lines while they are few, outweighing the instruction; a tick for a run that ended well; a run in a summary line with others; a Tool log header.
D-REV-5
The issues box stands where Chat's composer does and as its field is drawn (D-CHAT-6): in the conversation's column, one field on --surface-input inside a 1px --line-input edge of --radius-chip, showing its states as Chat's composer does (the placeholder, a draft's text, the editing outline and the caret, the keyboard's focus ring, D-SYS-13). It holds only the issue field, its text growing to eight lines, its placeholder Enter adds the issue in --ink-2, and at its right, aligned to its bottom, Add issue, a secondary button, drawn disabled while the field is empty. Nothing of the verdict or the claim stands in it. Before a claim it is not drawn: in its place one line in --ink-2 at --text-small, Claim it to write issues and a verdict. A review taken by another reviewer, a request that is not a waiting Human review (D-REV-6) and a submitted review have no issues box and no line in its place. the verdict choice, Submit or the claim inside the issues box; Add issue as the primary button; a disabled field standing in for a review that cannot be written; Add issue that adds an empty issue.
D-REV-12
The decision bar stands under the issues box, 8px apart, in the same column: a bar of its own, at least 40px high, on --surface-panel inside a 1px --line-group edge of --radius-chip, padded 8px at its sides. Two groups: at its left the claim, at its right the decision; too narrow for both, the decision drops under the claim on a second line, still at the right. The claim: where the claim stands in --ink-2 at --text-small (Waiting, Yours), then, before a claim, Claim to review, the primary button, and once claimed Release claim, a small quiet button; while a claim or a release runs, its seconds and Cancel stand after it, or its failure in --red; while any other work of the review runs, Release claim stays in place, unusable (its text in --ink-3). The decision: GREEN and RED as two words at --text-small, 16px apart, --control high, drawn as tabs are (D-SYS-13): each led by a 7px dot in its verdict's colour, the chosen word --ink-bright over a 2px --current-mark underline, the other --ink-off, stepping to --ink under the pointer, both at weight 400, with no edge or face, neither marked until one is chosen; then, at the bar's right end, Submit, the one primary button once claimed (the secondary face with a 1px --line-primary outline, D-SYS-13), naming the chosen verdict (Submit GREEN, Submit RED), Submit while none is. Submit is drawn disabled, with why just left of it in --ink-2 at --text-small, while no verdict is chosen (Choose GREEN or RED), while the issue field holds text not yet added (Add the typed issue first), and while the library says the chosen verdict cannot be submitted as it stands, in its words (RED needs an issue, GREEN takes no issues); it is disabled too while a claim or a release runs, and stays usable while a tool runs (J-HUMAN-GREEN). While a submission runs, its seconds and Cancel stand in Submit's place; a refused submission says what failed in --red where the why stands, the draft kept. Before a claim GREEN, RED (in --ink-3) and Submit are drawn unavailable. A review taken by another reviewer, and a request that is not a waiting Human review (D-REV-6), keep the bar with one line in it in --ink-2 at --text-small, Taken by {claimant} or why it is read-only, and nothing else. Submitted, the bar goes with the issues box, and the result as submitted stands as the reviewer's last bubble (D-REV-10), a key/value grid of its verdict in its colour and its issues, numbered, or none, read-only. the decision bar inside the issues box or merged with it; the verdict as two bordered buttons or a filled green or red button; a verdict marked by colour or weight alone; Submit at the left; two primary buttons at once; Submit enabled with nothing chosen; a typed issue dropped by Submit; a word or a toast saying it was submitted.
D-REV-10
The reviewer's issues stand at the conversation's bottom, above the issues box, oldest first, 8px apart, each drawn as a person's message is in Chat (D-CHAT-3): a block at the column's right, at most 80% of its width, on --surface-group with --radius-chip and no border, its text exactly as typed, never read as Markdown. At each one's left, outside the block, its two tools as icons alone in --ink-3, --ink under the pointer, each named by its accessible name and native tooltip: Edit (the edit icon) and Remove (the remove icon). Edit turns the block into a field in its place, as wide as the issues may be, on --surface-input with the 1px --current-mark editing outline and the caret, holding its text; its tools are not drawn while it is edited. An issue carries no verdict mark. a ✓ or ✕ or a verdict colour on an issue; the issues in a list box or numbered; an issue edited in a dialog; Edit and Remove inside the bubble or behind a menu; an issue rendered as Markdown.
D-REV-7
Review's Pane is one --surface-panel column: at its top the filter field, as Verify's Search is drawn (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group, D-PANE-1), holding its query as plain typed text; its suggestions drop under it as the Search's do. Its starting query is type:human waiting. Under it, the reviews it matches, grouped by project under the project's name as a group label (D-SYS-5), newest first, each one --row-pane row, chosen by a click anywhere on it (J-PANE-ROWS): the request's status icon in its colour (a waiting Human review the human icon in --human), the eval as {Artifact}/{eval} in --ink cut with an ellipsis, and at the right in --ink-2 with tabular figures how long it waits, led by {claimant} · once claimed. A review's eval is --ink-2; the open review's row is the darker --selection row with its eval in --ink (D-SYS-13); the pointer over a row gives it --surface-group alone, and the keyboard on it the 2px --focus ring inside the whole row. With none matching, one --ink-2 line says so. When the search matches more reviews than the library returns at once, More reviews ends the list as Older runs ends a timeline (D-PANE-3): a Pane row of its own, --row-pane high, its words in --ink-2 at --text-small, lit on --surface-group under the pointer and ringed by the keyboard's focus, which brings the next reviews in below the last, the open review and the list's scroll kept. a pager or page numbers; matching reviews left out with no way to reach them; a second line under each review; a count heading over the list; a waiting list in another mode's Pane; the filter's terms drawn as chips or blocks; a card per review; a dash where there is no claimant.
D-REV-8
Review's Main area starts with the stage header (D-MAIN-1), the same row as Chat's and Verify's: 32px on --surface-panel, closed below by --line-panel, controls only. At its left, only for a review opened by following Open in Review from Verify, the way back, a tool led by the back icon (D-REF-1): Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}, the Run's ID in --mono, by luminance alone, cut with an ellipsis at 320px. At its right the tools: Find in the conversation, the find icon alone, which turns into its field in its place and marks what it finds in the conversation as Chat's Find does (D-CHAT-11); then, after a group separator, Open in Verify, a stage tool of the open icon and its words (D-REF-1). The review's eval is named by the Pane's selected row (D-REV-7) and its title by the head line (D-REV-11). A review opened by a reference shows the same whether or not the filter lists it, and nothing says which (J-REVIEW-REF). The draft has no mark of its own: what is written is the issues and the verdict as they stand, the same in Review, the Detail and the pet overlay (J-HUMAN-DRAFT). the way back as a chip or a bordered button; the eval, its title or the claim's state in the stage header; Open in Verify as a primary button; a line, a banner or a dialog saying a review is outside the filter; the filter changed to list the review; a saved or draft word beside the issues box.
D-REV-9
The Points are not drawn until Point has added one (J-HUMAN-POINTS). Each point is one line of the conversation, added after its last line as it is pointed, with no header of its own: one --row row at --text-small: the point icon in --ink-3, the pointed text in --mono --ink cut with an ellipsis, where it was pointed ({Artifact} · {viewer}) and when (HH:MM) in --ink-2 with tabular figures, Insert, a small quiet button, and at the row's right the remove icon in --ink-2, which removes the point from the draft. Insert puts the text at the issue field's caret, else at its end, and leaves the point listed. A point just added stands on --accent-soft until the next one, as Point's only answer (D-VIEW-2). a Points header or count; a point added as an issue by itself; points in the submitted result; a toast for a point; a point drawn as a bubble.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a Human review shows read-only in the same conversation: its title suffixed read-only in the head line, in --ink-bright at --text-lead, then its sections as the request's text, its facts in a key/value grid in --ink-2 labels over --ink values, its Debug (a Run's Technical) a folded line that a click unfolds (D-INS-9); the Pane's list and filter unchanged by it and none of its rows selected; the decision bar says why it is read-only (D-REV-12). a disabled issues box or form standing in for read-only.
