Review: a Human review in the Main area

A waiting review opened from the Pane fills the Main area, its eval and Artifact named once, in the Main area's header row. It has one order, the instruction, the Tools, their Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements: with room, two panels that meet at a panel line, the Tool log's view at the left and the review's column at the right, where the Detail docks; in one column, on a Human eval's chip inside a Run's Detail or in a narrow Main area, it reads top to bottom. A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

  1. 1
    Head rowThe column's head row holds the stage pill alone: CLAIM while unclaimed or taken by another reviewer (the claimant beside it), REVIEW (yours) once claimed here. Once submitted it holds no pill: the open, completed result alone says the submission went well. It stands in one band with the view's head row and is closed below by a group line. The eval's title is said once, in the header row above; the request's ID, its mode, its claimant and how long it has waited fold under Debug at the column's end.
  2. 2
    ToolsA section of the inspector: its header row opened above by a group line that runs the column's whole width, as Result's and Debug's are. Each tool a small button led by the run icon, with its description in secondary ink. The declaring Artifact's name heads a group only when more than one Artifact declares tools.
  3. 3
    Tool log, the viewWhat the reviewer looks at gets the room: a panel of its own, named by its head row, monospace on the input surface from that row to the bottom, the run's start line and end line quiet, printed lines in ink, stderr in red behind a red rule; it follows its newest line unless scrolled up.
  4. 4
    ResultThree levels read apart: the section under its group line, a field's label, and the field itself, one step darker inside the faintest outline. GREEN and RED as a segmented choice outlined in the chosen verdict's colour; then the form from the eval's schema: label semibold, * on a required field, Optional in grey inside an empty optional one.
  5. 5
    ActionsThe last row of the Result section, right under the form: Release claim at the left, Submit, the one primary button, at the right; between them the busy state or a failure. A tool that ended well is said by the log's end line alone.
  6. 6
    Two panelsThe column takes the place and the width of the Detail, so what is acted on is always at the right. The header row is closed below by a panel line, and the two panels meet at a panel line that runs from it to the bottom: the darker view on one side, the panel surface on the other.
  1. 7
    Tool log, in the columnWith one column the log is a section like the others, its lines in an input-surface box inside the faintest outline, scrolling on its own.
  2. 8
    Same order, same actionsThe head row with the stage pill, the instruction, then Tools, Tool log, Result with its row of actions and Debug, each under a group line: nothing moves but the log.
  1. 9
    ChoiceA field of the input surface showing the chosen option, the unfold icon at its right; an empty one reads Optional in grey, or a dash when required.
  2. 10
    True/falseIts two values side by side as round options, the chosen one filled; an optional field adds a third option, Optional in grey, chosen while it has no value.
  3. 11
    NumberA one-line field of a number's width; empty and optional, it says Optional in grey.
  4. 12
    List of textNumbered items, each a text field with the remove icon at its right, then a quiet Add with the add icon. Each item's remove icon removes it, the last one too, leaving Add alone; only a list of groups keeps one to type in.
  5. 13
    List of groupsEach group in a faint group line, headed by the list's name and its number with the remove icon at the right, its fields inside; Add after the last group adds an empty one. Removing the last group leaves one empty group, as here.
  6. 14
    Actions while editingRelease claim stays at the left while the form is edited, the draft kept when it is used; Submit names the chosen verdict.
  1. 15
    Claimed, no form yetThe action row is there from the claim on: Release claim at the left, usable before GREEN or RED opens a form; Submit joins it at the right once a form is open.
  2. 16
    While a tool runsRelease claim stays in place but cannot be used while any work runs; between the two buttons the running work's seconds and Cancel. Submit is there but disabled while any work runs, a tool as well as a submission (here check-links, still printing), and so are the tool buttons; Ctrl+S does nothing until the work ends or is cancelled. A launch tool is not running work here: it returns once the file is handed to its application, whose lines keep arriving in the log.
  3. 17
    Tool log, clearedSubmission clears the Tool log: its section, or in two columns its view, stays where it was, empty, ready for what a tool prints next.
  4. 18
    Completed resultIn place of the form, the result as submitted, each field by name, open and read-only; no actions row, and no pill or word above saying it was submitted: the result says so.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review has one order, the instruction, the Tools, the Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements by the room it has. One column, on a Human eval's tab in the Detail and in a Main area narrower than twice the Detail's kept width: it reads top to bottom in one scrolling column, left-aligned and no wider than --measure, the Tool log a box between the Tools and the Result. Two columns, in a Main area at least twice the Detail's kept width: two panels under the Main area's header row, which a --line-panel line closes. The Tool log is the view, the panel that fills the left from that line to the area's bottom (D-REV-3), and the rest is a column docked at the right, where the Detail docks and of the Detail's kept width, on --surface-panel, in the same order (stage pill, instruction, Tools, Result with its actions, Debug), scrolling on its own; the two meet at a --line-panel line that runs from the header row's line to the area's bottom, and the column's width follows the Detail's, with no drag of its own. Each panel begins with a head row of --row-head on --surface-panel, closed below by --line-group, the two in one band: the view's holds its name, Tool log, semibold at --text-small, and the column's holds the stage pill. In both, the eval's title is said once, above the review: for a review opened from the Pane in the Main area's header row, as its headline in --ink-2 ({Artifact}/{eval} · {eval title}, D-MAIN-1), and on a Human eval's tab in the Detail's title (D-INS-2). The review itself opens with its head row (in one column too, under the Detail's chips), which holds the stage pill alone, of --radius-chip on --surface-group, or --accent-soft in --accent while it is yours; while another reviewer holds the claim, the claimant's name follows the pill in --ink-2. Under the head row the review has the inspector's anatomy, so its boundaries read at three strengths (D-SYS-3): the instruction, then Tools, Tool log (in one column), Result and Debug, each a section whose header row is opened above by a --line-group line that runs from the panel's left edge to its right, the panel's edges being where those lines end; and inside a section its inputs, on --surface-input inside --line-input. The request's facts (its ID, its mode such as DEFERRED, its claimant and how long it has waited) fold under Debug, last in the column and folded until asked for (D-INS-9), which t or a click on its row shows and hides (J-HUMAN-LAYOUT), as t does a Detail's Debug (D-INS-9); ← leaves the review as Esc's last step does, closing the Detail it is in or, for a review opened from the Pane, focusing the Pane (shown first if hidden), only while no work runs and no form is open. Tools, Tool log and Result stand under section header rows without the fold icon and with nothing beside their names (D-INS-9). Its states read without colour: Unclaimed shows the primary Claim to review button in the Result; Taken (another reviewer holds the claim) keeps the CLAIM pill, names the claimant beside it and says Read-only until released. in --ink-2 in the Result, with no Claim button and the tools disabled; Claimed shows REVIEW (yours), the GREEN / RED choice, the enabled tools and the action row with Release claim (D-REV-5); Editing adds the form and Submit; Submitted shows no stage pill in the head row and the completed result in place of the form, open and read-only, without the action row, and the Tool log cleared: its section in one column, or its view in two, stays where it was, empty (D-REV-3). A review opened from the Pane is left, not closed, when a branch or an entry of a branch's timeline is chosen: the Main area shows that view, and opening the review again from the Waiting stack shows its draft as it was left, the form with what was typed and its Tool log unchanged (J-MAIN-REVIEW), and its claim and stage as they are now: a claim, a release or a submission made elsewhere while it was out of view shows when it is opened again, as it would have in view (J-LIVE). a review closed, or its draft or Tool log dropped, by choosing a branch; a claim or stage shown as it was left when it changed meanwhile; a column stretched to the window's width; the form in the wide left and the log squeezed at the right; a third column; a whole line in a status colour; the Result above the Tools; the eval's title repeated inside the review; a claim said twice; a Taken review that looks claimable; parts of a review told apart by headings and gaps alone; section lines that stop short of the panel's edges; a card, a box or a shadow around the column or a section.
D-REV-2
Tools are grouped by the Artifact that declares them, the reviewed one first; a group is led by the Artifact mark and name semibold only when more than one Artifact declares tools. Each tool is a small (22px) button, the run icon then the operation's name, with its description in --ink-2 at --text-small; a tool's failure is said in words in the Result's action row (D-REV-5), and its run ends red in the Tool log (■ exit N or ■ error: …, D-REV-3); no other notice says it. Before a claim the buttons are disabled and a line says so; while any of the review's work runs (a tool, a claim, a release or a submission), they are disabled too, so one tool runs at a time. tool buttons in the accent; a tool without its description; a count beside Tools; a heading over the only group.
D-REV-3
The Tool log is monospace on --surface-input, --mono at --text-small: in one column a box with a --line-input border; in two columns the view itself, a panel at the left of the Main area without a box: under its head row (Tool log, nothing beside it, D-REV-1) its lines stand on --surface-input from that row to the area's bottom, with --pad around them, one step darker than the column's panel beside it. Either way: each run's ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2, the end line one of four: ■ exit N (in --red unless N is 0), ■ done, ■ launched once a file is handed to its desktop application, after which that application's lines keep arriving under the same run until it exits, or ■ error: … in --red when the tool could not run, printed lines in --ink, stderr in --red behind a 2px --red rule at its left, so it is told apart without colour too; lines wrap within its width; it scrolls on its own and follows its newest line unless scrolled up; its text selects. A submission clears it: its lines go, and its box, or its view with its head row, stays in place, empty. a Tool log section or view removed by a submission; lines of an earlier submission kept after it; a log that grows the review; a count of runs or a word for following beside its name; a marker typed into a printed line (the line keeps its text as printed).
D-REV-4
The Result begins with the GREEN / RED choice as two --control buttons with --line-group borders; the chosen one takes its verdict's colour as text and border, semibold, its face unchanged. Their keys (g and r before a form is open, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+R while editing, c for Claim to review) are named by Help and the palette, not on the buttons (D-SYS-12). While editing, Ctrl+G chooses GREEN and Ctrl+R RED, each keeping its verdict when it is already chosen; there Ctrl+R does not refresh, and F5 still does. The form's fields follow, one under another, each its label semibold with * in --red when required, then its input on --surface-input with --line-input, Optional in --ink-3 inside an empty optional one; the schema's constants are filled in and not shown. By kind: text, a field of two lines that grows; a number, a one-line field of --control height about 120px wide; true/false, its two values side by side as round options, the chosen one filled in --accent, with a third option for no value when optional, labelled Optional in --ink-3 as an empty optional field is, and chosen while the field has no value; a choice, a one-line field showing the chosen option with the unfold icon at its right, empty as Optional (or a dash when required). A list of text is numbered items, each a text field with the remove icon at its right, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. A list of groups is its groups one under another, each inside a 1px --line-group line of --radius-control, headed in --ink-2 at --text-small by the list's name and the group's number with the remove icon at the right, its fields inside, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. Removing the last group of a list of groups leaves one empty group to type in; a list of text has no such floor: removing its last item leaves the list empty under its label, with Add to start it again. A failing path shows in --red under the form and the entries stay. a filled green or red button; a key cap on a verdict; a JSON editor; a constant shown, even read-only; a list of groups emptied to nothing; an empty item forced back into a list of text; a checkbox for true/false that cannot say no value.
D-REV-5
One row under the form, the last of the Result section, holds the review's actions, inside the column and never pinned to the window's bottom: Release claim, a quiet button named for what it releases, at the left; Submit, the one primary (--accent) button, naming its verdict (Submit GREEN), at the column's right edge; between them, while a tool or a submission runs, the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel, or what failed in --red, a failed tool's run also ending red in the Tool log (D-REV-3). What ended well is not said here: a tool's end line in the Tool log and the completed result of a submission say it. The row is there in Claimed and in Editing: Release claim from the claim on, before any form is open too, and Submit once a form is open; Release claim stays in its place while the form is edited, so entering a result never removes the way to release, and using it keeps the draft. While any work runs, Release claim stays in place but cannot be used (disabled, its text in --ink-3) and u does nothing. Submit, too, stays in place but cannot be used while any work runs, a tool's run or a submission (disabled, and Ctrl+S does nothing), and every tool button is disabled meanwhile (D-REV-2). Submitted, the row is gone with the form. Ctrl+S submits and u releases (typed in a field, u is text), keys named by Help and the palette (D-SYS-12). Release claim shown only once a form is open; Release claim usable while work runs; two primary buttons; a notice as a toast; a notice for what ended well; Submit or Release claim a window's width away from the form; a button that says Release alone, which reads as releasing what is reviewed; a key cap on Submit or Release claim.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows its sections read-only in the same column, its title suffixed read-only in the Main area header; with focus in it, t shows or hides its Debug (a Run's Technical) unless its log is shown, and Esc or ← returns focus to the Pane. a disabled form standing in for read-only.
