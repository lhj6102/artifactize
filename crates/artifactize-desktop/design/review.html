Review: the reviews and a review's evaluation space

Review mode: its Pane holds the filter, a plain-text query (type:human waiting to start), and the reviews it matches; a review chosen there, or opened by a reference, fills the Main area, its eval and Artifact named once, in the Main area's header row, with its way back to the Run and Artifact in Verify. A review opened by a reference that the filter does not list says so under that row, and the filter stays as it was. A review has one order, the instruction, the Tools, their Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements: with room, its evaluation space, two panels that meet at a panel line, the review at the left and the Evaluation at the right, where the Detail docks; in one column, on a Human eval's chip inside a Run's Detail or in a narrow Main area, it reads top to bottom. A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review has one order, the instruction, the Tools, the Tool log and the Result, and two arrangements by the room it has. One column, on a Human eval's tab in the Detail and in a Main area narrower than twice the Detail's kept width: it reads top to bottom in one scrolling column, left-aligned and no wider than --measure, the Tool log a box between the Tools and the Result. Two columns, Review's evaluation space in a Main area at least twice the Detail's kept width: two panels under the Main area's header row, which a --line-panel line closes. At the left the review: the instruction, the Tools and, under them, the Tool log, a box that takes the rest of the panel's height (D-REV-3), then Debug; at the right, docked where the Detail docks and of the Detail's kept width, the Evaluation: its head row, then the Result (Notes, the GREEN / RED choice and the form, D-REV-4), scrolling on its own, and the actions along its bottom (D-REV-5); both on --surface-panel, meeting at a --line-panel line that runs from the header row's line to the area's bottom, the Evaluation's width following the Detail's, with no drag of its own. The Evaluation's head row is --row-head, closed below by --line-group: Evaluation semibold at --text-small, then the stage pill. In both, the eval's title is said once, above the review: for a review shown in Review in the Main area's header row, after its way back (D-REV-8), its eval {Artifact}/{eval} as the view's name and its title as its headline in --ink-2 (D-MAIN-1), and on a Human eval's tab in the Detail's title (D-INS-2). In one column the review opens with its head row, under the Detail's chips, which holds the stage pill and, on a Human eval's tab while the review is not submitted, Open in Review after it (D-INS-12); in both, the stage pill is of --radius-chip on --surface-group, or --accent-soft in --accent while it is yours; while another reviewer holds the claim, the claimant's name follows the pill in --ink-2. Under the head rows the review has the inspector's anatomy, so its boundaries read at three strengths (D-SYS-3): the instruction, then Tools, Tool log (in one column), Result and Debug, each a section whose header row is opened above by a --line-group line that runs from the panel's left edge to its right, the panel's edges being where those lines end; and inside a section its inputs, on --surface-input inside --line-input. The request's facts (its ID, its mode such as DEFERRED, its claimant and how long it has waited) fold under Debug, last in the column and folded until asked for (D-INS-9), which a click on its row shows and hides (J-HUMAN-LAYOUT), as a Detail's Debug does (D-INS-9). Tools, Tool log and Result stand under section header rows without the fold icon and with nothing beside their names (D-INS-9). Its states read without colour: Unclaimed shows the primary Claim to review button in the Result; Taken (another reviewer holds the claim) reads TAKEN in the pill, the claimant named beside it, and says Read-only until released. in --ink-2 in the Result, with no Claim button and the tools disabled; Claimed shows REVIEW (yours), the GREEN / RED choice, the enabled tools and the action row with Release claim (D-REV-5); Editing adds the form and Submit; Submitted shows no stage pill in the head row and the completed result in place of the form, open and read-only, without the action row, and the Tool log cleared: in two columns its box stays where it was, empty; in one column its empty section is no longer shown (D-REV-3). A review is left, not closed, when another mode is shown or another review is chosen: showing it again shows its draft as it was left, the form with what was typed and its Tool log unchanged (J-MODES, J-HUMAN-DRAFT), and its claim and stage as they are now: a claim or a release made elsewhere while it was out of view shows when it is shown again, as it would have in view (J-LIVE); a review submitted elsewhere meanwhile shows its completed result. a review closed, or its draft or Tool log dropped, by switching a mode; a claim or stage shown as it was left when it changed meanwhile; a column stretched to the window's width; the Evaluation at the left or wider than the Detail; a third column; a whole line in a status colour; the Result above the Tools; the eval's title repeated inside the review; a claim said twice; a Taken review that looks claimable; parts of a review told apart by headings and gaps alone; section lines that stop short of the panel's edges; a card, a box or a shadow around the column or a section.
D-REV-2
Tools are grouped by the Artifact that declares them or whose type provides them, the reviewed one first; a group is led by the Artifact mark and name semibold only when more than one Artifact has tools. Each tool is a small (22px) button, the run icon then the operation's name, with its description in --ink-2 at --text-small; a tool's failure is said in words in the Result's action row (D-REV-5), and its run ends red in the Tool log (■ exit N or ■ error: …, D-REV-3); no other notice says it. Before a claim the buttons are disabled and a line says so; while any of the review's work runs (a tool, a claim, a release or a submission), they are disabled too, so one tool runs at a time. tool buttons in the accent; a tool without its description; a count beside Tools; a heading over the only group.
D-REV-3
The Tool log is monospace on --surface-input, --mono at --text-small: a box with a --line-input border: in one column between the Tools and the Result, in two columns under the Tools, taking the rest of the review's height to the area's bottom (D-REV-1). Either way: each run's ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2, the end line one of four: ■ exit N (in --red unless N is 0), ■ done, ■ launched once a file is handed to its desktop application or an Artifact Viewer opens, after which that application's lines keep arriving under the same run until it exits, or ■ error: … in --red when the tool could not run, printed lines in --ink, stderr in --red behind a 2px --red rule at its left, so it is told apart without colour too; lines wrap within its width; it scrolls on its own and follows its newest line unless scrolled up; its text selects. A submission clears it: its lines go; in two columns its box stays in place, empty, and in one column the empty section is no longer shown (J-DETAIL-SECTIONS). a Tool log box removed by a submission in two columns; lines of an earlier submission kept after it; a log that grows the review; a count of runs or a word for following beside its name; a marker typed into a printed line (the line keeps its text as printed).
D-REV-4
The Result begins, once claimed, with Notes: a text field of three lines that grows, labelled as a form's field is, the review's free text whatever the verdict; what Point appends arrives there, each on a line of its own, standing in --mono on --accent-soft until the Notes are next edited (D-VIEW-2). Then the GREEN / RED choice as two --control buttons with --line-group borders; the chosen one takes its verdict's colour as text and border, semibold, its face unchanged. The buttons show no key cap (D-SYS-12); while a form is open they switch the verdict, each verdict keeping its draft (J-HUMAN-RED). The form's fields follow, one under another, each its label semibold with * in --red when required, then its input on --surface-input with --line-input, Optional in --ink-3 inside an empty optional one; the schema's constants are filled in and not shown. By kind: text, a field of two lines that grows; a number, a one-line field of --control height about 120px wide; true/false, its two values side by side as round options, the chosen one filled in --accent, with a third option for no value when optional, labelled Optional in --ink-3 as an empty optional field is, and chosen while the field has no value; a choice, a one-line field showing the chosen option with the unfold icon at its right, empty as Optional (or a dash when required). A list of text is numbered items, each a text field with the remove icon at its right, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. A list of groups is its groups one under another, each inside a 1px --line-group line of --radius-control, headed in --ink-2 at --text-small by the list's name and the group's number with the remove icon at the right, its fields inside, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. Removing the last group of a list of groups leaves one empty group to type in; a list of text has no such floor: removing its last item leaves the list empty under its label, with Add to start it again. A failing path shows in --red under the form and the entries stay. a filled green or red button; a key cap on a verdict; a JSON editor; a constant shown, even read-only; a list of groups emptied to nothing; an empty item forced back into a list of text; a checkbox for true/false that cannot say no value.
D-REV-5
One row holds the review's actions: in one column right under the form, the last of the Result section; in two columns along the Evaluation's bottom, opened above by --line-group, under its scrolling content, so they stay in reach however long the form: Release claim, a quiet button named for what it releases, at the left; Submit, the one primary (--accent) button, naming its verdict (Submit GREEN), at the column's right edge; between them, while any work of the review runs (a claim, a release, a tool or a submission), the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel, or what failed in --red, a failed tool's run also ending red in the Tool log (D-REV-3). What ended well is not said here: a tool's end line in the Tool log and the completed result of a submission say it. While a claim is being made, before the review is Claimed, the row stands alone under the disabled Claim to review button and holds the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel (or, once it failed, what failed in --red), with neither Release claim nor Submit, which a review shows only once claimed. The row is there in Claimed and in Editing: Release claim from the claim on, before any form is open too, and Submit once a form is open; Release claim stays in its place while the form is edited, so entering a result never removes the way to release, and using it keeps the draft. While any work runs, Release claim stays in place but cannot be used (disabled, its text in --ink-3). Submit stays in place and usable while a tool runs (J-HUMAN-GREEN), and is disabled the same way while a claim, a release or a submission runs; every tool button is disabled while any work runs (D-REV-2). Submitted, the row is gone with the form. Release claim shown only once a form is open; Release claim usable while work runs; two primary buttons; a notice as a toast; a notice for what ended well; Submit or Release claim outside the Evaluation or a window's width away from the form; a button that says Release alone, which reads as releasing what is reviewed; a key cap on Submit or Release claim.
D-REV-7
Review's Pane is one --surface-panel column: at its top the filter field, as Verify's Search is drawn (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group, D-PANE-1), holding its query as plain typed text; its suggestions drop under it as the Search's do. Its starting query is type:human waiting. Under it, the reviews it matches, grouped by project under the project's name as a group label (D-SYS-5), newest first, each one --row line: the request's status icon in its colour (a waiting Human review the human icon in --human), the eval as {Artifact}/{eval} in --ink cut with an ellipsis, and at the right in --ink-3 with tabular figures how long it waits, led by {claimant} · once claimed; its branch is the review's header's (D-REV-8). The open review's row has --selection and, while the keyboard is in the list, the 2px inset bar (D-SYS-7). With none matching, one --ink-2 line says so. a second line under each review; a count heading over the list; a waiting list in another mode's Pane; the filter's terms drawn as chips or blocks; a card per review; a dash where there is no claimant.
D-REV-8
A review's way back and its place in the filter: its header row begins, at its left, with a reference chip (D-REF-1), the back icon and Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}, the Run's ID in --mono; the eval as {Artifact}/{eval} and its title follow (D-REV-1). A review opened by a reference that the filter does not list has, under the header row and over the review's panels, one line of --row-dense on --surface-panel, opened above by --line-group: the open icon and Opened from a reference · outside the filter ({why}) in --ink-2 at --text-small, why being claimed by you, claimed by {claimant}, submitted or not a Human review; the line goes when the filter lists the review. The draft has no mark of its own: what is written is the form as it stands, the same in Review, the Detail, the pet overlay and a pointed reference (J-HUMAN-DRAFT). a banner in a status colour; the filter changed to list the review; a dialog to say a review is outside the filter; a saved or draft word beside the form.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows its sections read-only in the same column, its title suffixed read-only in the Main area header; its Debug (a Run's Technical) folds and unfolds by its header row (D-INS-9). a disabled form standing in for read-only.
