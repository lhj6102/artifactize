Review: a Human review in the Main area

A waiting review opened from the Pane fills the Main area, its eval and Artifact above it. It reads top to bottom as the instruction, the Tools, their Tool log and the Result. The same component shows on a Human eval's chip inside a Run's Detail, and a request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

  1. 1
    Title and stateThe eval's title at 14px and the stage pill (CLAIM while unclaimed or taken by another reviewer, REVIEW (yours) once claimed here, completed); under it the request's facts in the status colour, naming the claimant.
  2. 2
    ToolsGrouped by the Artifact that declares them, the reviewed one first, each a small ▶ button with its description in secondary ink.
  3. 3
    Tool logMonospace in an input-surface box: the run's start line and end line quiet, printed lines in ink, stderr in red; it follows its newest line unless scrolled up.
  4. 4
    ResultGREEN and RED as a segmented choice outlined in the chosen verdict's colour; then the form from the eval's schema: label semibold, * on a required field, Optional in grey inside an empty optional one.
  5. 5
    FooterBusy state or the last notice at the left; Release at the right; Submit is the one primary button.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review reads top to bottom in one scrolling column: title row (title at --text-title, the stage pill of --radius-chip on --surface-group, or --accent-soft in --accent while it is yours), the request's facts in the status colour, the instruction, Tools, Tool log, Result, then the footer. Opened from the Pane, the eval and its Artifact are named in one --ink-2 line above it. Its states read without colour: Unclaimed shows the primary Claim to review button in the Result; Taken (another reviewer holds the claim) keeps the CLAIM pill, names the claimant in the facts line and says Claimed by {reviewer}. Read-only until released. in --ink-2 in the Result, with no Claim button and the tools disabled; Claimed shows REVIEW (yours), the GREEN / RED choice and the enabled tools; Submitted shows the completed result in place of the form. two columns; the Result above the Tools; a Taken review that looks claimable.
D-REV-2
Tools are grouped by the Artifact that declares them, the reviewed one first, each group led by the Artifact mark and name semibold; each tool is a small (22px) ▶ operation button with its description in --ink-2 at --text-small, an error under it in --red. Before a claim the buttons are disabled and a line says so. tool buttons in the accent; a tool without its description.
D-REV-3
The Tool log is a --surface-input box with a --line-input border in --mono at --text-small: each run's ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2, printed lines in --ink, stderr in --red; lines wrap within the box, which scrolls on its own and follows its newest line unless scrolled up; its text selects. a log that grows the review; stderr told apart only by colour (the line keeps its text as printed).
D-REV-4
The Result begins with the GREEN / RED choice as two --control buttons with --line-group borders; the chosen one takes its verdict's colour as text and border, semibold, its face unchanged; each carries the key cap of the key that chooses it (g and r before a form is open, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+R while editing), as Claim to review carries c. The form's fields follow: the label semibold with * in --red when required, inputs on --surface-input with --line-input, Optional in --ink-3 inside an empty optional field; lists add with + Add and remove with ✕; a failing path shows in --red under the form and the entries stay. a filled green or red button; a JSON editor.
D-REV-5
Submit is the one primary (--accent) button, right-aligned under the form with its Ctrl+S key cap; the footer holds the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel, or the last notice (--green done, --red failed), and Release with its u key cap at the right. two primary buttons; a notice as a toast.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows its sections read-only in the same column, its title suffixed read-only in the Main area header. a disabled form standing in for read-only.
