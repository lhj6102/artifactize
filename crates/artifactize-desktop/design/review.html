Review: the reviews and a review's evaluation space

Review mode: its Pane holds the filter, a plain-text query (type:human waiting to start), and the reviews it matches, the open one selected, which names its eval; a review chosen there, or opened by a reference, fills the Main area under a header row that holds only its way back to the Run and Artifact in Verify. A review opened by a reference shows whether or not the filter lists it, the filter staying as it was. A review is one column, as a video editor's Inspector, the same in Review and in a Run's Detail: its title and the claim's state, the instruction, then Evidence (the tools, and the Tool log and the Points once there is something in them, folded to one line each) and the Verdict (GREEN or RED, only the chosen verdict's fields, and the actions), Debug folded last. A request opened by ID that is not a waiting review shows read-only in the same place.

Rules

D-REV-1
A review is one column, the same in Review and on a Human eval's tab in the Detail, as a video editor's Inspector: no second panel and no split, only what the reviewer reads, looks at and decides, in that order. In Review's Main area the column is --measure wide, centred, on --surface-panel, under the header row (D-REV-8), scrolling on its own; in the Detail it takes the Detail's width. It holds, top to bottom: the head row, the eval's title in --ink-bright at --text-lead cut with an ellipsis (in the Detail the Detail's title says it, and the head row keeps only the rest), and at its right, quiet in --ink-2 at --text-small, where the claim stands: Waiting, Taken · {claimant}, Yours, or nothing once submitted, the completed result saying it; in the Detail, Open in Review (D-REF-1) after it. Then the instruction, its own block at --text-lead in --ink, read first. Then two sections, each under a --line-group line with its header row (D-SYS-5): Evidence (D-REV-2, D-REV-3, D-REV-9) and Verdict (D-REV-4, D-REV-5). Last, Debug, folded to its header row in --ink-2 at weight 400, which a click (also t) unfolds into the request's ID, its mode, its claimant and how long it has waited. Nothing else stands in the column: no pill, no count and no second title. two columns or an Evaluation panel beside the review; a stage pill; the eval's title said twice; the Tool log or Points drawn empty; a section for each of Tools, Tool log and Points; the request's ID or mode outside Debug; a card or a box around the whole review.
D-REV-2
Evidence starts with the tools: one row of small (22px) buttons that wraps, each the run icon then its operation's name, its description its tooltip and accessible description, grouped by the Artifact that declares them or whose type provides them, the reviewed one first, a group led by the Artifact's name in --ink-2 at --text-small only when more than one Artifact has tools. Before a claim the buttons are drawn disabled, their tooltip Claim to run the tools; while any of the review's work runs (a tool, a claim, a release or a submission) they are disabled too, so one tool runs at a time. A tool's failure is said in words in the Verdict's action row (D-REV-5) and its run ends red in the Tool log (D-REV-3); no other notice says it. tool buttons in the accent; descriptions as lines under the buttons; a count beside the tools; a heading over the only group.
D-REV-3
The Tool log is not drawn until a tool of the review has run. Then, under the tools, it is one row of --row in --mono at --text-small, folded to the last run: the fold icon, its start (▶ HH:MM:SS · operation) in --ink-2, its last stderr line if it printed one, in --ink behind a 2px --red rule, cut with an ellipsis, and at the row's right its end line followed by how many lines it printed (■ exit 0 · 8 lines), or, while it runs, its newest line. A click on the row (also Enter on it) unfolds every run's lines in a box under it, the fold icon becoming the unfold icon, and another folds them; a run that fails unfolds nothing by itself: its red end shows folded. Unfolded, the box is --surface-input inside --line-input of --radius-control, --mono at --text-small, as tall as its lines up to half the column's height, then scrolling on its own and following its newest line unless scrolled up; each run's ▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact and its end line in --ink-2, the end line one of four: ■ exit N (its ■ in --red unless N is 0), ■ done, ■ launched once a file is handed to its desktop application or an Artifact Viewer opens, after which that application's lines keep arriving under the same run until it exits, or ■ error: … in --red when the tool could not run; printed lines in --ink, stderr in --ink behind a 2px --red rule at its left, a warning: or error: prefix it prints in --red; lines wrap within its width and select. A submission clears it: its row and its box go. an empty log drawn before any run; a log box open by default; a log box taller than its lines while they are few, outweighing the instruction; a Tool log header of its own.
D-REV-4
The Verdict begins, once claimed, with the GREEN / RED choice; until one is chosen nothing follows it but the actions (D-REV-5), and once one is, only that verdict's fields follow as two --control buttons with --line-group borders; each leads with a small dot in its verdict's colour, the same size on both; the chosen one has its word in --ink and a 1px --outline-selected border, at the other's weight, its face unchanged (D-SYS-13). The buttons show no key cap (D-SYS-12); while a form is open they switch the verdict, each verdict keeping its draft (J-HUMAN-RED). The form's fields follow, one under another, each its label in --label with * in --red when required, then its input on --surface-input with --line-input, Optional in --ink-2 inside an empty optional one; the schema's constants are filled in and not shown. By kind: text, a field of two lines that grows; a number, a one-line field of --control height about 120px wide; true/false, its two values side by side as round options, the chosen one a small --current-mark dot on the fixed dark face of every option, with a third option for no value when optional, labelled Optional in --ink-2 as an empty optional field is, and chosen while the field has no value; a choice, a one-line field showing the chosen option with the unfold icon at its right, empty as Optional (or a dash when required). A list of text is numbered items, each a text field with the remove icon at its right, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. A list of groups is its groups one under another, each inside a 1px --line-group line of --radius-control, headed in --ink-2 at --text-small by the list's name and the group's number with the remove icon at the right, its fields inside, then a quiet Add led by the add icon. Removing the last group of a list of groups leaves one empty group to type in; a list of text has no such floor: removing its last item leaves the list empty under its label, with Add to start it again. A failing path shows in --red under the form and the entries stay. a filled green or red button; a key cap on a verdict; a JSON editor; a constant shown, even read-only; a list of groups emptied to nothing; an empty item forced back into a list of text; a checkbox for true/false that cannot say no value.
D-REV-5
The Verdict's last row holds the review's actions, kept in view at the column's bottom however long the form (it sticks there while the column scrolls, on the column's surface): Release claim, a quiet button, at the left; Submit, the one primary button (the secondary face with a 1px --line-primary outline, D-SYS-13), naming its verdict (Submit GREEN), at the column's right edge; between them, while any work of the review runs (a claim, a release, a tool or a submission), the busy spinner with its seconds and Cancel, or what failed in --red, a failed tool's run also ending red in the Tool log (D-REV-3). What ended well is not said here. Before a claim the Verdict holds only Claim to review, the primary button, with a claim in progress beside it as its seconds and Cancel; a review taken by another reviewer holds one --ink-2 line, Taken by {claimant}. Once claimed, Release claim is in the row from the start, before a verdict is chosen too; Submit joins it once a verdict is chosen. While any work runs, Release claim stays in place but cannot be used (its text in --ink-3); Submit stays usable while a tool runs (J-HUMAN-GREEN) and is disabled while a claim, a release or a submission runs. Submitted, the row is gone with the form, and the Verdict shows the result as submitted, each field by name, read-only. actions that scroll out of reach under a long form; Release claim moved or hidden while editing; two primary buttons; a word or a toast saying it was submitted.
D-REV-7
Review's Pane is one --surface-panel column: at its top the filter field, as Verify's Search is drawn (a --surface-input field in --mono, closed below by --line-group, D-PANE-1), holding its query as plain typed text; its suggestions drop under it as the Search's do. Its starting query is type:human waiting. Under it, the reviews it matches, grouped by project under the project's name as a group label (D-SYS-5), newest first, each one --row-pane row, chosen by a click anywhere on it (J-PANE-ROWS): the request's status icon in its colour (a waiting Human review the human icon in --human), the eval as {Artifact}/{eval} in --ink cut with an ellipsis, and at the right in --ink-2 with tabular figures how long it waits, led by {claimant} · once claimed; its branch is the review's header's (D-REV-8). A review's eval is --ink-2; the open review's row is the darker --selection row with its eval in --ink (D-SYS-13); the pointer over a row gives it --surface-group alone, and the keyboard on it the 2px --focus ring inside the whole row. With none matching, one --ink-2 line says so. When the search matches more reviews than the library returns at once, More reviews ends the list as Older runs ends a timeline (D-PANE-3): a Pane row of its own, --row-pane high, its words in --ink-2 at --text-small, lit on --surface-group under the pointer and ringed by the keyboard's focus, which brings the next reviews in below the last, the open review and the list's scroll kept. a pager or page numbers; matching reviews left out with no way to reach them; a second line under each review; a count heading over the list; a waiting list in another mode's Pane; the filter's terms drawn as chips or blocks; a card per review; a dash where there is no claimant.
D-REV-8
Review's header row is the stage header's grammar (D-MAIN-1): 32px on --surface-panel, closed below by --line-panel, controls only. At its left the way back, a tool led by the back icon (D-REF-1): Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}, the Run's ID in --mono, by luminance alone, cut with an ellipsis at 320px. The review's eval is named by the Pane's selected row (D-REV-7) and its title by the column's head (D-REV-1). A review opened by a reference shows the same whether or not the filter lists it, and nothing says which (J-REVIEW-REF). The draft has no mark of its own: what is written is the form as it stands, the same in Review, the Detail and the pet overlay (J-HUMAN-DRAFT). the way back as a chip or a bordered button; the eval or its title in the header row; a line, a banner or a dialog saying a review is outside the filter; the filter changed to list the review; a saved or draft word beside the form.
D-REV-9
The Points are not drawn until Point has added one (J-HUMAN-POINTS). Then they stand in Evidence under the Tool log's row (or under the tools before any run), with no header of their own: one --row row per point, newest last, at --text-small: the point icon in --ink-3, the pointed text in --mono --ink cut with an ellipsis, where it was pointed ({Artifact} · {viewer}) and when (HH:MM) in --ink-2 with tabular figures, Insert, a small quiet button, drawn disabled while no verdict is chosen, and at the row's right the remove icon in --ink-2, which removes the point from the draft. Insert puts the text at the caret of the chosen verdict's text field last focused, else into its comment field, and leaves the point listed. Past five points they scroll on their own. A point just added stands on --accent-soft until the next one, as Point's only answer (D-VIEW-2). a Points header or count; a Notes field or any free text box apart from the form; points written into a field by themselves; points in the submitted result; a toast for a point.
D-REV-6
A request opened by ID that is not a Human review shows its sections read-only in the same column, its title suffixed read-only in the Main area header; its Debug (a Run's Technical) folds and unfolds by its header row (D-INS-9). a disabled form standing in for read-only.
