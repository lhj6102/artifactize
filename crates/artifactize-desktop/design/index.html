The desktop app, designed as a workbench

This Artifact plans the desktop app's UI and UX: one page per screen, drawn with the same anatomy the app must have, and ux-graph-doc.html, the relations between the screens. Each page ends in rules with ids (D-…); Agent evals judge the app's UI against them. The style applies what a professional workbench does well: the screen is divided by the work, the selection drives an inspector, surfaces are few and neutral, boundaries are lines rather than gaps, and colour means something only where it sits. The brand, images and icons of any other product stay theirs; every value here is our own token.

Layout by workflow

The window is one screen. A global bar carries what belongs to the window (brand, the reviewer, an update notice, Settings, the command palette). Below it, the work reads left to right: find (the Pane: a search, the live branches with their Runs, the reviews waiting for a person), look (the Main area: a Run's or a Current's graph, or a Human review) and inspect (the Detail, a panel docked at the graph's right edge, showing the selected Artifact or eval). Each view keeps the bar and the Pane and changes only what the Main area gives its main representation: a graph, a review, a read-only request.

Screens

  • MonitorThe one screen with a Run shown: bar, Pane, Main area and the Run graph.
  • CurrentThe Main area as a branch's Current: the scan header, the Current graph and the structure text.
  • DetailThe inspector, a panel docked at the graph's right edge: an Artifact's tab and an eval's Summary.
  • ReviewA Human review in the Main area: instruction, Tools, Tool log and the Result form.
  • SettingsThe modal over the screen: Providers, Remote and Limits.
  • OverlaysThe command palette, Help, Open by ID, the Leave and Update dialogs and the graph's find field.
  • UX graphHow one screen leads to another: every transition of UX-GRAPH.md, read by screen.

Tokens

Every colour, size and radius in the app comes from tokens.css, named by role. The app has one appearance, dark, whatever the system's; the swatches below render its tokens. A status has one colour wherever it shows (a list, a chip, a graph's node) and is read with a glyph or a word beside it, never alone.

Rules of the system

These hold on every screen; each screen's page adds its own.

D-SYS-1
Every colour, radius, row height and font size of the UI is a token of tokens.css, used by its role; a component declares no colour literal. a hex value or white in a component; a token used for a role it does not name (a status colour as a border of emphasis, the accent as a status).
D-SYS-2
Surfaces step by role: the bar is darkest, the window and the graph canvas next, a panel above them, a group inside the panel one step lighter; a field or log box is one step darker than its panel; what floats is raised one step and set apart by a panel line and at most a faint shadow (--scrim, 2px offset, 8px blur). gradients; large soft shadows; a card around every item; a status colour as a panel or row background.
D-SYS-3
Boundaries are 1px lines in the strength of what they separate: --line-panel between panels (the bar's bottom, the Pane/Main divider, the Detail's and a dialog's edge), --line-group between groups inside a panel (section tops, branch rows, the chip row's bottom), --line-input around a field. Large panels meet at a line, not at a gap. gaps of 16px or more between panels; boxes drawn around single items.
D-SYS-4
Density: a list row is --row (24px), a dense row --row-dense (22px), a control --control (24px) tall; a panel pads its sides by --pad (12px); spacing uses the 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 scale and never exceeds 16px between groups of one panel. 32px controls; 20px panel paddings; empty space doing a line's job.
D-SYS-5
Type: body 13px, secondary 12px, labels 11px uppercase and letter-spaced in --ink-2; a section label is neutral ink, never the accent. Numbers that align or change (ages, times, counts, tokens, IDs) use tabular figures. Names may be semibold; nothing else is bold but a status word, a chosen choice and a title. accent-coloured headings; proportional figures in an age column.
D-SYS-6
Colour carries meaning only on the element that has it: a status dot, glyph or word, a node's light and border, an edge, a verdict button, stderr lines. A meaning has one colour: a status takes the same token in the Pane, a chip, a row and a graph's node, and a graph has no palette of its own (in progress is --running on a node as in a list, waiting --queued, blocked --blocked, ERROR --error). The accent marks only what is interactive or emphasised (links, the primary button, the focused panel's top line, the active tab's line). reading every red as an error or every green as success; tinting a node's face, a row or a panel by status; a second colour for a status on a graph; a star or a selection in a status colour.
D-SYS-7
States are told apart without colour: every status shows its glyph or word beside its colour; the selected row has the --selection background and, while the keyboard is in its list, a 2px inset bar; keyboard focus is a 2px --focus ring on every control; hover only highlights and opens nothing: a row or a borderless control takes --surface-group, and a button, whose face already is that surface, keeps its face while its edge steps up to --line-input (the primary button's to --ink-on-accent); the one exception the journeys ask for is the whole's Minimize/Expand button, whose native title names its key m, and no other control has a tooltip. a selection shown by colour alone; a focus ring removed; a popup, summary or menu on hover.
D-SYS-8
The same action has the same mark everywhere: ▶ runs a tool and ■ ends its run, ☆/★ stars, ✕ removes, + Add adds, ▸/▾ folds, one chevron expands a branch, the Artifact mark precedes an Artifact's name. A key cap (Ctrl+K) names a key wherever a key does the same as a control. two glyphs for one action; an icon without a label or an accessible name.
D-SYS-9
Long and Korean labels keep the layout: a name wraps or is cut with an ellipsis inside its own box; nothing a panel shows widens the panel; logs and transcripts wrap within their width. a pane sized by its content; a title that pushes its actions out of the header.
D-SYS-10
The core task works with the side panels out of the way: a graph can be read and an Artifact opened with the Pane at its minimum width and the Detail closed; a Human review can be claimed, its tools run and its result submitted inside the Detail alone. a control that exists only in the Pane for a task of the Main area.

Before handing over a screen

  • The selected node and the open Detail name the same Artifact.
  • Every state is told apart without colour (glyph, word, weight, outline).
  • A status has the same colour everywhere it shows.
  • A long Korean label and large text keep the layout.
  • Keyboard focus is visible on every control.
  • The core task works with the Pane narrowed and the Detail closed.
  • Contrast and click targets are checked in our own environment.
  • No value is copied from another product's screenshot; every value is a role token.
