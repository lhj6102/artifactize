# Desktop app: Review journeys

Shared app, Window and Settings rules live in [index.md](index.md).

## Review

Review is where Human reviews are found and evaluated: its Pane holds a filter and the list of
reviews it matches; its main area shows one review, its evaluation space.

- **J-REVIEW-LIST**: Review's Pane is one column: a filter field at the top, its query as typed, and below it the reviews of every repository on this machine that the filter matches, those of removed branches included while their folder exists, grouped by project, newest first, each one line: its eval as `{Artifact}/{eval}`, how long it waits and, once claimed, who claimed it. Its starting filter is `type:human waiting`, the Human reviews waiting for a person. A review whose repository folder is deleted leaves the list on its own, unless another worktree still waits on the same review, which then lists it as its own. With none matching, the list says so. When more reviews match than the library returns at once, **More reviews** ends the list and brings the next ones in below, the open review and the list's scroll kept. A click on a review shows it in the main area.
- **J-REVIEW-FILTER**: a click in the filter field, or the palette's "Filter the reviews", focuses it, showing Review and the Pane first when they are not shown. Terms must all match: `type:human` (also `type:agent`) keeps the requests of that kind, `waiting` those still waiting for a reviewer to claim them, free text matches part of an eval, a project or a branch, `repo:` and `branch:` part of that one field. The query is plain text, edited as typed, and the field suggests terms as the search does (J-PANE-SUGGEST); Esc puts back `type:human waiting`.
- **J-REVIEW-REF**: following a reference to a review (Open in Review in Verify, Open a request by ID, the launch's `review ID`, the pet overlay's ID) shows Review with that exact review open, whether or not the filter lists it, for example once it is claimed or submitted; the filter and the list stay as they were, and nothing more is said about it.
- **J-REVIEW-BACK**: a review's stage header (J-STAGE) holds, at its left, its way back when the review was opened by following Open in Review from Verify: `Verify · {Run} › {Artifact}`, a borderless tool, which shows Verify as it was left, that Detail open; a review opened from its Pane row, the pet overlay, Open a request by ID or the launch has none. At its right, Find in the conversation, which finds text in the review's conversation as Chat's Find does (J-CHAT-FIND), then **Open in Verify**, which shows Verify with the Run that asked for the review chosen and the reviewed Artifact's Detail open on the review's eval. The Pane's selected row names the review's eval.
- **J-HUMAN-DRAFT**: the result being written for a review is that review's draft for this reviewer: its points, the places Point added (J-HUMAN-POINTS), its reasons (J-HUMAN-REASONS), the verdict chosen, and what is typed in the chosen verdict's own fields (J-HUMAN-FORM). Artifactize keeps it: it stays as written whichever window or mode has focus, through mode switches, the pet overlay and an Artifact Viewer, and after the app restarts; it is kept when the claim is released and gone once the review is submitted, which submits the chosen verdict made of the reasons and those fields alone. Review, the Detail's review, the pet overlay and Point all write the same draft, and a change made in one shows in the others; a point or a reason that arrives while a field is being typed in changes nothing typed. Text typed in a reasons field and not yet added is that field's own until Enter adds it.

## Human review

- **J-HUMAN-CLAIM**: the Claim to review button, at the left of the review's decision bar until the review is claimed, claims a waiting Human review for this reviewer (also `c`); **Release claim**, in its place once the review is claimed, releases it once nothing of the review is running, also while reasons are being written, the draft kept (also `u` outside a field). Beside the button the decision bar says where the claim stands: `Waiting` or `Yours`. While a claim or a release runs, its seconds and Cancel stand beside its button, and a failure says why there. A claim held by another reviewer shows as taken, the claimant named beside its stage; one this reviewer made elsewhere (another window, the CLI) is theirs and shows as `Yours`.
- **J-HUMAN-LAYOUT**: a Human review is a conversation, the same in Review's main area and on a Human eval's tab in the Detail, read as Chat's is (J-CHAT-SEND, J-CHAT-READ): the review's request asks at the top and the reviewer answers under it. At its top a head line: the eval's title (in the Detail, the Detail's title says it, and the head line holds Open in Review). Then the request, drawn as Chat's agent text is, without a box: its instruction, which reads first; the Tools, usable where they stand (J-HUMAN-TOOL); while a verdict is chosen whose eval asks more of it than reasons, that verdict's own fields (J-HUMAN-FORM); and **Debug**, one quiet folded line, which a click unfolds into the request's ID, its mode, its claimant and how long it has waited, and folds again (also `t`). Under it, newest last as they happen, each tool's run as one folded line (J-HUMAN-LOG) and each point Point adds (J-HUMAN-POINTS). Under the request, the reviewer's reasons, each a bubble as Chat's messages are, oldest first (J-HUMAN-REASONS). At the bottom, where Chat's composer stands, two parts apart from each other: the **reasons box**, drawn as Chat's composer field, holding only the reasons field and **Add reason** (J-HUMAN-REASONS); and under it the **decision bar**, a bar of its own, with the claim at its left, where it stands and Claim to review or Release claim (J-HUMAN-CLAIM), and the decision at its right, the verdict choice, GREEN or RED, and Submit (J-HUMAN-GREEN, J-HUMAN-RED). Before a claim the reasons box is not shown: one quiet line says that a claim opens the reasons and the verdict, and the decision bar holds Claim to review, its GREEN, RED and Submit unavailable. A review taken by another reviewer has no reasons box, its decision bar saying `Taken by {claimant}`; a request opened by ID that is not a waiting Human review (J-OPEN-ID) has none either, its decision bar saying why it is read-only, its request's sections shown read-only above. The conversation scrolls on its own and follows its newest line while it is read at its end. In Review it is a readable measure wide, centred under the stage header; in the Detail it takes the Detail's width. Nothing in it draws the eye away from the instruction; a field shows its focus only while it is typed in. What ended well is said by a run's end or by the completed result alone: once submitted, the reasons box, the decision bar and the runs go, and the result as submitted stands as the reviewer's last bubble, each field by name, read-only.
- **J-HUMAN-TOOL**: the Tools stand in the review's request under its instruction, grouped by the Artifact that declares them or whose type provides them, the reviewed one first, a group named by its Artifact only when more than one has tools, each a `▶` button named by its operation, its description shown on hover; before a claim they are disabled, saying `Claim to run the tools`; after a claim a click on one runs it at once, its run added to the conversation (J-HUMAN-LOG). While any work of the review runs, a tool's run, a claim, a release or a submission, every tool button is disabled, so one tool runs at a time. A launch tool opens its application and returns at once; a viewer tool opens its Artifact Viewer (J-VIEWER) and returns at once; a failing tool says its error on its run's line.
- **J-HUMAN-LOG**: each run of a review's tool is one line of the conversation, added under the request when it starts, as a Chat tool's line is: its operation and Artifact, then, while it runs, its newest line, its seconds and Cancel, which cancels it; once ended, its last stderr line if it printed one and its end with how many lines it printed: `exit N` (red unless 0), `done`, `launched` once a file is handed to its desktop application or an Artifact Viewer opens, or `error: …` in red when the tool could not run. A click on the line unfolds its lines under it, and another folds them: `▶ HH:MM:SS · operation · Artifact`, then each line the tool prints, stderr marked by a red rule, its `warning:` or `error:` prefix in red, then its end line (`■ exit N`, `■ done`, `■ launched` or `■ error: …`). A launched application's lines keep arriving under its own run until it exits. The lines are monospace, wrap, scroll on their own past half the conversation's height and follow the newest unless the reader scrolled up; their text can be selected and copied into a reason. A submission clears the runs from the conversation.
- **J-HUMAN-POINTS**: the places Point added to the review (J-POINT) are its **Points**, each one quiet line of the conversation, added under the request as it is pointed, newest last: its text, where it was pointed (the Artifact and its viewer) and when, **Insert**, which puts its text into the reasons field at its caret, and a remove button that takes the point out of the draft. The Points show once there is one. They are the reviewer's own aid: a submission keeps only the verdict, and the points go with the draft.
- **J-HUMAN-REASONS**: a reviewer writes the review's reasons one at a time in the reasons box: **Add reason** at the field's right (also Enter) adds what is typed as the newest reason and empties the field (Shift+Enter starts a new line in it); Add reason is unavailable while the field is empty. Each reason shows as a bubble at the conversation's right, as a Chat message does, exactly as typed, with Edit and Remove beside it: Edit turns that bubble into a field in its place holding its text, where Enter keeps the change and Esc leaves it as it was; Remove takes the reason out. A reason carries no verdict: whichever verdict is chosen, its result is made of the reasons when it is submitted (J-HUMAN-GREEN, J-HUMAN-RED).
- **J-HUMAN-GREEN**: after a claim the decision bar's GREEN chooses GREEN (also `g`) and Submit, then `Submit GREEN`, submits it (also Ctrl+S) with the reasons as its comment, or with none, also while a tool of the review runs: the submission cancels that tool first. While it runs, its seconds and Cancel stand in Submit's place. The completed result stays shown.
- **J-HUMAN-RED**: the decision bar's RED chooses RED (also `r`) and Submit, then `Submit RED`, submits it (also Ctrl+S) with the reasons as its change list, so RED needs at least one reason. GREEN and RED switch the verdict at any time, the reasons kept. Submit is unavailable, saying why just left of it, while no verdict is chosen, while text typed in the reasons field is not yet added, and while the chosen verdict cannot be submitted as it stands, as RED without a reason or a required field of its own left empty (J-HUMAN-FORM); a result the library still refuses is not submitted: what failed is shown and the draft stays correctable.
- **J-HUMAN-FORM**: an eval may ask more of a verdict than its reasons: its pass or fail schema's other fields. While that verdict is chosen they stand in the review's request under its Tools: text, number, true/false and choice fields, a list of text with `+ Add` and `✕` per item, and repeated groups of fields with `+ Add` for a group and `✕` per group, one group always left to type in. Constants are filled in and not shown; `*` marks a required field and an empty optional one says Optional in grey; the field the reasons fill is not among them. There is no JSON editor.
- **J-HUMAN-TEXT**: text fields take any language, including Korean composed by an input method; no key typed during a composition, and no key without Ctrl (⌘) typed in a field, does anything but enter text.
- **J-HUMAN-ESC**: Cancel beside the running work cancels it. In the Detail, Close or a click on the graph's background first cancels the review's running work, then closes the Detail, the draft kept (J-DETAIL). (Also Esc, which first leaves a reason being edited or the reasons field, then takes the same steps and, for a review shown in Review, returns to Review's Pane as its last step, showing it first when it is hidden.)

## Artifact Viewer, Point and the pet overlay

An Artifact's type gives it the tools of the plugin that provides that type: `files` (File
Explorer), `markdown` (Markdown viewer) and `artifactize` (Artifactize Viewer) are built in,
and a project can add others, such as the `html` example. A plugin's Human tool opens its
viewer in an Artifact Viewer window of the app.

- **J-VIEWER**: a viewer tool, run from a review's Tools or an Artifact's Tools in the Detail (`▶`), opens an Artifact Viewer: a window of the app of its own, titled `{Artifact} — {viewer}`, showing that Artifact in its plugin's viewer, never the system's default application. Running it again while that Artifact's viewer is open brings that window to the front. Closing a viewer's window closes it alone. A Custom Artifact's `launch` and `output` tools work as before (J-HUMAN-TOOL).
- **J-VIEWER-FILES**: the File Explorer shows the Artifact's files as a tree at its left, folders folding and unfolding by a click, and a click on a file shows its text in the document view at its right, with line numbers, read-only.
- **J-VIEWER-MARKDOWN**: the Markdown viewer shows the Artifact's Markdown rendered, headings, lists, links, tables and code as they read; an Artifact of several Markdown files lists them at its left, a click showing one.
- **J-VIEWER-ARTIFACTIZE**: the Artifactize Viewer shows what an Artifact of type `artifactize` covers: the Artifacts declared under its path as nodes and the references between them as plain connections, with no verdicts (it shows what the project declares, not a Run). A click on an Artifact opens its panel at the right: its name and type, its evals, each with its kind, its title, its criteria and the Artifacts it reads. The graph pans and zooms as Verify's does.
- **J-VIEWER-PLUGIN**: a project's own viewer plugin (the `html` example included) shows its own page inside the Artifact Viewer; for `html`, the Artifact's page in an embedded browser that can be used as in a browser. A plugin's page reaches the Artifact's files and the active review only through the viewer's bridge, nothing else of the app. A viewer page asks the bridge for its context when it starts: until the page first reaches the bridge the strip says *Loading {plugin}…*, and a page that finished loading without reaching it is said, in the strip, not to have connected, until it does. Once connected, the strip shows the latest status line the page reports (for `html`, the page shown and its title once loaded, or why it failed), each replacing the last; the strip's Point tells the page to point (J-POINT).
- **J-POINT**: while this reviewer holds a claimed review, its active review (the one last shown in Review), every viewer that supports it shows **Point**. A click on Point, then a click on a place in the Artifact, adds that place as readable text to the review's Points (J-HUMAN-POINTS), with the Artifact and viewer it was pointed in: an HTML element as a CSS selector (`#checkout .total`), a Markdown position as its line and column (`L42:C9`), an Artifact or an eval in the Artifactize Viewer as its ID (`front-design`, `front-design/matches-layout`). Pointing never submits and never writes a reason. Esc, or Point again, stops pointing without adding anything. With no claimed review, Point is not shown.
- **J-PET**: while this reviewer holds a claimed review and works anywhere but its evaluation space (another mode, an Artifact Viewer, another application), a small pet overlay stays at the bottom right of the screen, above every window, tied to the active review: the pet, the review's eval and Run (a click on them shows the review in Review, J-REVIEW-REF), and the same draft, split as Review's is, compactly: its points, each with Insert, which puts its text into the overlay's reasons field (J-HUMAN-POINTS), and its reasons, each with Remove; then the reasons field with **Add** (also Enter, J-HUMAN-REASONS) and **Point** beside them (pointing in the Artifact Viewer of the review's Artifact that is open, and disabled, saying why, while none is); then, apart, a row with the GREEN and RED choice and **Submit**, unavailable and saying why as in Review (J-HUMAN-RED). Typing in it writes the draft, kept as focus moves between the overlay, a viewer and other windows. Submit submits the review as Review's does (J-HUMAN-GREEN, J-HUMAN-RED). The overlay goes when the review is submitted or released, and while the review's evaluation space is in front.
