# Desktop app: Verify journeys

Shared app, Window and Settings rules live in [index.md](index.md).

## Verify

Verify is the machine's monitor: its Pane holds a search and the live branches with their
Runs; its main area shows a Run's graph, with the Detail of the chosen Artifact. A branch's
Current is not here: it is Chat's, in a session's Graph view (J-CHAT-GRAPH).

- **J-PANE**: Verify's Pane is one column: a search field at the top and the branches below it. The divider between it and the main area is shown whenever the Pane is (J-PANE-TOGGLE). There is no Scope heading, no Runs list and no list of waiting reviews: those are Review's (J-REVIEW-LIST), and the bar counts them (J-BAR).
- **J-BRANCHES**: each branch reads as its project and branch, `artifactize / feat/x`, the branch in bold and the project quieter: a Git worktree that exists now with the branch it has checked out (a detached one by its folder's name), or a folder outside Git that exists now and holds its definitions (`.artifactize/`). Its projects are those with Runs on this machine and the one the app was started for with `--repo` or `graph --repo`; a worktree or folder that was removed, or whose worktree Git no longer lists, is not shown, and worktrees added or removed appear or go within seconds, without a reload. Who ran a Run, an agent session or a person, makes no entry of its own: a branch's Runs are the Runs of that project on that branch, from whichever folder they ran in.
- **J-BRANCH-RUNS**: a branch is folded or expanded, any number expanded at once, with a chevron before its name. Folded, its newest Run sits under its name, a dot in its status colour, its status word and its age on the right (a running one its time so far); a branch without Runs shows nothing there. Expanded, its timeline sits there instead, newest first: one entry per Run, its status dot, status word and age and, below, one line: the comment the Run was requested with (`verify --comment`), cut short to one line, or else its Run ID, the dots joined by a line; while a Run is in progress on the branch the timeline starts with it. **Older runs** at its end brings older Runs in below the oldest. A click on a branch expands it and shows its newest Run; a branch without Runs shows, in the main area, one line saying so and **Open in Chat** (J-OPEN-CHAT); a click on its chevron only expands or folds it; a click on an entry shows that one in the main area, the shown entry a darker row across the Pane, its words brighter: it is what names the shown Run, which the stage header does not repeat (J-STAGE). The branch the app was started for starts expanded. (Also keys: ↑ ↓ or `j`/`k` through the branches and the timelines, → and ← to expand or fold, Enter into the main area, and Esc from there back to the Pane.)
- **J-BRANCH-STAR**: a star at the end of a branch's name line, faint until it is on, pins it: starred branches are listed before the others, each group in the search's order, and stay starred for every later monitor of this machine's user until the star is taken away.
- **J-PANE-SEARCH**: a click in the search field at Verify's Pane's top, or the palette's "Search the branches", focuses it, showing Verify and the Pane first when they are not shown (also `/`, and Ctrl+F, in a field too, unless a graph or a Chat conversation is shown and focus is outside the Pane and outside fields, J-GRAPH-FIND, J-CHAT-FIND); its starting query is `sort:recent`. Free text and `key:value` terms must all match: free text matches part of a project or a branch, `repo:` and `branch:` part of that one field; `sort:recent` puts the branch with the most recent Run first and branches without Runs last, `sort:name` orders them by project and branch.
- **J-PANE-SUGGEST**: while a Pane's search or filter field is focused, typing a term lists suggestions under it that complete that term: the field's keys and, after a key, its values (the projects, the branches, the sort orders, the review kinds). A click on one puts it in place of the term; a key taken lists its values next. The suggestions close when the field loses focus or is emptied (also ↓ opens them, ↑ ↓ choose one, Tab or Enter takes the chosen one or the first, and Esc closes them, then puts back the field's starting query, then leaves the field).

## Verify: main area

- **J-MAIN-RUN**: a Run chosen in the Pane fills Verify's main area with the graph of its Artifacts and their verdicts, under the stage header with the graph's tools (J-STAGE); the Run's entry in the Pane, the selected row, is what names it: its status and age, and its comment or ID. Run details, among the tools, opens the Run's Detail (J-DETAIL): its outcome with what failed and what waits for a person, its tally, how long it took, its comment in full and what started it. With nothing chosen, while loading, on an error or with an empty graph the graph's place stays and says so. What went wrong, such as Runs that cannot be read, shows as a few words in the graph's status line at its top left, without moving the graph; a click on it shows it in full, and another click folds it back.
- **J-OPEN-CHAT**: where Verify leads to a branch's Current, its **Open in Chat** (the last of a Run's tools in the stage header, and for a branch without Runs in the main area) opens the branch's newest session whose checkout can be worked on, in Graph view (J-CHAT-GRAPH). If none can, it opens the newest saved session, which shows why it can't be worked on and keeps its conversation readable. If the branch has no session, New chat on that branch (J-CHAT-NEW).
- **J-RUN-ORIGIN**: a Run started by a Chat session says so at the stage header's left (J-STAGE): `Chat · {session title}`. A click on it shows Chat with that session, scrolled to the Run's reference (J-CHAT-RUN) and not following its newest line until it is read at its end (J-CHAT-READ). A Run started from the CLI or an MCP client has no such link; the Run's Detail names what started it.

## Graphs: Detail

Both graphs, a Run's in Verify and the Current in a Chat session's Graph view, take the pointer (also the keyboard: Tab reaches their Artifacts and Enter on one does what a click does). What acts on each graph is among the stage header's tools (J-STAGE): Find, Zoom out, the zoom in percent and Zoom in, and Fit View; on a Current also Refresh (J-MAIN-CURRENT). What its marks mean, and the keys it has, are in Help (J-HELP). On a graph rest only its status line at the top left, Find's list while it is open, the Navigator (J-GRAPH-NAVIGATOR) and, in Chat's Graph view, the floating composer and its reply card (J-CHAT-GRAPH).

- **J-DETAIL**: a click on an Artifact opens its Detail, a panel docked at the graph's right edge over the graph, from the graph's top to the main area's bottom without a gap, below the stage header, whose tools stay readable and usable, never beside the Pane, the graph staying visible on its left. It opens on the Artifact's own tab, which has no chips: its list of evals leads to each eval's tab (J-DETAIL-NEIGHBOURS). On an eval's tab the Detail's tabs read as a path, `[Artifact icon] {name} / [eval] [eval] …`: the Artifact's own chip, which leads back to the Artifact's tab, the Artifact icon before its name, read aloud as "Artifact {name}"; only the name is cut short when long (the Detail's title shows it whole), then a `/`, then one chip per eval showing its verdict's glyph and colour, on a Current whether it would run, would be reused or is still being checked. Eval chips that do not fit on one line wrap onto the next, within the Detail; the `/` stays with the Artifact's chip. Close is the one control of the Detail's header besides an Artifact's star (J-DETAIL-STAR). A click on another Artifact shows that Artifact's Detail; a click on the graph's background or Close closes it (also Esc), after first cancelling a Human review's running work (J-HUMAN-ESC). Run details among a Run's tools (J-STAGE) opens the Run's Detail, and closes it again: its outcome (its status, the evals that failed, its tally, how long it took and its comment in full), what started it (the CLI, an MCP client or a Chat session) and what it waits for. Everything in the Detail, transcript and log lines, long words and code included, stays within its width.
- **J-DETAIL-STAR**: a ☆ beside the title of an Artifact's Detail stars the Artifact, a favorite: it turns ★, and a click on it again takes the star away. A starred Artifact stays starred on every branch of its project, in Runs and Currents alike, for every later monitor of this machine's user until the star is taken away.
- **J-DETAIL-NEIGHBOURS**: the Artifact's own tab is shown first. It lists the Artifact's evals with their verdicts (a click on one shows its tab). Under Neighbours, folded until it is unfolded (J-DETAIL-SECTIONS), it draws the Artifact between its neighbours with the graph's nodes and edges, the Artifacts its evals use on its left and those whose evals use it on its right; the drawing does not pan, zoom or drag, and a click on a neighbour opens that neighbour's Detail. Then come its Human tools (J-DETAIL-TOOLS) and, on a Run, its Path, the target and, for a Live Artifact, its address; on a Current, its Definition and Source (J-DEFINITION, J-DEFINITION-SOURCE); every other fact, what it waits for included, folds under Debug. Each of these is a section under its own header row (J-DETAIL-SECTIONS).
- **J-DETAIL-REVIEW**: on a Run's graph, an Artifact's tab lists each Human eval waiting for a person with its stage beside its verdict: claimable, yours, or taken by its claimant; under it, **Claim to review** while it can be claimed, **Release claim** while it is this reviewer's, and **Open in Review**, which shows Review with that review open (J-REVIEW-REF). A claim or a release made here is the same claim as in Review (J-HUMAN-CLAIM). The Human eval's tab holds the review itself and its own Open in Review.
- **J-DETAIL-SECTIONS**: below its tabs the Detail shows sections, each under a header row that is its name alone, with no count or value beside it; a section with nothing to show is not shown: an Artifact's Evals, Neighbours, Tools, Tool log (once a tool has run) and Path, or on a Current Definition and Source, an eval's Result, Execution options, Execution metadata and Fingerprint, a Run's Outcome, Waits for, What and Provenance, and Debug, or a Run's Technical, last. A click on a header row folds its section to that row alone, and again unfolds it (also Enter or Space on it focused). A section stays folded or unfolded as a person left it, by its name, in every Detail opened later and in the next launch. Until then what a tab is opened for is unfolded (Evals, Tools, Path, Definition, Result, Outcome, Waits for) and what is read now and then starts folded (Neighbours, Source, Execution options, Execution metadata, Fingerprint, What, Provenance, Debug and Technical). A Human review on an eval's tab is its conversation, as in Review (J-HUMAN-LAYOUT), under the Detail's tabs without section header rows, each tool run folding by its own line (J-HUMAN-LOG).
- **J-DETAIL-TOOLS**: an Artifact's chip lists the Human tools of the toolsets its declaration composes, each named by its tool name (`files.open`), with the look and behaviour of a Human review's Tools and runs (J-HUMAN-TOOL, J-HUMAN-LOG): a click on a tool's `▶` runs it at once in the branch's worktree as it is now, whichever Run is shown, with no claim, and records nothing as a review's evidence. While one of its tools runs, the Artifact's tool buttons are disabled and a line under them shows it running with Cancel, which stops it; a tool that cannot run says why there in red. Its runs show in the Artifact's Tool log while the app runs, each run one line as a review's runs are (J-HUMAN-LOG); the Tool log shows from the first run on, and a run unfolds a Tool log that was folded. The graph's nodes carry no tool buttons.
- **J-DETAIL-SUMMARY**: an eval's tab shows its Summary: its status, beside it in grey what running it took and spent (time, tokens in `k` or `M`, model) or what reuse saved; its instruction; its result, its issues as numbered items, an Agent eval's each with its priority and a runtime eval's with its exit code; then Execution options, Execution metadata and Fingerprint when present, folded until unfolded (J-DETAIL-SECTIONS); every other fact, what it waits for included, folds under Debug. What the status says is not repeated below it. On a Current it shows whether the eval would run, would be reused or is still being checked and why, and the result it would reuse. An eval with a log switches below the tabs between its Summary, shown first, and its log, a plain text choice a level below the tabs: an Agent eval's Agent log (its transcript, with its own scrollbar) or a runtime eval's Runtime log (its saved output). A Human eval's tab in a Run holds its review and sign-off.
- **J-GRAPH-COLOURS**: a node says its verdict (GREEN, running or waiting for a Human, waiting, RED, ERROR, blocked), on a Current what would happen (reused, would run, waiting, blocked) or that it is still being checked, with that verdict's icon before its name and a strip along its bottom, both in the verdict's colour; its face and its outline stay neutral. After its name a node with evals says how many of them are GREEN of how many it has (`2/3`), on a Current how many would be reused; one still being checked says none. Edges are neutral, except an edge that leaves a RED, ERROR or blocked Artifact, drawn in that Artifact's colour, so what holds a blocked Artifact shows. Icons, strips, counts and edges change in place when a result arrives or a scan learns what would happen. A verdict has one colour wherever the app shows it: a node's colour is the colour the same status has in a Pane, in a chip and in a row.
- **J-GRAPH-PARTS**: on both graphs an Artifact made of parts, those its `parts` names, is drawn as a box that encloses its parts' nodes, its name, its verdict's icon and a strip in its verdict's colour at the box's top; a part with parts of its own is a box inside it, as deep as the parts go. The box is the whole and the nodes in it are its parts, and each is an Artifact of its own: a click on the box outside its parts opens the whole's Detail and a click on a part opens the part's. Edges to and from a whole meet its box; edges between Artifacts in different boxes are drawn and coloured like any other. The layout is made for parts that belong to one whole, each whole's parts laid out together inside it. A part of several wholes is drawn inside each of them, each copy with the part's edges; selecting one copy highlights every copy of it, and its Detail says that it is drawn in each of those wholes, naming them. The find field lists such a part once and goes to its copy in the first of its wholes.
- **J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE**: a whole's box has a Minimize button at its top right; a click on it draws the whole as a node of a node's size: its name, its verdict's icon and colour, how many parts it has and an Expand button, whose click draws its box again. The palette's "Minimize or expand the whole" does the same for the whole whose Detail is open (also `m`, for the whole focused on the graph or whose button is focused, else the whole whose Detail is open, while focus is outside the Pane, focus staying where it was). The edges to and from the Artifacts a minimized whole hides meet it instead, those between the same two drawn once in the light that comes first, as an Artifact's light comes from its evals'; edges among them are not drawn. A minimized whole is otherwise a node: a click on it outside its button opens its Detail, and it is highlighted while an Artifact it hides is open. The find field lists what it hides as before and goes to the minimized whole for it. A whole stays minimized on every graph of its branch, its Runs' and its Current, until it is expanded or the app quits; a part of several wholes stays drawn inside those not minimized.
- **J-GRAPH-LARGE**: a graph too large to draw at once stays navigable: panning or zooming the camera draws the Artifacts it brings into view. Zoomed out so far that names would be too small to read, each Artifact is drawn as a block of its verdict's colour and a whole as its box's outline in its colour, and a click on one still opens its Detail; zoomed in until names read, each is a node again (J-GRAPH-COLOURS).
- **J-GRAPH-NAVIGATOR**: whenever part of a graph lies outside the view, whatever the graph's size, a Navigator shows at the graph's bottom right, left of the Detail when one is open and, in Chat's Graph view, above the floating composer and its card: the whole graph in small, each Artifact a block of its verdict's colour, with the part in view framed. A click or a drag in it moves the camera so that place is in the middle of the view, the zoom kept. It goes when the whole graph is in view again. Hovering it opens nothing.
- **J-GRAPH-FIND**: the graph's tools have Find (also Ctrl+F with focus outside the Pane and outside fields, on the graph or in the Detail). With a Run's graph or a Current shown, a click on it turns it into a find field in its place in the stage header, focused, its list under it over the graph. With nothing typed it lists the graph's starred Artifacts (J-DETAIL-STAR) as if they had been found, in the graph's order, each with its verdict's glyph and colour; when none of the graph's Artifacts is starred, it says how to star one. Typing lists the Artifacts and evals whose names contain the text, ignoring case, in the graph's order: an Artifact by its whole name, an eval as `{Artifact} / {eval}`, each with its verdict's glyph and colour (on a Current, what would happen); the first 50, then how many more match, or that none does. No row shows as chosen when the list appears (also ↑ and ↓ choose a row, and Enter opens the chosen one, or the first while none is chosen). A click on a row opens it, an Artifact's Detail on its Artifact tab and an eval's on that eval's tab, closes the field and moves the camera so the Artifact sits in the middle of the graph left of the Detail, zooming in only as far as its name needs to be readable. A click outside the field closes it and does nothing else, the graph and the Detail as they were (also Esc), and Find is back in its place.
- **J-GRAPH-CAMERA**: a graph shows whole when it first appears, never enlarged beyond its nodes' own size: a small graph sits at that size in the middle of the graph's place. The graph's place, for this first view and for Fit View, leaves out the status line at the graph's top and, in Chat's Graph view, the floating composer and its card at its bottom, so no Artifact is shown under them. From then on only the person moves its camera, by panning, zooming (the wheel, or the stage header's Zoom out and Zoom in, the zoom in percent between them), Fit View (whole again, with the same limit), the Navigator (J-GRAPH-NAVIGATOR), opening what the find field found (J-GRAPH-FIND), minimizing or expanding a whole (J-GRAPH-MINIMIZE), which keeps that whole's top left corner where it was on the screen, or the keyboard moving focus to an Artifact out of view, which pans just enough to bring it in: opening, switching, resizing or closing the Detail, a result or scan arriving and a new layout never zoom or pan it.
