# Introduction checklist

- **W-INTRO-SENTENCE**: The first headline is the exact project sentence:
  “Artifactize: Evaluation-driven development for people and AI agents.”
- **W-INTRO-FIRST-READER**: A first-time reader can understand what the project does
  and why it helps without already knowing the project.
- **W-INTRO-PLAIN-LANGUAGE**: Use clear, direct language for software developers without
  defining everyday developer tools (such as Git, terminals, JSON or TOML). Explain
  Artifactize's own terms (such as Artifact, Artifact Graph, eval and Run) in plain words
  when they first appear.
- **W-INTRO-CONTRIBUTOR**: Address the reader as someone who builds and reviews with
  teammates and AI agents, not merely someone operating a list of features.
- **W-INTRO-SHOW-CONCRETE**: Show a concrete `.artifactize/<name>.artf` declaration and
  `artifactize verify` output early so the reader sees how declarations and checks look in
  practice.
- **W-INTRO-FEATURES**: Explain the core features concisely and concretely.
- **W-INTRO-NO-RAMBLING**: Long-winded explanations, repeated explanations and unnecessary
  detail are failures, not extra credit.
